# A38: twins on H3 and a frozen-feature probe

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random render. H3 = StyleGAN3 morphs against single-person renders. SBI = self-blended training fakes. SWA = weight averaging.

- **Question.** Does removing SBI from our protocol restore H3 AUC, and is morph-versus-render evidence linearly readable from frozen CLIP or ConvNeXt features?
- **What was done.** Registered and read 6 Nov 2026, 0.625 H100-hours. Trained SBI-free and SWA-free twins of two methods; fitted linear probes on frozen features of H3.
- **Result.** Removing SBI raised H3 original-pool AUC by +0.071 [+0.046, +0.099] for Inception-MAD (meets the +0.05 bar) and +0.040 [+0.014, +0.068] for PW-MAD (below it); removing SWA did not raise it. The SBI-free twins are near chance on the selection set (EER 48.4 and 51.1% against 24.1 and 16.8%), so the rise is mostly drift toward 0.5 (0.456 to 0.526 and 0.484 to 0.524). Probes reach AUC 0.91 from pretrained CLIP or ConvNeXt features and 0.80 to 0.83 from our arm c networks, whose own heads score 0.42 to 0.50 (control floor 0.609).
- **Verdict.** Twin hypothesis: "neither" by the registered rule in both pools. Probe hypothesis: supported in both pools.
- **Caveats.** The probe is trained and tested on one generator and follows closeness to the generator's average latent, so it shows decodability, not identity evidence or detection. H3 is now consumed for choosing methods. Over 96 networks lower selection-set EER goes with lower H3 AUC (Spearman +0.65, post hoc).
