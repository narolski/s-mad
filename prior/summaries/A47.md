# A47: the training interventions

Terms: EER = equal error rate. E7 = resolution-matched genuine pool. M1 = Inception-MAD recipe, M3 = MADation ViT-B/16 recipe. I-4 = label-permuted negative control, I-3 = morph-free positive control, I-1 = class-symmetric augmentation. X3 = test of reconstructions against copies.

- **Question.** What do SMDD-trained networks of two published recipes learn: does E7 move a chance-level network, can a network that never saw a morph solve the diffusion cells, and does symmetric augmentation cost accuracy?
- **What was done.** Registered 7 Nov 2026 22:55; 288 score files read 8 Nov 14:11 to 14:16. Two seeds for I-4, three for I-3 and I-1, two recipes.
- **Result.** I-3 (trained on SMDD genuine images against their shrunk copies, no morphs): M3 MorDIFF 0.00% EER and the six Diffusion-Morph (DiM) cells 0.00 to 1.17%; M1 MorDIFF 14.88%. X3 AUC 0.951 (M3), 0.635 (M1). I-1: M1 augmentation costs accuracy (eligible native EER +52.45 points); M3 not applicable (reference rise 0.49 points). I-4: not at chance (6 of 17 cells for M1, 11 of 17 for M3 off chance).
- **Verdict.** Independent review (8 Nov 14:18): accept-qualified. The eight primary decisions stand. I-3 is a constructive demonstration that morph training is not needed to reach these numbers in this setup; the exact cue, a universal cue and identity detection are not established.
- **Caveats.** The label-permuted control failed its chance gate for both recipes, so I-3 lacks its negative control. The last-checkpoint I-1 check also changes which cells count. Two or three seeds only; person intervals are conditional on those seeds. The ledger entry (status "running") is stale. I-3 is not yet registered in PROTOCOL.md per `02_REVIEW_AND_SHORTCOMINGS.md`.
