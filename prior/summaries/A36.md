# A36: published detectors and shortcuts on held-out sets

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random genuine photo (0.5 chance). H3 = StyleGAN3 morphs against single-person StyleGAN3 renders. EER = equal error rate.

- **Question.** Do published single-image detectors rely on shortcuts (resolution, file size, eye geometry, GAN-render traits) rather than morphing evidence?
- **What was done.** Registered 5 Nov, finished 6 Nov 2026, 7.588 GPU-hours. Scored our retrains of published recipes, the official MADation weights, the SelfMAD release and later the MixFaceNet-MAD release on H3, on resolution-matched diffusion sets and on camera-photo controls.
- **Result.** 12 primary rows: H3 AUC 0.35 to 0.59; below chance for both MADation retrains, the official ViT-L/14 and the SelfMAD release (0.35 to 0.45); official ViT-B/16 0.55. Official MADation ViT-B/16 on MorDIFF: 4.9% to 19.6% EER against resolution-matched photos (delta [+11.2, +18.0]); sanity check reproduces the published 1.10 within 5 points (4.90). Our protocol lowers H3 AUC against the published recipe in 14 of 14 method-by-data cells (-0.008 to -0.197, post hoc). MixFaceNet release: H3 0.581, E7 47.7% EER.
- **Verdict.** Absence of GAN-morph separation on this test (rule label "neither", ledger "open"); the diffusion finding for ViT-B/16 later became a descriptive row in A41.
- **Caveats.** The render flags depend on the camera-photo pool and on a selection-set threshold where the SMDD-trained rows are near chance (EER 30 to 51%), so the flag verdicts are uninformative; one generator; one release per row; several tests post hoc.
