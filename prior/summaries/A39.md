# A39: the batch before the run window closed

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random genuine or render image. EER = equal error rate. H3 = StyleGAN3 morphs against single-person renders.

- **Question.** Is our comparison with published detectors fair (does the MixFaceNet-MAD release run correctly through our loader), and what lets a detector pass H3: selection-set skill, SBI or typicality?
- **What was done.** Registered 6 Nov, read 6 and 7 Nov 2026, 0.278 GPU-hours. Parts R1 to R4, S2 and P were read. Parts S1, S3 and T were withdrawn by the author on 7 Nov and never run.
- **Result.** S2: the release separates 2,000 plus 2,000 of its own training images (EER 0.45%, AUC 0.9997); at its own threshold it flags 0 of 204 FRLL, 5 of 2,000 FFHQ photos, so it fails by missing morphs. R4: inside H3, latent spread alone separates morphs from renders (AUC 1.000), so no analysis inside H3 can tell identity mixing from averageness. P: a frozen read-out with AUC 0.92 to 0.93 on H3 scores spread-matched renders as morph-like as the morphs (0.45 CLIP, 0.49 ConvNeXt; matched against plain renders 0.95 and 0.93). R3: SelfMAD release flags downsampled FFHQ photos +7.2 points more [+5.8, +8.6].
- **Verdict.** P and the adapter check supported; the SelfMAD downsampling test came out "neither" (close to falsified).
- **Caveats.** The design has almost no power against a two-identity component: it shows H3 offers nothing beyond averageness, not that detectors lack identity evidence. The in-sample adapter check does not verify the score scale. S1, S3 and T have no result.
