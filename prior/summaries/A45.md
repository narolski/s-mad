# A45: SMDD-only reproduction retrains

Terms: SMDD = synthetic training set. MAD22-5 = mean EER over five MAD22 morph families. EER = equal error rate. Gate = pre-set test of whether our retrain matches the published number.

- **Question.** Do our SMDD-only retrains of Inception-MAD and MorphHRNet, and our FD-MAD re-implementation, reproduce the published MAD22 numbers, and if so do their cells show the E7 rise?
- **What was done.** Registered 7 Nov 2026; read 8 Nov (reviewed 13:47). Five Inception seeds, five HRNet-W18 seeds, five existing HRNet-W48 networks, three FD-MAD subsample seeds at 256 and 512 px. MAD22 held copy is 185 genuine and 4,069 morphs.
- **Result.** Mean MAD22-5 EER against published: Inception 32.72% against 10.53%; HRNet-W18 40.91% against 9.42%; HRNet-W48 34.93% against 9.42%; FD-MAD global 256 56.02% and 512 27.61% against 16.62% (global-only comparator).
- **Verdict.** Every arm FAILS the reproduction gate, so no E7 test applies. Independent review: corrections required, not blanket acceptance.
- **Caveats.** The committed JSON still holds 85 control p-values (17 cells in each of five failed arms) that the protocol said not to compute; exclude them from inference. The FD-MAD arms are a partial (global branch only) re-implementation; fusing the landmark branch was not run, so this does not show the full published FD-MAD fails. Reader guards do not check every input hash, although the reviewer matched all 73 copy-back files. The ledger entry (status "running", headline "not run") is stale.
