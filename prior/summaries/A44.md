# A44: the MADation mechanism block

Terms: LoRA = light fine-tuning layers. A_rec = AUC of published morphs against reconstructions of genuine photos. "R-last" = our retrain taken at its final checkpoint, as the authors did. Logit = the raw score before the sigmoid.

- **Question.** Why does the released MADation ViT-B/16 separate MorDIFF morphs from reconstructions, and which part put that behaviour there: CLIP pretraining, SMDD data, LoRA adaptation or the files' construction?
- **What was done.** Registered 7 Nov 2026. Zero-GPU reads and 11 platform gates run 7 Nov (0.096 GPU-hours); the retrain arms were replaced by an R-last scoring (A44R) read on 8 Nov. Most mechanism tests (F1 to F4, F6) were not run.
- **Result.** The released ViT-B/16's logit on 185 genuine photos rises monotonically with downsampling dose (Spearman 1.0 over six rungs; +1.45 logits at dose 3.30); the ViT-L/14 shows none. Our recipe trained to the last step reaches the authors' regime: MorDIFF 4.44% EER (n 8) against 34.68% at the selected checkpoint. A44R (8 Nov): A_rec 0.476, E7 rise +11.39 points (seed bounds [8.00, 14.78]), H3 AUC 0.530, all 8 networks passed platform checks.
- **Verdict.** F5 dose response supported. A44R accepted with qualifications (`INDEPENDENT_REVIEW_A44R_A45_20261108.md`): the sentence "not a consequence of the recipe" is too broad (seed 8 has A_rec 0.706). Ledger entry is stale (it stops at 7 Nov 23:22).
- **Caveats.** Regime was already known when R-last was registered; matching one benchmark is not equivalence to the released weights; LoRA-removal tests (F3, F4) and head decomposition (F6) have no result recorded.
