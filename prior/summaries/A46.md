# A46: diffusion family, reproduction register and raw-file runs

Terms: MTCNN = the face detector used by the released crop script. EER = equal error rate. MAD22-5 and FRLL-5 = five-family mean EERs. N1 to N3 = registered tests: N1 and N2 concern the size of the E7 rise, N3 is a validity check whose refuter is separation of morphs from reconstructions (exact definitions are in PROTOCOL.md, not verified here).

- **Question.** Does any published regime reproduce on raw files with the authors' own crop, and do the undecided diffusion cells meet or refute N1 to N3 with a positive control that can pass for the right reason?
- **What was done.** Registered 7 Nov 2026; read and then repeatedly corrected on 8 Nov. Included official boxes, the MTCNN crop, mechanism ladders, photos-only arms, FFHQ-Morphs and kernel and dose ladders.
- **Result.** Released MixFaceNet checkpoint on retained crops: 13.61% on held MAD22-5 against 13.69% published, 9.02% on FRLL-5 against 8.96%. Official-box ViT-B/16 MIPGAN-I 38.90% against 33.37% and MIPGAN-II 27.57% against 22.21% (not reproduced). ViT-B/16 on DiM-C and Greedy-DiM-S meets N1 and N2 (rises +23.12 and +22.47) but its N3 refuter fires (A_rec 0.724, 0.745). RGB-only secondary (accepted with qualifications, 16:19 CET): MixFaceNet 15.34% MAD22-5, 9.90% FRLL-5, 8.91% native MorDIFF.
- **Verdict.** Independent review: corrections required. The MTCNN branch is suspended (released script passes BGR to an RGB detector and leaves negative crop starts unbounded). The paired principal study stays BLOCKED, new reproduction claims NOT RUN. Ledger status "running" is stale.
- **Caveats.** FaceMorpher acquisition 806 of 1,222 morphs and MorDIFF 948 of 1,000 fail the completeness gate; their p-values are excluded; retained-crop agreement is not full-benchmark reproduction; no A41 grade changed.
