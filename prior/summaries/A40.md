# A40: SelfMAD downsampling response by native detail

Terms: SelfMAD release = the authors' published detector weights. SBI = self-blended training fakes. H2 = diffusion morphs made by embedding midpoints. Tercile = third of the pool.

- **Question.** Is SelfMAD's response to downsampling a loss of fine detail, does SBI create the "distinct from average" reading, and is the A38 probe specific to StyleGAN latents?
- **What was done.** Registered 6 Nov, read 7 Nov 2026, zero GPU-hours. Only part F (a zero-GPU read of existing scores) was run. Parts Q (replication with and without SBI) and H2 (probe on diffusion morphs) were withdrawn by the author with A39 S1 and never run.
- **Result.** The release's flag response to roughly 256 px downsampling of FFHQ photos does not grow with the photo's file size: top minus bottom tercile -2.44 points, 95% [-5.96, +1.02] (terciles +8.40, +7.15, +5.96). Without a threshold, downsampling raises the score by about +0.26 (0-1 scale) in every tercile. Flags repeat exactly on 2,250 byte-identical copies (scores differ by at most 9.6e-05); a proportional dose response is not supported (+0.52 [-2.28, +3.31]).
- **Verdict.** "Fine-detail loss" hypothesis falsified; determinism supported; Q and H2 untested.
- **Caveats.** Post-result review: "small on FFHQ" holds only at the flag threshold. The cue word is "genuine-photo processing history; cue not identified". One network.
