# A42: six descriptive follow-ups of the A41 reviews

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random comparison image. A_rec = AUC of published morphs against reconstructions of genuine photos. Kernel = the resampling filter.

- **Question.** Do the frame border, the zero-filled region, image detail, kernel top-band energy, path curvature or alignment explain the A41 effects the reviews left open?
- **What was done.** Registered and read 7 Nov 2026 (zero-GPU parts 16:42, window part 18:08, summary 18:08:58), 0.074 GPU-hours. Descriptive and post hoc: no A41 grade can change.
- **Result.** Matching border area leaves ViT-B/16's MorDIFF separation at 0.844 [0.787, 0.895] (0.847 unmatched). In the central 256 px window (no zero-fill) it falls to 0.61 [0.56, 0.65] while the rise against downsampled photos grows to +32.9 [28.9, 37.7]. Lanczos copies carry more top-band energy than area copies in all 204 photos yet push SelfMAD as far as bicubic. Endpoint gradients do not follow each photo's score change (Spearman 0.16 and 0.00). Alignment energy does not track the SelfMAD E7 change (Spearman -0.167).
- **Verdict.** Border hypothesis: (a) falsified, (b) supported, combined "mixed". Kernel and gradient hypotheses "mixed". Alignment: supported (absence of evidence).
- **Caveats.** One network and the same 204 photos; the window enlarges the face about 2x, so scale is not separated; never call the window a border test and never write "the kill is spurious".
