# A49: DINOv3 constrained adaptation with processing consistency

Terms: DINOv3 = a large self-supervised vision encoder. P0 to P3 = four arms: P0 head only, P1 LayerNorm adaptation, P3 LayerNorm adaptation plus consistency loss; P2 is not described in the files read. EER = equal error rate. JS = Jensen-Shannon divergence.

- **Question.** Does adding a processing-consistency loss to DINOv3 LayerNorm adaptation (P3 against P1) lower sensitivity to processing without costing native accuracy?
- **What was done.** Plan 8 Nov 2026; five matched seeds, 20 networks, 20.4992 H100-hours (below the 24 budget). Registered analysis ran on CPU 8 Nov 20:31 UTC. No selection, ensembling or seed picking.
- **Result.** Processing instability P3 minus P1: +0.00287 probability units, 95% seed interval [-0.00489, +0.01063] (superiority failed). Native 17-family mean EER P3 minus P1: +0.246 points [-0.234, +0.727] (the +2 point non-inferiority condition passed). Absolute native means P0, P1, P2, P3: 7.70, 10.71, 7.88, 10.96%. Fixed-direction AUCs for P3: self-pair diagnostic 0.1404, H3 0.4153, X4 0.4615.
- **Verdict.** Registered joint primary criterion FAILED; independent review (8 Nov 20:34 UTC): PASS for faithful reporting. Adaptation is not shown to beat head-only P0.
- **Caveats.** Not a faithful reproduction of the published GenD method; the X3, H3 and X4 diagnostics were already seen, so they are design-motivated; MAD22 is 185 and 4,069, not 204 and 4,483; five seeds on fixed images, no person-level uncertainty.
