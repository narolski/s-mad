# A41: the shortcut proof (registered rules, grades final)

Terms: EER = equal error rate. AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random comparison image. E7 = genuine photos shrunk to the morphs' face size. "Grade" = the label A41's rules assign, not a level of certainty.

- **Question.** Under one registered family of tests with pre-written gates, are released detectors' FRLL-based diffusion results shortcut-carried, and is the absence of GAN-morph separation robust?
- **What was done.** Registered 7 Nov 2026; zero-GPU parts read 7 Nov 04:13; amendment G (12:11) and G2 (13:48) added GPU items (1.037 H100-hours in all). Person-clustered intervals over 102 FRLL persons.
- **Result.** C1, SelfMAD release (own preprocessing): Greedy-DiM EER 8.37% to 90.74% under E7 (+82.38 [77.32, 87.35]); MorDIFF 2.53% to 61.29%; published regime reproduces on raw files (6.39% against 7.60% published). It ranks DiffAE reconstructions (AUC 0.330 [0.284, 0.374]) and merely resampled photos (0.092) above published MorDIFF morphs. C2, MADation ViT-B/16 on MorDIFF: rise holds in all five crops (+13.22 to +21.04) but it separates morphs from reconstructions (0.847). C3: 12 published rows, no H3 AUC at or above 0.65; on spread-matched renders 11 of 12 rank the renders higher. B: file attributes alone separate classes in 10 of 10 cells.
- **Verdict.** Grades final: C1 strong, C2 descriptive row, C3 strong but narrow, B meets its criterion, C4 construction sentence. Family: F1 to F6, F8 supported; F7 not supported. G2 changed no grade.
- **Caveats.** Rules were written after earlier estimates were seen; the border test could not be evaluated (a pool artefact is not formally excluded); kernel ladder at the AUC ceiling counts as one result; one network per claim; cue word stays "genuine-photo processing history, cue not identified"; MADMamba not evaluated (sealed).
