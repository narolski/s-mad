# A50: the missing photos-only controls under our recipe

Terms: A = SMDD alone; B = A plus genuine FERET and FRGC photos; C = B plus our classical morphs. P = our training recipe. Capped EER = each family's EER capped at 50, then averaged over seven diffusion families. M1, M2, M5, M6 = Inception-MAD, MorphHRNet-style, PW-MAD, OrthoMAD.

- **Question.** Under one fixed recipe P, does adding our classical morphs (C against B) help on held-out diffusion morphs?
- **What was done.** Proposed and run 8 Nov 2026: 20 new B/P fits (five seeds per carrier), 43 B and C networks analysed, 13.654722 H100-hours (budget 40). Replacement design: 30% of all draws (60% of stored-morph draws) swapped from SMDD to our morphs.
- **Result.** Capped endpoint C minus B: M2 -2.106 points [-3.464, -0.748], M6 -2.198 [-3.562, -0.834] (four-way Holm p 0.028 each); M1 -1.19, M5 unresolved. Uncapped mean EER got worse for three carriers: M1 +1.656, M2 +1.816, M5 +3.979; M6 -2.953. M2's capped gain coexists with Greedy-DiM EER 50.99% to 68.92%.
- **Verdict.** Independent review (8 Nov 21:31 CET): accept-qualified. Small, family-specific, exploratory effect; the earlier four-of-five finding stays a joint recipe-and-data contrast, not a morph-only gain.
- **Caveats.** Replacement, not count-matched addition; reduced SMDD exposure and source cues are bundled with the morphs; fixed already-seen test sets, four historically chosen carriers; seed-only intervals, not simultaneous; nonsignificance is not equivalence. Still a post-freeze exploration.
