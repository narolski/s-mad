# A43: the MorDIFF two-by-two

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random comparison image. MorDIFF = published diffusion morph set. DiffAE = the diffusion autoencoder used to render. "Step count" = decoding steps of the generator.

- **Question.** Does the official MADation ViT-B/16 separate published MorDIFF morphs from reconstructions of genuine photos because of how they were rendered (decoding step count), a file-specific cue, or because it sees two identities?
- **What was done.** Registered, run and read 7 Nov 2026 (22:02), 1.175 GPU-hours generation plus 0.108 H100-hours scoring. Re-rendered the same reconstructions, same-pair morphs and self-morphs at 100 and at 20 decoding steps, stored identically. 200 published pairs, 98 persons; Holm over 20 tests.
- **Result.** Published morphs against 100-step reconstructions: AUC 0.833 [0.766, 0.896]; against 20-step reconstructions (the step count that best reproduces the published pixels, chosen score-blind): 0.487 [0.381, 0.594]. Published against our 20-step same-pair morphs: 0.505 [0.473, 0.539]. Two-identity against one-identity 20-step morphs: 0.706 [0.625, 0.785], undetermined because self-morphs mix two expressions.
- **Verdict.** Registered joint cell: shortcut (processing history: the decoding step count) shown for MorDIFF. Identity blending is not needed to explain the result and is not separately shown.
- **Caveats.** One released network (never the MADation method); post hoc with a disclosed forecast; the author approved the wording of claim C2 on 7 Nov about 22:25 CET; no A41 grade changed.
