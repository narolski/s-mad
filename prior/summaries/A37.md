# A37: StyleGAN3 render controls

Terms: AUC = chance a random morph outranks a random render (0.5 chance). H3 = StyleGAN3 morphs against single-person renders. Latent spread = how far a face's code sits from the generator's average.

- **Question.** Which property of single-person renders do 84 networks read: latent spread, typicality (truncation) or stored fine detail?
- **What was done.** Registered 6 Nov, finished 7 Nov 2026, 2.628 H100-hours. Re-scored 84 networks (14 arms, 6 seeds) on renders matched to the morphs' spread, truncated renders and renders re-stored through our resample step.
- **Result.** On spread-matched renders no network ranks the morphs above the renders (AUC 42.8 to 49.2, descriptive). Two arms above chance on H3 fall to chance (ConvNeXt 63.6 to 46.5; CLIP 57.1 to 46.4). Spread-matching moves the arm-b score 2 to 7 points toward chance. Truncated renders score far below both classes. Re-stored renders are still flagged 96 to 99% by arm a.
- **Verdict.** Registered labels: spread hypothesis "neither" (5 of 8 arms supporting, one short); truncation hypothesis "overshoot"; re-store hypothesis falsified; scoring-drift hypothesis supported.
- **Caveats.** The registered read refused at its 0.01 reproduction limit (0.0101) and the author chose a results-blind amendment on 7 Nov; scores drift up to 0.015 in CLIP networks but no AUC moved more than 0.0002; one generator, 90% intervals, thresholds from the selection set; no published detector and not MADMamba tested; exploratory.
