# Cue matrix: which benchmark cues exist, and which ones detectors are shown to use

Prepared 2026-11-11 for Paweł Narolski. A read-only compilation of the experiment record. Nothing was computed, scored or run for this page. No file whose name contains "fst", "valx" or "A12SP" was opened.

## How to read this page

The page keeps two questions apart for every cue:

1. **Is the cue in the files?** Does some property differ between the morphs and the genuine ("bona fide") photos of a benchmark cell, so that it alone could separate the two classes?
2. **Does a detector use it?** Has an intervention changed this property (and, ideally, only this property) and moved a detector's score?

A cue is crossed out only when question 2 has an answer. "The cue exists" never means "a detector uses it".

**Status words** (exactly one per row):
- **USED**: an intervention moved a named detector in the direction the cue predicts.
- **NOT USED**: an intervention or a matched comparison left a named detector unchanged, or the cue cannot reach the detector at all (marked "by construction").
- **PRESENT, USE UNTESTED**: the cue is measured in the files, but no intervention on it has been read.
- **ABSENT**: the cue is not in the benchmark files.
- **UNKNOWN**: nobody has measured whether the cue is in the files.

Where one cue behaves differently in different benchmark groups, it gets one sub-row per group (for example 4a and 4b), each with one status.

## Terms and abbreviations

- **S-MAD**: single-image morphing attack detection (one image in, "morph or genuine" out). **D-MAD**: differential MAD (a trusted reference photo is also given).
- **EER**: equal error rate, in %, where the false-alarm rate on genuine photos equals the miss rate on morphs. 50% is chance; above 50% the ranking is reversed.
- **AUC**: area under the ROC curve, 0 to 1; 0.5 is chance, below 0.5 means reversed.
- **O**: the one-feature separability used in A41, O = max(AUC, 1 - AUC) of a single file attribute over a whole cell (`smadcvpr/PROTOCOL.md` l.14369-14370). O = 1.000 means the attribute alone separates the classes perfectly; O = 0.500 means it carries nothing.
- **Benchmarks.** **FRLL**: Face Research Lab London set; **FRLL-Morphs** (landmark tools AMSL, FaceMorpher, OpenCV, WebMorph, plus a StyleGAN family). **MAD22**: SYN-MAD 2022 (FaceMorpher, OpenCV, Webmorph, MIPGAN-I, MIPGAN-II). **MIPGAN**: a StyleGAN-based morph generator. **MorDIFF**: diffusion morphs built on FRLL. **DiM / Greedy-DiM**: diffusion morph families (DiM-A, DiM-C, Fast-DiM, Fast-DiM-ODE, Greedy-DiM, Greedy-DiM-S), also on FRLL photos. **SMDD**: a synthetic (StyleGAN2-rendered) morph training set used by the published releases. **FFHQ** and **FEI**: other face photo sets used as controls. **G-test**: our own StyleGAN2 W+ morph set on FEI photos.
- **Detectors (official releases).** SelfMAD release (HRNet-W18, trained with self-blended images); MADation ViT-B/16 release and MADation ViT-L/14 release (CLIP vision transformers with LoRA adapters; **CLIP** is a pretrained image-text model, **ViT** a vision transformer, **LoRA** low-rank adaptation); MixFaceNet-MAD release.
- **Detectors (our re-implementations of published recipes, trained on SMDD only).** M1 Inception-MAD (Inception-v3), M2 MorphHRNet-style (HRNet-W48), M3 MADation recipe ViT-B/16, M3L MADation recipe ViT-L/14, M4 SelfMAD-style (HRNet-W18 with self-blended fakes), M5 PW-MAD (DenseNet-121 with pixel-wise labels), M6 OrthoMAD (ResNet-18), M8 MixFaceNet-MAD recipe, M8O MixFaceNet-MAD official training code. No M7 row appears in the records I read.
- **Our own networks.** MADMamba E10 (our frozen configuration, from scratch, with a frequency stream), its no-stream twin, and the W3 league rows. Their results are reported separately because they are not published detectors.
- **Controls.** **E7** (also "resmatch", "RSX"): the same genuine photos shrunk to the morphs' median face size before the same alignment. **X3**: published morphs against DiffAE reconstructions of genuine photos (one identity, no morphing). **DiffAE**: diffusion autoencoder. **H1**: a controlled Poisson-blended landmark set where both classes share source, size and border. **H3**: StyleGAN3 two-person morphs against single-person renders of the same generator. **X4**: the same morphs against renders matched to the morphs' latent spread. **X6**: the proposed causal GAN test (replace camera photos by same-generator output on the benchmark cells; not run). **X8**: a second, non-FRLL genuine pool (proposed, not run).
- **IED**: inter-eye distance in pixels. **HF**: high-frequency energy. **BPCER**: bona fide presentation classification error rate (false alarms on genuine photos). **APCER**: attack presentation classification error rate (missed morphs). **JPEG / PNG**: lossy and lossless image formats. **EXIF**: camera metadata block. **ICC**: colour profile block. **Chroma subsampling**: JPEG's reduced colour resolution (for example 4:2:0). **MTCNN**: a face detector used by the MixFaceNet-MAD crop script. **BGR / RGB**: colour channel orders. **SBI**: self-blended images, a training augmentation. **CI**: confidence interval. **H-new-N**: a numbered hypothesis in `smadcvpr/PROTOCOL.md`.

**Path shorthand** (under `/home/narolski/mambamorph-code/` unless absolute): **C** = `experiments/CONCLUSIONS.md`; **P** = `smadcvpr/PROTOCOL.md`; **R/** = `smadcvpr/results/`; **L/** = `experiments/` (ledger folders); **D** = `/home/narolski/smad-next/01_EVIDENCE_DOSSIER.md`. "[via D]" marks a number taken from the dossier without reopening its primary file today.

**One fact that shapes several rows.** Most scoring in this programme runs on our 512-px eye-aligned frames, stored as PNG (`L/A32-RSX_resolution-shortcut/README.md` l.48 "Eval PNGs (512 aligned)"; A36's matching uses "aligned-PNG size", `L/A36_published-detector-shortcuts/README.md` l.34). Every detector reads decoded pixels. So file-level attributes (container, metadata, native width) can only reach a detector through what they leave in the pixels: compression artefacts, resampling traces, face scale, borders.

## Notes per cue

**1. Container.** The cue is perfect in the diffusion and MIPGAN cells, but it is a byte in the file header. No detector reads headers, and every number in this programme comes from decoded pixels. This row can be crossed out by argument. What survives decoding is the JPEG trace on the genuine side and its absence on the morph side; see row 4.

**2. Pixel dimensions.** Width and height separate almost every cell perfectly. On our 512-px frames a native dimension reaches the detector only through the resize it forces (row 5) and the face scale (row 6). Nobody has changed the canvas without resampling. The E07 correlations are among our own networks' scores and are not an intervention. Not verified: whether the releases' own crops (SelfMAD's dlib crop, MixFaceNet's MTCNN crop) are sensitive to canvas size apart from face size.

**3. File size.** The cue is present almost everywhere. The tests are stratifications: within the E7 pool (H-new-137) or within aligned-PNG deciles (H-new-74), scores do not follow bytes. A36 notes that "the native file size still separates every matched subset", so native bytes cannot be matched inside a cell. This is "not used" in the matched sense only. The A12-BR observation (M1 scores OpenCV-tool morphs at 3.3% EER from MAD22's 1351-px files vs 53.6% from FRLL's 431-px files) is descriptive [via D l.68], and the drop list forbids calling them "the same OpenCV morphs" (C l.224).

**4a. JPEG history, GAN cells.** This is the only file-construction cue with a registered intervention. Re-saving the morphs as JPEG did not move M3L or the ViT-L/14 release on the cells named. It moved the ViT-B/16 release in a graded way (up to +15.68 at JPEG 50 on FRLL StyleGAN). The registered result is "no row reaches storage-format cue". The SelfMAD own-crop row has no JPEG scores.

**4b. JPEG history, landmark and diffusion cells.** JPEG quality alone separates the FRLL landmark cells perfectly (both classes are JPEG, at different quality). On diffusion cells the morphs are lossless. No morph-side storage ladder has been run on these cells. The genuine-side exposures show that at least the ViT-L/14 release reacts strongly to heavy compression (BPCER 0.054 to 0.529 at JPEG 50), so the cue is not inert.

**5. Resampling history (E7).** This is the best-supported cue. It is a joint change: E7 shrinks the photo, which changes effective resolution and file size, and in the MorDIFF pool it also moves border content (row 7). C1's cue word is therefore "bona fide processing history; cue not identified" (C l.211). "Resolution shortcut", "aliasing", "fine-detail loss" and "interpolation trace" are forbidden words. Counter-examples: the ViT-L/14 release and the M3/M3L recipes hold, and SelfMAD keeps detecting MAD22 FaceMorpher under E7 (0.11 [0.00, 0.89]). A47 shows the cue suffices to reach the published regime without ever seeing a morph (M3 at last.pt; "partial" at es.pt), not that the releases use it. The MixFaceNet-MAD release's E7 rises are descriptive, and its raw-file MTCNN numbers are suspended (row 8). Mechanism note, untested: SelfMAD's released code blends each photo with a Lanczos-resampled zoom of itself to make self-morphs, and never resamples the genuine partner (C l.207).

**6. IED / face scale.** Native IED is matched away by the alignment, and matched-subset tests find no geometry carry-over. A42's central-window result for the ViT-B/16 release mixes two changes (the peripheral content removed and the face enlarged about 2x), so "zoom" vs "periphery" is open.

**7. Border fraction.** The cue is strong in our aligned landmark frames. E7 leaves borders in place, which is why no landmark cell has a shortcut grade (D l.59, l.76). For published detectors the only attempted test (XA3c) could not be evaluated, and A42's border-area matching concerns the X3 diffusion contrast, not the landmark cells. Our from-scratch networks clearly learned "border => genuine". Not verified: whether the zero borders are in the tools' output or come from our alignment padding (data-shortcut.md l.18 calls it "a protocol confound ... (prep_eval.py)").

**8. Crop defects.** This is a defect of how one release's crop script was run, not a property of the benchmark files. It produced class-asymmetric artefacts (MorDIFF edge strips), and its results are suspended. It belongs on the list as a warning that pipeline defects can create cues. The SelfMAD crop script also re-saves .jpg inputs at quality 95, "one JPEG-95 asymmetry ... in the claimed direction, likely small" (L/A41 README l.279-280).

**9a. Renderer fingerprint, GAN.** The descriptive picture is consistent: detectors separate renders from camera photos about as well as morphs from camera photos, and none separates morphs from same-generator renders. The intervention on the benchmark cells themselves (X6) has not run. Re-stored renders stay separated from FRLL photos (AUC 0.989-0.999, our A37 arm a), so the generator-vs-camera difference is not fine texture alone (L/A37 l.251-256).

**9b. Renderer fingerprint, diffusion.** X3 keeps the renderer and removes the second identity. The SelfMAD release scores those reconstructions as more morph-like than the published morphs, which is direct evidence that its MorDIFF and Greedy-DiM results need no identity mixing. Caveat: the X3 positive control failed, so the assay's power to show identity use is not established (C1's "What it does not show", C l.182).

**10. Step count.** One network and one family. The cue explains why the ViT-B/16 release's "morph vs reconstruction" refuter fired in C2. It does not show that the release uses step count on the benchmark cell, where camera photos have no step count.

**11a / 11b. Sharpness.** For the releases, the blur-only exposure moves scores, but the reaction is not a monotone "softer = morph" rule. The reader labels are secondary and stored as open. C2's registered cue word for the ViT-B/16 release is "fine-detail softness". For our own networks the frequency stream is a sharpness detector on genuine photos, and A22's class-symmetric augmentation removes that behaviour.

**12. Capture source and background.** Only genuine-side, cross-pool effects are logged. No one has measured whether morphs and photos inside a cell differ in background, hair or clothing.

**13. Metadata and colour.** EXIF separates 16 of 17 cells but cannot reach a pixel detector. Chroma subsampling separates MAD22 Webmorph perfectly, which is the only file attribute that does so there. It leaves a pixel trace (colour resolution) and is untested. Pixel colour statistics have never been measured.

**14. Identity overlap.** Not examined. This is a cheap zero-GPU check.

**15. Expression.** The FRLL genuine pool mixes neutral and smiling photos. Whether morphs and photos differ in expression mix per cell is not tabulated. Indirect hints only: expression-split effects appear on our G-test and in A43.

**16. Blending trace.** H1 shows the CLIP-based rows read some landmark-morph signal beyond file cues. It cannot say whether that is the blend or the second identity.

**17. Identity mixing.** Nothing in the record shows a detector using it, and nothing shows a detector failing to use it with a test that could have detected it. This is the central open item (D section 4 point 5).

**18. Latent averageness.** This cue is specific to latent-average GAN morphs. It answers why H3 can look separable for our networks. It is not shown to drive any published benchmark number.

**19. SMDD construction.** A training-side asymmetry the releases could have learned (off-size morphs, StyleGAN2 genuine class). Not tested.
