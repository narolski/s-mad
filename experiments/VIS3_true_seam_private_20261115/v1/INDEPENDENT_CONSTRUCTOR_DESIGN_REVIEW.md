## Design decisions

1. The unchanged fallback ellipse defines an actual mixing boundary and is acceptable. Call it a known soft transition of a controlled construction, never a verified anatomical seam, benchmark morph, realistic attack or certified two-identity morph. No anatomical alignment is established. No new landmarks or photographs are required for this claim.

2. Captured-source exchange is NEW reciprocal two-photo recombination. Equal RNG seeds preserve stochastic parameters and mask, while texture, clipping, appearance and anatomy remain different. Same-photo SBI contains processing/mixing artifacts; it is not a no-op or a second photograph of the same person. Cross-minus-same cannot isolate identity. Retain both hosts and all four conditions regardless of outcomes.

3. Source inspection supports the profiler: it captures the final local source/mask from the exact extracted function and checks byte equality with its unchanged return. Bodies/defaults are AST-derived from the pinned file, avoiding unrelated dataset imports. This establishes equivalence to that extracted implementation under the declared runtime, not every historical training execution. RNG reset/restore requires an isolated process without concurrent RNG consumers.

4. The four selected boundary cells [3,4,10,13] versus corners [0,7,56,63] are acceptable descriptive equal-area regions. The row-major rule favours the upper boundary; these four of seventeen candidates are not representative of the whole boundary. Say “four prespecified boundary-overlap patches.” Their mean is the mean of four separate single-patch interventions, NOT one simultaneous 16384-pixel removal. Patches contain more than the transition, corners differ in position/content, and occlusion creates artifacts. Even consistent large contrasts cannot establish seam reliance.

5. Four conditions x 64 cells x two fills x three models = 1536 full perturbation responses suffices for the illustrative purpose, excluding baseline/repeat/no-op/anchor forwards. Minimum controls are registered same-photo constructions, reciprocal hosts, literal-copy/repeat checks, all four original native/history anchors, both fills and all maps. No extra photos, seeds, models or training are required. Genuine maps need their own read admission and never acquire fake seams. Patches and reciprocal versions are not independent subjects: no population inference, significance testing, ranking rescue, shortcut-free or identity-causal conclusion.
