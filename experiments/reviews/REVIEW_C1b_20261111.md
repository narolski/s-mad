# Independent review of the expression cue use study (C1b), 11 Nov 2026

Verdict: corrections required. Every number recomputed by the reviewer matches, and the benchmark
arithmetic stands. The name of the cause does not.

## What stands

- 12 of 17 benchmark cells are imbalanced (11 scored): the morphs are built from the neutral
  photographs of the 102 FRLL subjects, while half of the genuine class is their smiling photographs.
- With a neutral-only genuine class the released MADation ViT-L/14 error rises by 4.5 points on
  average over the 11 cells (95% interval 3.4 to 5.8; AMSL 17.7 to 24.5); the frozen-encoder ViT-B/16
  release moves the other way (-9.4); the fine-tuned ViT-B/16 release and SelfMAD change by under 1.5.
- Rankings move: mean error over the 11 cells, all photos against neutral-only: SelfMAD 5.3 / 3.9;
  frozen-encoder ViT-L/14 14.6 / 13.7; ViT-L/14 15.6 / 20.2; ViT-B/16 16.3 / 16.8; frozen-encoder
  ViT-B/16 27.7 / 18.3. No release should be called "most robust".

## What does not stand

- **"Only the expression differs" is false.** In the FRLL files the smiling photograph is larger in
  102 of 102 persons (median ratio 1.43, same size and JPEG tables) and its backdrop is about five
  times noisier (102 of 102). The smiling half differs from the morph sources in expression and in
  capture detail.
- **"Uses the expression cue" is not supported for the ViT-L/14 release.** Its ordering of the two
  photos fades as detail is removed (0.99 on our frames, 0.78 when stored at 256 px, 0.53 at about
  150 px) and is absent on two other photo pools (FERET same-session pairs 0.56; FRGC 0.51).
- For our ViT-B/16 retrains at the last checkpoint the ordering follows fine detail, not expression
  (it vanishes or reverses once detail is removed while the smile remains).
- Expression itself is read by early-stopped retrains and by frozen-encoder releases, with opposite
  signs, and that replicates on other pools (reviewer preview, post hoc).
- "CNN recipes do not show it" is wrong for the SelfMAD-style retrains (ties were counted as half).

## Consequences

- Cue table: the cue is renamed "smiling half of the FRLL genuine class (expression together with a
  capture-detail difference)" and its status returns to "present, use untested": no intervention
  isolates expression.
- Tables: neutral-only genuine photographs become the headline column for the 12 cells, the
  all-photograph number beside it.
- **Any FRLL control that mixes neutral and smiling photographs inherits the file difference**: the
  same-person mixes of the MorDIFF ladder (AD1), the FRLL pool of the landmark ladder (AR1), the
  diffusion mirror check (AD1b) where it uses the smiling photo. Use neutral-only pairs or the FRGC
  and colorFERET pool for conclusions.
