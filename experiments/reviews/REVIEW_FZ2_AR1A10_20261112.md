## Findings, most severe first

**F1 (major). Production set B is a re-render of the same images, not a replication.** I matched add-on REC, SAME, X and MIR at 20 steps to the production images by (host, partner): 536 of 536 match.
- About 23% of the PNGs are byte-identical (about 90% on the Athena shards, about 1% on the rented ones).
- The rest agree almost exactly: Spearman/Pearson correlation of s401, P_XS and Laplacian at least 0.9993; largest head-score difference 0.055.
- The sampler is deterministic (DDIM, eta 0, `dg1_gen.py:14`), so the matching numbers (0.662 / 0.662, 0.941 / 0.941, median d 29.1 / 22.5) are by construction.
- Fix for result.md line 7 and README section 5: "Production set B: the same 536 hosts and pairs re-rendered with the same deterministic sampler; its agreement with the add-on is a cross-platform reproducibility check (Athena A100 vs RTX 3090), not an independent replication."
- On the positive side, this is a far stronger platform-equivalence check than DG1's 8-image gate. Record it in DG1.

**F2 (major). H-new-4 is falsified only for pixel disagreement. A noise-space disagreement explains part of the effect (post hoc).**
- DG1 logs `theta_xT`, the angle between the two inverted noise maps (`dg1_addon.py:212`, in `gen_rows.csv`).
- theta alone separates X from SAME at **0.916** (median 1.564 vs 1.537 rad). The image-blind pixel measure d manages only 0.669.
- Inside SAME, theta correlates with the P_XS score (Spearman 0.495). Inside X it does not (0.000).
- Stratifying P_XS by theta quintiles lowers it from 0.941 to 0.876 (Δ ≈ 0.065, above the registered 0.05). The strata are thin (17/197, 145/69, 186/28, 188/27; only 4 usable) and there is no bootstrap interval.
- Rewrite for line 53: "falsified for pixel-level input disagreement d (Δ +0.008). Post hoc: the angle between the two inverted noise maps separates X from SAME by itself (0.916), and stratifying on it lowers P_XS to about 0.88. The direction may partly read noise-map similarity of the input pair."

**F3 (major, under-claim). P_XS is a detector of same-person mixes, not of a second person.** This is already in the data (line 72):
- Mean P_XS scores: X +2.17, REC +2.23, FRESH1 +3.25.
- Two-person doses stay flat at about +2.3 at every dose (DOSEX vs REC 0.51 to 0.52).
- Same-person doses fall steadily from +1.25 to −2.42 as the dose grows.
- Along D_SR the order is REC < X < SAME (X vs SAME 0.157).
- My Laplacian check: median face-box sharpness is REC 87, X 96, SAME 128. Same-person mixes are about 47% sharper than reconstructions.
- Inside SAME and NOISES, theta correlates negatively with sharpness (−0.40 and −0.34). In X, NOISEX and SEMS it does not (+0.09, +0.09, −0.10).
- So the same-person signature appears to travel through the slerp of correlated noise maps.
- Add to the reading: "Along P_XS, two-person mixes and every two-person dose sit with the reconstruction; what separates X from SAME is a property of the same-person mix."

**F4 (moderate). The "platform" split is a database, flag and partner-cycle split.**
- Athena has 109 colorFERET + 25 FRGC hosts; the rented machine has 402 FRGC hosts.
- Flagged hosts: Athena 51 of 134 (38%), rented 15 of 402 (4%).
- Only 1 of 88 partner cycles spans both platforms. The largest cycle (163 persons) is entirely rented.
- Within FRGC there is no platform effect: P_XS 0.938 on Athena (n = 25) vs 0.951 rented. The quoted 0.906 vs 0.951 is colorFERET (0.903) against FRGC (0.950).
- No contrast mixes platforms within a host, and re-renders agree (F1), so no reading is threatened.
- Wording: "the platform rows repeat the database rows; within FRGC, Athena 0.938 (25 hosts) vs rented 0.951."

**F5 (moderate). On question 4: seed and file writing are ruled out; the trace is the render path.**
- Seed: FRESH1 vs FRESH2 gives probe AUC 0.518 (clip) and 0.499 (dinov2); PNG size, Laplacian, asymmetry and image SD are all 0.50 to 0.50.

## Question 5: what the paper can say about one-person vs two-person diffusion outputs

On diffusion-autoencoder morphs of 536 new persons (one generator, 20 steps):
- Frozen CLIP and DINOv2 features separate two-person from same-person mixes (0.94 to 0.95). The separating direction mostly marks the same-person mix as unusual (sharper, made from correlated noise maps). Two-person mixes and two-person doses from 10% to 40% sit with plain reconstructions along it.
- Frozen SMDD-trained heads rank two-person above same-person mixes only weakly (0.66), no better than a 32×32 thumbnail (0.70).
- Any image not decoded from the photo's own inverted noise map is separable from the reconstruction (0.90 to 1.00), even a 5-grey-level noise-only change.
- So we cannot claim that frozen features or heads detect a second identity. What is readable is the render path.

## New hypotheses

- **H-A (coherent-noise sharpening).** When the two inverted noise maps are correlated (same person), slerp amplifies their shared fine structure, producing over-sharp renders; P_XS reads this.
  - Predictions: P_XS on NOISEX vs NOISES ≥ 0.90 and on SEMX vs SEMS ≤ 0.65. A new SAME_orth rung (x_T(S) orthogonalised against x_T(H) before slerp) gives P_XS(X vs SAME_orth) ≤ 0.65 and a Laplacian at X's level.
  - Falsified if: SAME_orth vs X ≥ 0.85, or SAME_orth stays as sharp as SAME.
  - Cost: 0 GPU for the transfer part. SAME_orth plus a matched X_corr rung is about 1,072 images at 20 steps, about 0.5 GPU-hours on a rented A100 (about 0.25 USD). Needs a DG1 amendment registered before running. Fits before 14 Nov.
- **H-B (render path, not content).** A high-frequency residual floor (AR1(b)'s 12 statistics) reaches ≥ 0.95 on NOISES vs REC and FRESH1 vs REC.
  - Falsified if: ≤ 0.85 (then the features read more than residual statistics).
  - Cost: 0 GPU, CPU minutes.
- **H-C (heads read sharpness).** Registered on FZ1's A43 frames: s401 X vs SAME stratified by Laplacian ≤ 0.62.
  - Falsified if: ≥ 0.65.
  - Cost: 0 GPU.
- **H-D (AR1(b) replicates on fresh data).** On the FRLL secondary pool, with the combined floor registered in advance: ViT-L/14 excess ≥ +0.03 with lower bound > 0, and the SelfMAD OpenCV interval includes 0.
  - Falsified if: ViT-L/14's lower bound is ≤ 0.
