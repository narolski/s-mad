**3. Major: the face-only form is not purely "both warped to the mean" (item 1).**
What matches (checked):
- Same warper and blender files (sha 76c0d1ac… / 7b98a8d5… in both trees).
- Same `np.rint((p1+p2)/2)` points, the same `.tem` files (D12a `_work/pairs_facemorpher.tsv`) and the same `warp_image`.
- Same mask: `mask_from_points` with no feathering in either; plain PNG `cv2.imwrite` in both.
- Per layer: FM = two warps, each truncated to uint8, then `addWeighted` rounding. W = one truncated warp.

The mismatch: the tool's mask is the hull **eroded by a 10×10 kernel** (`blender.py`). `blank.py` keeps the **uneroded** hull. I measured all 299 pairs (read-only numpy script at `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-narolski-mambamorph/c2840dc9-dd37-4619-9347-d8a76080b93f/scratchpad/ring.py`):
- A ring of about 5 px, 7.6% of the hull (range 6.8–9.0%), survives blanking.
- In that ring FM(H,B) equals the **unwarped** average of H and B on 299/299 pairs: double edges plus the composite seam. W equals raw H on 299/299.
- A one-line smoothness statistic gives AUC 0.787 on the face-only form, 0.761 inside the eroded mask only, and 0.690 on the ring alone.

Fix: no new image build. Add to the DATA caveats: "About 7.6% of the face-only hull is a ring outside the tool's eroded mask, where FM(H,B) is the unwarped H/B average (ghost edges, seam) and W is raw H. 'Face-only' means face plus the tool's halo." An eroded-hull blanking would be a new DATA request, so it is an author or lead item.

**4. Major: the read rules (item 2) need a ceiling rule, a floor rule and an exact licence.** Add to revision b:
- "Decision form: face-only. Ceiling: if X's seed-mean AUC ≥ 0.97 (a +0.03 gain is then impossible), Sb − X is 'ceiling, not informative'. If all three arms have a lower bound ≥ 0.95, the read is 'all arms at ceiling'. Floor: an arm whose block-bootstrap 95% interval contains 0.50 'does not separate blend from warp'."
- Licences:
  - (a) AUC at or below about 0.5: this blend is not what the networks flag.
  - (b) Above 0.5 but not above the same-data learner (0.859): consistent with blending and noise averaging; no evidence beyond low-level statistics.
  - (c) Above the learner on the block bootstrap on ≥ 4 of 5 seeds: "beyond hand-made statistics trained on the same list".
  - No outcome licenses "detects identity mixing". There is no same-person blend control.
- Sb − A is confounded: A's training list has no `lad_R2w` warp-only negatives (B and Sb have 156 each). Label Sb − B as the primary pair and Sb − A as secondary.
- `check.exceeds` bootstraps over components (`check.py:86-92`) and has no xf1b contrast. The reader must compare against `xf1b_rows_scores.csv` using `block`.

**5. Major: blanking shift (item 3).** No arm trained on blanked copies: `train_{A,B,Sb}.csv` contain 0 blank rows. So the grey surround is equally unfamiliar to all three arms. AUC compares only within one form, so the face-only form is fair. However, only Sb trained on surround-matched splices (`lad_S0`/`S3`/`S4`: 156/156/430 rows), which have the structure of this contrast. Disclose: "Sb's face-only advantage, if any, is partly the transfer its recipe was built for." Keep face-only as the decision. The whole frame is surround-dominated (surround floor 0.849, all-cue gbt 0.981), so it stays printed-only. Print the whole-minus-face AUC per arm as the blanking-shift control.
