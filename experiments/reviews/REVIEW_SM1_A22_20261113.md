**Verdict.** The rules were applied as registered and the numbers in `a22_read.json` match result.md. The one error is how H-E is interpreted, and it changes the conclusion. "Does not match" on the semantic-only rung comes from a sign flip in the multivariate floor, not from the network responding beyond pair similarity. Measured properly, the network sits below pair similarity on both rungs. H-F and H-G are read correctly but need wording fixes.

**Findings (most severe first)**

1. **Major: the floor is not a fair yardstick for semantic-only mixes.** I ran a post hoc check with the same fit as `a22_read.py:44-48` (803 training rows, standardised features).
   - The floor's weight on the semantic-code cosine is **+1.16**: given the noise features, more semantic similarity counts as more X-like. Marginally it runs the other way: X20 vs SAME20 AUC 0.787 on shards 0-5. The weight flips because the semantic cosine and θ are correlated (r = −0.46) and both are mixed together in training.
   - SEMX keeps the host's own noise map, so the two features no longer move together. The floor then extrapolates outside its training data.
   - On SEMX20 vs SEMS20 the semantic-cosine term alone gives AUC **0.129**, i.e. reversed. All other terms together give 0.486. The registered 0.440 is these two combined.
   - Plain semantic-code dissimilarity on the same 67 shard-6 hosts, (H,B) vs (H,S), separates them at **0.871** (mean cosine 0.969 vs 0.983).
   - So the network (0.718) is *below* semantic pair similarity, just as it is below noise pair similarity (0.922 vs 0.994).
   - The registered verdict "does not match" stands mechanically. Its reading "not explained by pair similarity" does not.
   - The semantic-cosine feature itself is the right analogue for SEMX: `interp` mixes the codes of (H,B) and the floor's cosine is of that pair. Small registration fix: the semantic code is *linearly* interpolated (`dg1_addon.py:209`), not slerped. Only the noise map is slerped.

2. **Minor: d is a pair feature, not a rung-image statistic.** Its value is identical across X20, NOISEX20 and SEMX20 (32.49) and across SAME20, NOISES20 and SEMS20 (25.35). In SEMX it therefore carries (H,B) information; its weight is −0.31, the same suppression pattern. The registration's "d from the rung images" is wrong. Add a note.

3. **Minor: H-F is near-degenerate by design.** REC, SEMS and SEMX all sit at distance 0, a three-way tie. The rung means could already be computed from the A19 scores. "Falsified" in the JSON is fine, but it only restates SEMS over REC 0.93.

4. **Minor: the H-G wording implies movement that did not happen.**
   - Deciles average 0.456 against 0.443 for A21's PNG quintiles, so no change.
   - Nearest-neighbour matching is lower (0.417).
   - Nearest-neighbour pairs are drawn from all hosts, not the same host.
