**Findings**

1. **Major: the disclosure is wrong about DINOv3's history.** prereg §8 says "DINOv3 networks have never been scored on them". BENCHMARK_EXPOSURE.md:21 and item 8 (:52) record frozen DINOv3 ViT-L/16 heads (tier 0, seeds 401–405) read on H1, MAD22 and the 191-host test ladder (PN1 prereg :427). It also records that "protocol plus spliced on DINOv3, MAD22 2.0 / 3.0%" was chosen on MAD22 and motivated the Sb arm. So the Sb idea came from this backbone. A DINOv3 "repeat" is not independent of how the arm was selected, and the sealed-read robustness row on MAD22, H1 and FRLL is compromised for DINOv3 too. Fix: correct §8 and add this caveat to every §7 sentence and to the §10 sealed-row label. Text only.
2. **Major: the registered fit script cannot be the submitted script.** `bb2_fit.sbatch` holds `__BS__`/`__ACCUM__`, which are filled after the smoke. That is after the registration pins the sbatch sha256 (prereg §11), so the hashes will differ. The `--time=05:00:00` is hard-coded, while §9 derives the limit from the smoke. Nothing refuses an unfilled template: it would copy the 8.75 GB pack and then fail in argparse. Fix: register the two exact filled variants (256/1 and 128/2) with their sha256s, or pass BS, ACCUM and `--time` via `sbatch --export`/command line with the script unchanged. Add `case "$BB2_BS/$BB2_ACCUM" in 256/1|128/2) ;; *) echo refuse; exit 1;; esac` before any copy.
3. **Major: the memory rule is fragile.** The DGX measurement implies about 70 GB at batch 256. A 100-step pass on one H100 does not guarantee every fit fits: lem-gpu-short may mix 80 and 94 GB cards (unverified), and memory varies slightly from run to run. Nothing says what happens if a fit runs out of memory or hits the time limit. Fix (register now): use 256 x 1 only if the smoke's peak `memory.used` is at most 72 GiB, else 128 x 2. An out-of-memory or timed-out fit is rerun once with a doubled time limit or at 128 x 2, and is labelled.
4. **Major: the reader does not implement the registered read-seed rule.** §6 requires "no non-finite training loss in train.log". `info_of()` computes `train_log_nonfinite`, but `rec["read"]` uses only `F.load`. Fix: make the flag part of `read`, or amend §6.
5. **Minor: timm version gap not checked.** WCSS has timm 1.0.30 and torch 2.11; the 318/318 check was on DGX timm 1.0.27. Fix: in the WCSS smoke, compare pooled features on 8 fixed pack images at init against a DGX reference, within 1e-3.
6. **Minor: partition time limit unconfirmed.**
7. **Minor: contrast 2 with one read seed** prints "uninformative" instead of "not read (too few seeds)".
8. **Minor: §6 wording**: floor on "every read seed" vs code's every testable seed (FS1's behaviour).
9. **Minor: late seeds**: state whether seeds after 14 Nov 18:00 are never read or a labelled addendum.
