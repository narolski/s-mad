# Pre-run review of the existing-network reads (PR1): GO WITH FIXES

Nothing reads closed data and no rule is undecidable. The reader's selectors and pool counts match the 19,797-row manifest, and the AUC direction, seed counting and permutation p are implemented correctly. The problems are in labels, in which decisions the verdicts feed, and in some code that deletes files. Findings 1-6 must be fixed before any read or job; 7 onward can go in the same dated amendment.

## Findings
1. Major (hard rule 6): GD ops code deletes files: `build_gd_pack.py:99` shutil.rmtree(tree); `remote_gd.sh:43,:54` rm -rf work dirs; `drive_gd.sh:26` rm -f results tar and rm -rf work, which can destroy a real result tar on restart. Fix: rename or use a timestamped directory per run; never remove a results tar.
2. Major: a GD0 "transfers" verdict cannot be told apart from StyleGAN-render detection. The StyleGAN morphs are 1024 px renders against camera photos of nearly the same size, with no one-person StyleGAN reconstruction rung, and SA1 row 14 shows that GAN one-person renders get flagged. The verdict feeds a SOTA/FEI decision. Fix: label H-GD0 "morph vs camera photo; GAN render cue not separated"; couple it to GD2, so that if Sb's GD2 is "flagged like a morph" or "inconclusive", "transfers" is written "not shown to be morph detection".
3. Major: Z2a is in-source and cannot settle F10. τ5 is set on the FFHQ calibration half and checked on the FFHQ evaluation half, which gives about 0.05 by construction; it is also checked on test R0 from the same source as c1. Fix: label Z2a "within calibration sources"; register now a Z2b-τ5 line (flag shares at τ5 on the TT1-FS unseen pools, G_unseen ≤ 0.10 on ≥ 4 of 5).
4. Major: the Z2-ρ permutation p is anti-conservative. The 15 networks form 3 arm clusters, so ρ ≤ −0.6 can come from the three arm means alone (effective n about 3). Fix: print the within-arm permutation p and the arm-mean ρ, and label the verdict "across recipes (3 arms)".
5. Major: the Z3 rule leans towards "engine gap". R2w OpenCV is a trained negative of B and Sb, and the FaceMorpher seam is weaker. A false gap launches arm #6 (5 GPU-h). Fix: also require Δ_R0 ≥ 0.03 for "gap" and ≤ 0.01 for "no gap".
6. Major: "underfit → train twice as long" does not follow. S4 rows are repeated about 3.5 times and S3 rows not at all, and S3 and S4 differ only in the identity of the spliced face. Fix: add an outcome "S3 underfit with S4 fit: class weighting or identity, no SL1 without an author decision"; print train-vs-dev gaps.
7. Minor: the reader crashes on a missing or off-pin tar; it should record None and keep the denominator at 5.
8. Minor: the GD1 frame-free rule still compares across resampling history (G is downsampled to 256 px, REC is native 256). Label A's GD1 verdict "history-confounded" and print G's flags beside test R0's.
9. Minor: Z0's holds/differs comparison is not implemented, and boot_auc is never called.
10. Minor: R2w is a trained negative of B and Sb; state it on the Z4 row 6 and Z6 labels.
11. Minor: ar1.csv has 1,326 FRLL rows and ar1.npz is loaded whole; filter both to manifest ids at load. The engine check should cover A and B as well as Sb. State that AF0 morphs use the same Delaunay/OpenCV engine.
12. Minor: the Z6 quintile strata are very unbalanced (2 vs 75, 67 vs 9), leaving residual softness; print deciles, or require ≥ 4 strata with ≥ 10 of each class.
13. Minor: Z5 should say "networks that passed the C2P gate". Computing A's Δm_att from the hold-out tar is an extra look at a freeze hold-out and needs the C2P owner's and the lead's OK.
14. Minor: the GD tolerance gate is looser than TT1-FS's byte identity on the same GPU and would pass the excluded GB10 differences. Report the TT1-FS gate beside it; add a non-finite rule. A smoke test beside the running fit breaks the lock's intent.
15. Minor: disclosure. The mock printed real cue numbers (lapvar-alone 0.772 and 0.503, stratum counts), and PN1 amendment 9 frozen heads have read DG1. Add both to section 1.
