# Review of the own-crop re-score of the audit rows (RS1), 12 Nov 2026

**Verdict: needs qualification. One central conclusion (4) is unsupported, and several numbers in 1, 2 and 6 rest on corrupt crops.** The acceptance rule was applied as registered. Most FRLL-landmark and MAD22 rows hold. But the primary crop (variant 1, the unchanged SMDD script) does not crop the face on any image whose face fills the frame. It cuts out a strip a few pixels wide, and the detector then scores that strip. This hits most MorDIFF morphs, every MorDIFF ladder image, about half of FRLL FaceMorpher and over a third of FRLL OpenCV. As a result, the variant-1 MorDIFF row (0.00 against 1.10), the variant-1 MorDIFF history effect (+3.9), the 96% stored-small flag rate, the 10.8% at the 0.5 rule and the whole variant-1 ladder (0.991 / 0.503 / 0.529 / 0.481) are crop artefacts. They are not readings of the detector.

## Findings, most severe first

**F1 (blocker): the unchanged script turns face-filling inputs into slivers.**
- Cause: `image_preprocess.py` line 40 slices `img[round(y1):round(y2), round(x1):round(x2)]`. When the 5% margin pushes x1 or y1 below 0, the slice start wraps to the end of the image, so the crop is an edge strip, or empty when the end index is smaller.
- CPU re-run of the script logic (BGR into MTCNN on CPU, 6 images per set; scratch script `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-narolski-mambamorph/c2840dc9-dd37-4619-9347-d8a76080b93f/scratchpad/rs1_v1crop.py`):
  - MorDIFF morphs give crops of 6x11, 3x5, 200x7, 188x8, 3x14 and 1x17 pixels.
  - Stored-small photos (A43 arm E) give 21x17, 14x146, 10x9 and 0x3.
  - FRLL FaceMorpher gives 16x35, 23x20 and 2x26.
- The same check across all images, using the variant-2 boxes as a proxy and the variant-1 drop list (`rs1_box2.py`):
  - MorDIFF: 987 of 1,000 boxes start below 0; 933 are kept as slivers and 55 come out empty (the "dropped").
  - Each A43 arm: 85 to 97% slivers.
  - FRLL FaceMorpher: 572 of the 806 kept morphs are slivers.
  - FRLL OpenCV: 439 slivers.
  - Not affected: AMSL, WebMorph, StyleGAN2, MAD22, DiM, raw E7, aligned frames and raw FRLL photos (0 boxes start below 0).
- What the detector does with them: ViT-B/16 variant-1 logits are about -0.6 for every sliver pool, whatever the content. Medians are E -0.62, D20 -0.58, B20 -0.55 and MorDIFF -0.57, against photos at -11.90. ViT-L/14 gives the same plateau at about -0.4. In variant 2 the same images spread from -11.5 (E) to +6.2 (B20).
- So variant-1 "stored-small vs photo 0.991" only means "sliver vs real crop", and the other variant-1 rungs compare slivers with slivers (about 0.5).
- Fix:
  - Register amendment 7. Every variant-1 cell where more than 5% of crops are slivers or empty is labelled "release crop script fails on these files"; it gets no reproduction label and no audit claim.
  - Use variant 2 as the only valid rows for MorDIFF, the ladder, FRLL FaceMorpher and OpenCV.
  - Withdraw the variant-1 rows ViT-L/14 MorDIFF 2.95, FE ViT-B/16 2.85, FE ViT-L/14 5.90 and MixFaceNet 6.36 as evidence about non-reproduction.

**F2 (major): conclusion 4's crop contrast is not a storage-format effect.**
- All A43 images and all MorDIFF morphs are PNG, so both variants store their crops as PNG (`crops_sha256.txt`: every a43_* and M_mordiff crop is .png).
- The 0.503 → 0.941 change has the same format in both arms. It is the sliver defect. It is not selection either: variant 2 restricted to the images variant 1 kept gives 0.940, 0.660 and 0.473.
- Only "stored-small vs photo" mixes formats, because in variant 1 the photo crop is a JPEG generation and E is PNG.
- Valid ladder for ViT-B/16 (variant 2 only):
  - stored-small vs photo 0.890; this still mixes DiffAE's tight framing and size with storage;
  - reconstruction vs stored-small 0.941;
  - morph vs reconstruction 0.664 [0.562, 0.754];
  - published vs our morph 0.481.
  - At the 5%-photo threshold it flags 100% of one-photo reconstructions.

**F3 (major): the FRLL history arms are internally consistent, but the flag rates use a mismatched baseline.**
- The history effect compares aligned E7 against aligned native. Both arms are our frames through the release crop, stored PNG, with the same raw morphs, so the aligner is not confounded between the arms.
- The flag rates, however, use t5 set on raw JPEG-crop photos. At that threshold, aligned native photos with no history are already flagged at 10.3% (ViT-B/16), 9.3% (MixFaceNet) and 8.8% (FE ViT-B/16), not 5%.
- Report "38 to 56% against 10.3% for the same aligned photos without history". The owner's "38 to 54" omits WebMorph (55.9).
- Add a descriptive t5 set on the aligned native pool.

**F4 (major): the raw-history evidence lives only in variant 2.**
- The variant-1 MorDIFF +3.9 compares E7 photos (real crops) with sliver morphs and is void.
- Variant 2, ViT-B/16, MorDIFF reproduced (0.55 against 1.10): raw E7 raises the EER by +12.2 [8.3, 16.3] and flags 67.6% at t5 (aligned native 7.4%).
- MixFaceNet variant 2: +21.1 [17.2, 25.0]. FE ViT-B/16 variant 2: +26.0.
- At the 0.5 rule, variant 2, ViT-B/16: raw E7 2.0% and stored-small 1.5% (not 10.8%). The same rule catches only 4.0% of AMSL, 0.8% of StyleGAN2 and 21% of WebMorph morphs, so the release rule is not a meaningful operating point for this release. Print miss rates beside it.

**F5 (major): near-zero cells and the window.**
- For published values of 6 or less, W = 1.5 means a "reproduced" label only shows the EER is not much worse than published.
- 0.00 against 1.10 (a sliver artefact in any case) and MAD22 FaceMorpher 0.00 against 0.0 confirm only "no worse".
- At about 25% the window is ±10 points (FS ViT-L/14 MorDIFF 37.7 against 40.9).
- Labels flip with platform or crop variant:
  - ViT-B/16 WebMorph: 1.88 in A46, 2.33 here;
  - ViT-B/16 AMSL: 3.92 in variant 1 (reproduced), 6.38 in variant 2 (not reproduced);
  - ViT-B/16 OpenCV: 1.97 in variant 1 (with slivers), 1.06 in variant 2 (not reproduced).
- The authors' own log gives 12.08 for ViT-L/14 AMSL, yet we "reproduce" 7.26. Matching a table value therefore does not show the same pipeline.

**F6 (minor): dropped images.**
- All are attack-class or control images: 416 FaceMorpher and 55 MorDIFF morphs, 10 OpenCV, 116 in the A43 arms. No genuine FRLL photo is dropped; FFHQ about 3%, FEI 4.
- They are not random: they are the images whose face box touches the top or left edge.
- Bias on the EER is small for ViT-B/16 (variant 2 on the kept subset 0.56 against 0.53 on all) and moderate for MixFaceNet FaceMorpher (4.01 against 2.94).
- The drops matter less than F1. Report drops and slivers together.

**F7 (minor): ViT-L/14 "does not show it" over-claims.**
- Its genuine logits are saturated (variant 1, raw photos: median -12.80, SD 0.056). An absent EER change is partly a floor.
- MAD22 FaceMorpher shows +1.98 [0.56, 4.59], with 17.6% flagged against 3.4% for aligned native.
- Stored-small paired shift +11.1 logits, though only in variant 1 (slivers); variant 2 flags 6.4%.

**F8 (minor): multiplicity and SelfMAD.**
- About 7 releases × 17 cells × 2 variants × 5 pools, with no correction. The headline effects have lower bounds of 8.8 or more and would survive Bonferroni at about 100 tests; FaceMorpher +1.85 [0.11, 3.44] would not.
- SelfMAD: the genuine-class definition differs per cell. Greedy-DiM +80.8 holds only with all photos as genuine (neutral-only 3.07 is not reproduced); the FRLL rows hold only with neutral-only. Print both every time.
- The Greedy-DiM +80.8 is the cleanest raw-file history row in the experiment: raw E7 against raw photos, its own crop.

## New hypotheses

- **H-new-1: the published MorDIFF numbers came from larger or uncropped MorDIFF files, not `MorDIFF_crop`.**
  - Why: variant 1 (slivers) is far off; variant 2 matches the frozen-encoder rows (18.1 / 22.1 against 17.9 / 22.9); the authors' log equals Table 1 on MorDIFF.
  - Test, zero GPU: read the release log and configs for MorDIFF paths and sizes. If a full-frame copy exists, run the unchanged script on it (CPU-sized job).
  - Falsified if the authors' path is `MorDIFF_crop`.
- **H-new-2: on MorDIFF, ViT-B/16 detects DiffAE decoding more than morphing.**
  - Prediction: in variant 2 the 100-step reconstructions (arm D) score as high as or higher than D20, and both above the 5% threshold. The same holds for the same-person mixes (C20 against D20 about 0.5).
  - Test: existing `rs1_ladder*.csv`, zero GPU.
  - Falsified if D is flagged at under 50% at t5 or C20 against D20 is above 0.65.
- **H-new-3: the AMSL reproduction of ViT-B/16 depends on the extra JPEG generation in the crop step.**
  - Why: variant 1 gives 3.92, variant 2 gives 6.38.
  - Prediction: in the existing JPEG-vs-PNG check, AMSL morph logits rise and photo logits do not.
  - Test: `rs1_jpeg_storage_check.json` per family, zero GPU.
  - Falsified if the AMSL morph shift is no larger than the photo shift.
- **H-new-4: ViT-L/14 is blind to history mostly because of saturation, not invariance.**
  - Prediction: more than 50% of its genuine logits are tied at the float32 floor, and the history shift is concentrated in the unsaturated photos.
  - Test: zero GPU, existing scores.
  - Falsified if under 20% are tied.
