## Findings
1. **Major: the gain intervals leave out seed-to-seed variance.** They resample host blocks only. A seed-level t-interval on the 5 paired gains gives Sb − B: SD 0.030, [−0.027, +0.049]; Sb − A: SD 0.023, [−0.003, +0.054]. s405 is the weakest seed for all three arms (A 0.811, B 0.824, Sb 0.862). "The lower bound for Sb − A is above zero" may not be written without that caveat.
2. **Major: "3 of 5" is not a partial licence (c).** Write "(b); (c) not reached". The counts are not comparable across arms, because the bars differ: own gbt learner A 0.885, B 0.833, Sb 0.859.
3. **Major: the adversary floors are cross-fitted on these rows; the own-list learners are not.** The floors are in-domain supervised references; the networks and the own-list learners never saw FFHQ or FaceMorpher. Write "in-domain hand-made statistics fitted on these rows outrank every network". The best single statistic reaches 0.878 [0.856, 0.901], about the B/Sb level, with a winner's-curse caveat.
4. **Major: the flags on W.** Face-only W is flagged at A 0.51–0.90, B 0.47–0.99, Sb 0.69–0.93; median logits on face-only W are above 0 for 14 of 15 networks (B s407 −0.17). On the whole frame W is flagged at 2–14%, close to H (1–10%). The grey surround alone flips most warp-only images to "attack". The face-only result is about ranking, not deployment behaviour. Whole minus face (+0.094 to +0.115) confirms the blanking shift.
5. **Minor: predictions.** The arm-AUC predictions missed (predicted Sb 0.60–0.80, B 0.55–0.75, A 0.50–0.70; observed 0.889, 0.879, 0.864). "Sb − B inconclusive" and "no arm reaches (c)" held, the latter narrowly at 3 of 5.
6. **Minor: whole-frame order reverses.** Sb 0.983 vs B 0.990, so Sb − B ≈ −0.007 (printed only, near ceiling). Any reading of a face-only Sb advantage must mention it.
7. **Minor: halo ring.** About 7.6% of the hull. The ring-share terciles are flat (Sb 0.888–0.897): weak, descriptive evidence that the ring is not the main cue.
