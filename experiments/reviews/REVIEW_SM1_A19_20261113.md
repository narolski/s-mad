## Findings, most severe first

**F1 (major: H-N4 over-claim, and the A16 text now contradicts it).**
- `result.md:817-819` says "so smoothness does not explain it". Only face-box Laplacian quintiles were held equal. Stratifying moved arm e by just +0.007 (0.241 to 0.248), so this one statistic is ruled out.
- PNG size and other detail measures are not ruled out. The previous review found within-host correlations of −0.26 to −0.40 with PNG-size differences (REVIEW_SM1_A16_A18 F6), stronger than with sharpness.
- `result.md:808-811` still says "consistent with the heads reading smoothness as morph-like (partial explanation; A19 H-N4 below tests it)".
- Rewrite of the H-N4 sentence: "Holding face-box Laplacian sharpness equal (quintiles) barely moves the ranking (e 0.241 → 0.248), so this sharpness statistic does not explain why the reconstruction scores above the fresh-noise renders; other detail measures (PNG size, high-frequency energy) are not tested."
- Rewrite of the A16 sentence at :809-811: "The reconstruction is the smoothest rung (median face-box Laplacian 87.5 against 103 and 128), but holding that sharpness equal does not remove the ordering (A19 H-N4, falsified); the cause is open."

**F2 (major: H-N2 is undecided by design, and its sign is ambiguous).**
- **Power.** With 66 rows the interval is about ±0.23 wide. The falsifying branch (upper bound < 0.25) therefore needed ρ of about 0.02 or lower. The design could almost only return "undecided".
- **Over-control.** The extended floor contains the similarity of the two noise maps. In DiffAE the noise map carries identity: a fresh-noise render keeps a host cosine of only 0.33, against 0.96 for the reconstruction (DG1 README:491-497). Controlling for that floor removes much of the identity variance the test was looking for.
- **Sign.** The render-to-partner cosine is mostly driven by how alike host and partner are, and partners were assigned as look-alikes (`src/AR1/pool.py:153-169`). A network that reads two identities would score more-alike pairs lower. So "rises with partner cosine" is not a clean prediction. The all-X read given F, −0.147 [−0.312, +0.031], fits either account.
- Rewrite of `result.md:829-830`, replacing "No sign that…": "No sign that the score rises with render-to-partner similarity; the test had little power (66 rows, interval width 0.46), its floor contains the noise-map similarity that in DiffAE also carries identity, and with look-alike partners the expected sign is not fixed."

**F3 (moderate: H-N1, the platform difference cannot change the registered branch).**
- I compared the CPU scores with the stored scores on the 401 check rows (`/mnt/t7/smad_next/SM1/a19/res/*/check_scores.npz`). The test AUCs agree to the fourth decimal: X20/SAME20 0.9887 / 0.9886 / 0.9893 on both platforms, and SAME20/REC20 differs by at most 0.0003.
- The logit differences have median 0.0001 and 99th percentile 0.013–0.032. I added resampled differences of that size to the rung scores. NOISEX/NOISES then moves by at most 0.0075 and SEMX/SEMS by at most 0.0081.
- SEMX/SEMS (2-seed mean 0.738) would need to reach ≤ 0.70 or ≥ 0.85 to land in a decided branch. So the registered reading is foreseeably "undecided".
- Under-claim. The descriptive pattern is informative and should say so. Noise-only mixes separate better than semantic-only mixes (0.92 against 0.74), even though SEMX moves further from the host's identity than NOISEX does (host cosine 0.578 against 0.744; DG1 README:494). That argues against an identity-dilution reading. The semantic path still carries some signal (0.74).
- Add this sentence: "Post hoc, platform sensitivity (reviewer): on the 401 check rows the test AUCs agree to 1e-4, and perturbing the rung scores by the observed differences moves SEMX over SEMS by at most 0.008, so the registered branch cannot be reached by the platform change (needs −0.038 or +0.11)."
