- colorFERET: two-person 5, same-person 0.

Also print two-person same-day scores against all same-person rows as a labelled n ≤ 8 anecdote, or drop it.

**F2 (major): the matched-W design has no caliper, no balance check and no conclusion rule.** Lines 173-186 do greedy nearest-neighbour matching without replacement, from the highest same-person W down, with no caliper. The amendment ties H-new-2's prediction to read (b) (`prereg.md` line 124) but gives no rule. Fix the code: drop a pair if |ln W_two − ln W_same| > 0.10, and print n matched, n dropped, the mean |Δ ln W| and medians. Replacement text:
> "Matched 1:1 nearest on ln W, without replacement, caliper 0.10 in ln W; unmatched pairs dropped and counted. Descriptive only: with n ≈ 40 per side the AUC's SE is about 0.065, so the read can only say 'consistent with H-new-2' (AUC ≤ 0.60) or 'against H-new-2' (AUC ≥ 0.70); otherwise 'not resolved'. No verdict on H-new-2 from (b)."

### Checked and ok
- **Stratifiers are per class, not per host.** `strat_auc` uses pooled quantiles of each row's own E, W or D (`bd1_read.py` lines 41-56).
- **W** is the mean over 106 points in the 512 frame (images are 512×512, the same `.tem` files that make the hull). All .tem files exist. Two-person W is H to B and same-person W is H to S.
- **E** is the sum over B1-B5 inside the H ∪ B hull of the centred half-difference (`tb1_measure.py` lines 99-106), and p_k = E_k / ΣE.
- **Bootstrap:** copies of a component keep their label and so share a fold.

- The script reads only development data: the FRGC/colorFERET feature file, BD1's D values, the pool file and the .tem landmark files. It touches no benchmark, sealed or held-out files.
