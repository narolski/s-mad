## Findings
1. **Major (calibration).** The fm61 half is in-distribution. train_SbM3 holds 200 fm61 copies of FFHQ u50 morphs, made with the same engine and the same e7 operator (MS1 prereg 97-101). amsl112 is the informative half.
2. **Moderate.** Seed 406 amsl112 (−0.101) is driven by 2 of 149 morphs (+2.96 → −1.74, +1.21 → −3.08). That seed's median is +0.010 and its SE is 0.113. These are rare large flips, not a mean drift.
3. **Moderate (ceiling).**
   - Native miss is 0.7-1.3% and AUC ≥ 0.999, so the test can detect only large evasion.
   - Mitigation: SbM3's native SD of 0.43-0.65, against 0.84-1.19 for Sb, makes the test stricter for it.
4. **Minor (scope).**
   - Only the e7 storage operator is tested.
   - This is not the C2P.4 freeze requirement.
5. **Minor (stale labels).** Update the Z5 heading and the "could not be verified" line. Mark FFHQ as untrained for Sb.
