# Pre-run review: SM1 amendment A18 (training on our own diffusion ladder, H-n2)

Reviewer: experiment-reviewer (Opus), 12 Nov 2026, read-only. Report as returned, unedited.

**Verdict: GO WITH FIXES.** The operations are sound. The decision rule as written cannot tell a second-identity signal from the noise-correlation trace, so fixes 1 to 3 must be in the prereg before registration.

I checked everything read-only against `/home/narolski/smad-next` at commit 0fa4e9e and `/mnt/t7/smad_next`. I opened no benchmark, FST, A12SP or valx files.

**Blockers**

B1. **The falsifier sits below a trace floor that survives the Laplacian stratification.**
- On the test shards 6–7, θ alone separates X20 from SAME20 at oriented AUC 0.906. Within the same Laplacian quintiles the decision uses, θ still separates them at 0.872.
- θ is higher for X20 than for SAME20 in 94% of test hosts.
- So an RS network that reaches the falsifier (0.75, lower bound > 0.65) can do it entirely by reading the render trace. The "falsified" sentence (`prereg.md` A18 decision bullet) would then be unsupported.
- Matching on θ is not workable: θ quintiles hold 0/54, 4/49, 38/16, 47/6 and 45/9 X/SAME, so a matched subset has about 30 SAME rows and its interval is roughly ±0.11.
- Fix: replace the decision with an **incremental test over a fixed floor**. Exact text:
  > "Floor F = logistic regression of X20 vs SAME20 on (θ, face-box Laplacian, PNG bytes, asymmetry, d), fit on shards 0–5 only. Primary read = ΔAUC on shards 6–7 of a logistic model on (F inputs + network logit) over F alone, both fit on shards 0–5 training rows, 2-seed mean, partner-cycle bootstrap. Falsified (signal beyond measured traces) if ΔAUC ≥ 0.05 with lower bound > 0; supported if the upper bound < 0.03; otherwise undecided. The Laplacian-stratified and θ-stratified AUCs are secondary."
- Caveat: `hn2_read.py` already writes scores for the training rows, so the fit is possible. Training rows are memorised, though (see M1), so it is safer to cross-fit on test-shard host folds instead. Register one of the two now.

B2. **Both outcome sentences overclaim.**
- "Supported" (≤ 0.65) while θ gives 0.906 means the recipe failed to learn even a known, strong trace. That is a statement about the recipe's sensitivity, not the absence of second identity.
- Supported text: "With this generator and this recipe (6,250 steps on 803–1,205 images, 2 seeds), the network adds no X-vs-SAME information beyond simple statistics and the noise-angle θ. This does not show that no second-identity signal exists in diffusion morphs."
- Falsified text: "…adds information beyond the measured traces (θ, sharpness, size, asymmetry); unmeasured render traces are not excluded."

**Majors**

M1. **The "unchanged recipe" is about 50 times more epochs than usual.** 6,250 steps × batch 256 is about 1,990 epochs on arm R (803 rows) and 1,330 on RS (1,205 rows). The SMDD fits ran about 40 epochs (39,734 rows in `train_sm1a_s401.csv`). Memorisation could produce either outcome. Fix: disclose this in the claim and report training-row AUC per fit. Changing the step count needs an amendment, and I do not recommend it now.

M2. **The bootstrap clusters on the wrong unit.**
- `hn2_read.py` resamples X20 hosts. But X20(A,B) shares person B with SAME20(B,B) and REC20(B).
- The test shards hold 55 partner cycles (largest 11), which is enough to resample. Fix: "bootstrap over partner cycles (55 in test)".
- At n = 133/134, the half-width is about 0.06–0.07, so the B1 thresholds can be detected.

M3. **No rule for a missing or non-finite fit.**
- `remote_hn2.sh` stops retrying when the loss goes non-finite, which is correct. But the reader's `glob` then silently averages whatever tars exist.
- Add to the prereg: "A non-finite fit is never rerun and is reported. The decision needs both RS seeds. With fewer, the verdict is 'undecided (incomplete)'." Add the matching assert to the reader.

**Minors**
- Split: hosts and partner cycles are disjoint. Only 1 cycle spans both sides and its 2 crossing rows are dropped. The 163-person cycle is entirely in training.
- Test hosts are all rented-GPU FRGC renders. Training includes 134 Athena hosts (109 colorFERET). Each host contributes one X, one SAME and one REC, so platform and database are balanced across labels. Limit the claim to "FRGC, single render platform".
- θ has no missing values on any X/SAME row. `d` is NaN only for REC20, so the floors are valid.
- Flag counts are uneven: X20 has 10 flagged test rows, SAME20 has 3. Report the decision with and without flagged rows.
- Arm R (X vs REC) repeats what A15 and PN1 already show (about 0.65). Keep it only as descriptive. If cost matters, a third RS seed (403) gives more.

**Improvements (ranked)**
1. **Linear probe on frozen features, 0 GPU.** Per-image CLIP and DINOv2 features already exist for every DG1 shard (`/mnt/t7/smad_next/FZ2/results/dgx/out/clip_mad_addon_s{0..7}.npz`, 768-d). Train on shards 0–5, test on 6–7, X vs SAME, with the same incremental-over-floor read. It answers "is there linearly decodable signal beyond θ" in minutes. Register it as a parallel arm with the same thresholds; that needs a prereg line only.
2. B1's floor model needs only CPU.
3. A partner-cycle bootstrap.
