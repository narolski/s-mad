- PR1 result.md:117 (Z4, 191 test hosts, OpenCV, unstratified) printed R4 vs R3 as A 0.72-0.75, B 0.74-0.79, Sb 0.80-0.85. That is about +0.06 for Sb − B and +0.08 for Sb − A.

- BD1 result.md:25 and :37 printed D-stratified S4 vs S3 as Sb 0.697-0.729 against A and B 0.513-0.560.

The direction and rough size of all four FS1 verdicts were therefore public before the "prediction" (prereg.md:583-584). Replace 586 with:
> "Known before this amendment and not checked by the owner at registration: PR1 Z4 (result.md:117) printed unstratified R4 vs R3 on the 191 test hosts for A, B and Sb at 403-407 (0.72-0.75 / 0.74-0.79 / 0.80-0.85); PR1 Z3b the engine gap; BD1 (result.md:25, 37) D-stratified S4 vs S3 for Sb (0.697-0.729) and for A and B (0.513-0.560). The FS1 read is therefore a re-read of the same networks on the development plus 80 clean test hosts with the amendment-4 statistic, not an independent or blind replication; the predictions were made with these values public."

Also relabel it: "a second seed set and platform for the recipe; not an independent test of the frozen networks".

**2. Major: the 10% switch is not data-dependent, so fix the deciding statistic now.** Whether a bootstrap draw is readable depends only on D and on how many images of each class fall in each D stratum (ab1_pm_read.py:54-71). It never depends on scores. The committed mock files already show the result: the primary read has 0 unreadable draws, and S4 vs S3 on the 80 clean hosts has 754 of 2,000 (37.7%) unreadable, for both pairs.

The switch therefore always sends S4 vs S3 to the unstratified statistic. That brings back the D difference between classes that BD1's statistic was built to remove, and in that branch the reader drops every stratified condition (pm2 `extra_ok = True`). Replacement text for 561-565:
> "Deciding statistic, fixed now from the score-free draw counts (mock: 0 of 2,000 unreadable on the primary, 754 of 2,000 on S4 vs S3 clean80): primary R4 vs R3 decides stratified with 4b's unstratified-agreement condition; S4 vs S3 clean80 decides on the unstratified seed-mean gain and its bootstrap interval, and 'present' also requires the stratified seed-mean gain on the full 80 hosts to be > 0. The unreadable share is printed. The amendment-4 S4-vs-S3 verdict stands as read; its unstratified interval is recomputed and printed beside it."

Reader change: in the unstratified branch, set `extra_ok = out["seed_mean"] is not None and out["seed_mean"] > 0`, then re-pin the reader.

**3. Major: the Sb − Sp wording mixes up what Sp lacks.** prereg.md:52-59:
- Sp has 5,223 unique images, Sb 5,535 and B 5,099. Sp lacks exactly the 156 S0 and 156 S3 negatives.
- Sp gets no repeated images to match volume, and has 7,762 rows after balance against Sb's 8,386 (B's 7,762 is the same as Sp's).
- Steps are the same (6,250 × 256), so total samples match. Each Sp image is seen about 1.06× as often as an Sb image, and the training class mix shifts toward positives.

So Sb − Sp tests the 312 same-person negatives together with their count and class-mix effect. There is no volume-control arm for Sp. Sp "keeps" the 124 S4 positives, not Sb's image count. Replace 568-572 with:
> "Sb − Sp tests the 312 matched S0 + S3 negatives (with their image count and class-mix shift; no volume control exists for Sp). If it is 'absent' or 'inconclusive' while Sb − B is 'present', the gain is attributed to what Sp keeps over B: the 124 S4 splice positives (and their count). The S4-vs-S3 prediction is near-certain from H-sp3b (Sp − Sb development −0.057 to −0.095) and is not a test."
