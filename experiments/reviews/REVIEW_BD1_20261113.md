## Findings
1. **Major: the S0 control is weak.**
   - S0 is a trained negative with logits at the floor (SD 0.14-0.52, against 2.46-2.72 for S3).
   - S0's D is warp disagreement (D_dc median 1.01, against 7.51 for S3).
   - Restricted to S0's D range, S3 keeps ρ 0.20-0.30. The "needs blended content" sentence over-claims.
2. **Major (post hoc): colour does not explain the residual.**
   - D is mostly mean colour shift (ρ(D_dc, D) 0.86 within S3).
   - Sb within quintiles of D_dc: 0.701-0.736; of D_ac: 0.690-0.730; of D_lin: 0.691-0.725; of a cross-fitted 7-statistic propensity: 0.681-0.719.
   - These are oracle diagnostics (they use the counterpart image).
3. **Moderate (post hoc): FR similarity.**
   - ρ(S3 logit, cos_HS) is −0.31 to −0.38 on 14/14 Sb networks (A and B −0.18 to −0.19). The partial correlation given D and colour is −0.17 to −0.25.
   - ρ(k, cos_HS) is −0.34.
   - Within S4, ρ(logit, cos_HB) is about 0, but cos_HB covers a narrow range.
   - The glintr100 cosines already exist (`AR1/pool.py`), so no approval is needed.
4. **Moderate: the residual is inside the hull** (blanked rows: face-blanked 0.48-0.50, background-blanked 0.66-0.75).
5. **Minor: the threshold is met only on point estimates.**
   - Paired CIs of the excess: [0.106, 0.257], [0.092, 0.243], [0.069, 0.225], [0.105, 0.248], [0.089, 0.238].
   - Pass is excluded; +0.12 is not cleared by the lower bounds. Print the registered primary CIs.
6. **Minor: host-paired read.** On the 74 hosts with D_S3 ≥ D_S4, Sb ranks S4 above S3 in 0.568-0.649 of hosts.
7. **Minor: wording.** Sb was trained on this contrast. A and B are not a mechanism control.
