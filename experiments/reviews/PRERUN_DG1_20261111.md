## 1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES (production already started; do not cancel it)

Production was submitted at 19:15 (array 3545841, estimate 2.6 GPU-hours) while this review was running. The smoke job passed at 19:11 and the equivalence gate passed (per-image mean difference 0.017 to 0.059 grey levels, max 4).

The registered rungs are built correctly and matched, so nothing running needs to change. The batch is incomplete for the sprint's readings, not wrong. Three fixes, in order of urgency:

2. **Tonight or tomorrow:** a self-contained add-on array with the audit rungs and saved codes, about 4 GPU-hours (findings 2 to 4).
3. **At manifest time, no GPU:** person-use and cue columns (findings 5 and 6).

## 2. Findings

**1. Major: no reading rule exists for these images.**
- `prereg.md:4` says no detector is scored, and no other registration names DG1.
- The validity table (Laplacian variance and asymmetry per rung) is itself the simple baseline of every contrast, and it is computed at copy-back.
- Fix: a one-page reading registration first, covering:
  - Primary contrast: two-person morph against same-person mix (set B, 20 steps).
  - Morph against reconstruction is printed only beside the fresh-noise render.
  - The mirror mix is a trace contrast only.
  - AUC with the two-way person bootstrap.
  - Every detector number as excess over the best of PNG bytes, Laplacian variance, asymmetry, 32-pixel thumbnail and surround-only thumbnail.
  - Flag rates under both class policies at a camera-photo threshold.
  - Which approved path brings the 256-px files to each detector, identical for all rungs.

**2. Major: the reconstruction is the only rung rendered from its own noise map, and the rungs that test this are missing.**
- `dg1_lists.py:142-153` builds no fresh-noise, noise-only or semantic-only cell.
- The paired-differences first pass (PD1, `diffae/result.md:29-33`) already shows the problem: PNG size alone gives 0.731 for morph against reconstruction, and same-person mixes score above morphs (0.249).
- The frozen-features experiment (FZ1) has Laplacian variance at 0.966 at 100 steps.
- Round 3 asked for these cells: `paired_vetting.md:333, 368-371, 544-545`; `mechanism_vetting.md:249-254, 280-283`.
- Fix: add-on cells per host:
  - Fresh-noise render of the host's semantic code (two seeded draws).
  - Noise-only and semantic-only mixes, each with the partner's photo and with the host's own second photo.
- This needs a prereg amendment and the lead's yes (`paired_vetting.md:378`).

**3. Major: the codes are not saved.**
- `dg1_gen.py:168-187` keeps them in memory only.
- Saving was requested three times: `mechanism_vetting.md:290, 440` and `literature/crossfield/diffusion_check.md:360`.
- Consequence: the add-on must invert again (1,914 sources at 3.4 s, about 1.8 GPU-hours).
- Fix: the add-on saves the semantic codes, noise maps and fresh-noise draws (about 1.5 GB).
- It should also re-render reconstruction, same-person mix and morph in the same process. Then no contrast mixes two runs, and the re-renders give a free run-to-run check.

**4. Major: no unconfounded same-person rung at 100 steps.**
- Set B is 20 steps only (`prereg.md:60-67`).
- Set A's 100-step same-person rung is the neutral plus smiling mix, marked confounded (`prereg.md:50`).
- The strongest separations in the record are at 100 steps (0.966, and 0.852 for the frozen-encoder release in the MorDIFF ladder re-read, AD1).
- Fix: reconstruction, same-person mix, morph and fresh-noise at 100 steps on set B, about 1 GPU-hour.

**5. Major: person use is not carried in the manifest.**
- `prereg.md:58` states only disjointness from FRLL.
- What I measured on the pool:

| Group | Count |
|---|---|
| Validation-reserved hosts | 8 |
| Other hosts whose partner is validation-reserved | 8 |
| Test-read hosts under the protocol-network split (PN1) | 191 of 536 |
| Training hosts under that split | 156 |
| Set-B hosts the existing PD1 heads were fitted on | 152 (60 of them test hosts) |
| Dose hosts that are test-read | 36 of 100 (4 dose hosts are validation-reserved) |
| Largest partner cycle | 163 hosts |

- Folds by host alone leak the partner, because of the cycles.
- Fix: manifest columns for split role, test-read, validation-reserved (host and partner), cycle id, and PD1-training person. Extend the dose series to all test-read hosts in the add-on.

**6. Minor: pipeline cues to flag and print as baselines** (measured on the 1,072 aligned inputs).
- Black wedges: 26 of 109 colorFERET host photos and 29 of 109 second photos have them (FRGC: 1 and 3 of 427). A mix then shows a half-dark band that a reconstruction never has; it is visible in the DGX check morph.
- Greyscale: 7 colorFERET photos are greyscale, giving 7 same-person and 7 two-person mixes of colour with grey.
- Probable twins or duplicate identities: `frgc:04637`/`04638` and `frgc:04692`/`04856` (cosine 0.56 to 0.69), so 4 "two-person" morphs are doubtful.
- Pair disagreement: two-person input pairs differ more than same-person pairs (median 34.1 against 27.0 grey levels, AUC 0.67). Use it as a dose covariate.
- Set-B crops carry the aligner's blurred reflect padding at the top (seen in a sample crop, not counted), so the "genuine write" is not an untouched crop.
- Fix: flag columns, sensitivity lines, and database as a stratum.

**7. Minor: equivalence wording.**
- The registered tolerance (2 grey levels, `prereg.md:35`) is about 80 times the same-machine difference (0.024). The observed result passes any sensible limit.
- Morphs differ more across machines than reconstructions (max 2 to 4 against 1).
- So `README.md:149-150` ("may be combined with DGX images") should not be used inside one contrast. Set A regenerates every neutral arm, so it is not needed.

**8. Minor: operations and record.**
- One non-finite image ends a task after all renders with no tar (`dg1_gen.py:263-264`, `dg1_run_task.sh:26-37`, RAM disk). For the add-on: write the tar first, then fail; check the mixing angle is finite before rendering.
- `prereg.md:88` still says local scratch, but the jobs ran on the RAM disk. Add a deviations section. `README.md:215-219` is stale on the same point.
- The aligner found two faces in `frgc_04700_S` (`pool_align_p1.log`: 263 iterations for 262 files). I re-ran the detector: the first detection is the 268-px face, and `align.py:158-159` keeps the first, so the crop is right. `AD1b/README.md:44` says the opposite.
