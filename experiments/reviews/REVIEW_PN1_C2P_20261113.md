# Post-result review: contrast 2-prime (C2P), PN1 fresh-seed networks

**Verdict: the verdicts are sound as registered, with two wording qualifications. Nothing forbidden was read. A re-run on an A100 PCIe is admissible only as a disclosed, author-approved amendment that fixes TF32 on. An unchanged TF32-off re-run would test the wrong thing and could fail again.**

## In short

The test did not get to the networks. All 15 networks were rescored on an L40S with TF32 off. The logits stored for them were made on an A100 PCIe with TF32 on. The gate's maximum condition then removed all five plain networks, which are the positive control, so the contrast could not be decided.

- The registered verdicts follow mechanically from the rules.
- The four protocol-plus-splice (Sb) networks that passed the gate show small absolute storage reactions on unseen persons, and stored morphs did not become easier to miss.
- The registered question was "reduced relative to the plain recipe", and that cannot be answered yet.
- A re-run on the A100 PCIe the networks were trained on (pn1fs) is defensible. It must be registered first, use the TF32 setting that made the stored logits, and keep the plain networks' L40S hold-out scores unread until the re-run has been read.

I checked all numbers against `c2p_read.json` (sha256 62ee0bf4…) and the hashes of `C2P_m1.tar` (4e25fcc1…), `c2p_read.py` (45e72914…), `rescore.py` (6f6eede7…), `remote_c2p.sh` (263d764a…) and `drive_c2p.sh` (8b23d9aa…). Every hash matches its pin, and the numbers match your reading.

## Q1. Do the verdicts follow, and was anything forbidden read?

- **A's control met on 0 of 5 seeds.** This follows. C2P.2 says that when A fails the gate, "the control is not met on that seed". Gate maxima: A 0.249, 0.122, 0.153, 0.119, 0.205.
- **Contrast 2-prime "uninformative" for B and Sb.** This follows. C2P.3 says "Uninformative if A's control is met on ≤ 3 seeds at either size."
- **H-new-1 for Sb "falsified".** This follows, but report it as "4 of 4 read seeds, seed 404 not read". C2P.2 drops gate-failed seeds from the H-new-1 count, and 4 of 4 values are ≥ −0.10 at both sizes. The verdict has wide margins: the lowest value is −0.024 at 112.7 px (seed 403), against a bar of −0.10.
- **H-new-1 for B "inconclusive"; H-new-2 "inconclusive".** Mechanically correct. But nothing was measured for H-new-2 (its values are empty), and B had one seed. Rewrite both as: "not read: one B seed passed the gate / no A network passed the gate (C2P.2)". That avoids suggesting the data were ambiguous.
- **Freeze: no method.** This follows from FINAL section 1 (6691adc).
- **Nothing forbidden was read.** The reader opened:
  - the MS1 and FS1 evaluation manifests;
  - the sharpness (cues) file;
  - the FS1 stored `scores_eval.npz` files (gate rows);
  - `C2P_m1.tar`.

  No valx, FST or A12SP file was touched. One disclosure is needed: `c2p_read.py:137-141` loads the hold-out logits of every network, the gate-failed ones included, into memory before line 149 skips them. They were never computed on or written (the JSON `per_network` holds only B_s405 and Sb_s403/405/406/407). Record: "loaded, not used".
- **My own read.** For Q3 I read only the af0 and ffhq gate rows of the tar (development rows the gate had already used). I opened no ms1ffhq member. Script: `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-narolski-mambamorph/c2840dc9-dd37-4619-9347-d8a76080b93f/scratchpad/c2prev/gate_diag.py`.

## Findings (most severe first)

**F1, major (design): the gate tested a mismatch the protocol already forbade.**
- `PLATFORM_PINS_FOR_FINAL_READ.md` was committed at 02:10 (f2c2023), five hours before C2P was registered (07:03). Item 7 requires the TF32 setting to "equal the setting that produced any stored score". The stored logits come from `pn1_score.py:45-46` (TF32 on).
- The C2P run used `rescore.py` with torch defaults, which leaves matmul TF32 off (`env.txt`: "tf32 False").
- A wrapper that fixes this was already pinned: `src/TT1/rescore_tf32.py` 0d0b6b6f. It runs `rescore.py` unchanged with TF32 on.
- The registration also says "never two GPU models", yet it compared L40S scores against A100 scores.
- The pre-run check saw the mismatch ("measures GPU model and precision together. Say so.") and then wrongly said that tightening the max to 0.10 "costs nothing".
- So the failure comes from the set-up, not from the networks.

**F2, major: the gate is biased against the control arm.**
- On the 6,339 gate rows, rows with |Δ| > 0.05 number 32-46 for A, 6-24 for B and 0-12 for Sb. The 99th percentiles are A 0.036-0.043, B 0.016-0.036, Sb 0.012-0.019.
- Relative error at the 99th percentile is A 0.043-0.063 against Sb 0.017-0.034.
- The maximum sits at mid-range logits (stored −2.0 to +1.6), not at floor rows.
- So any cross-platform gate removes the plain networks first. Here that is the positive control.
- **Fix:** a confirmatory storage read takes all its scores from one job on one stack, the stack that stored the gate logits.

**F3, major (only if re-run): the read is now only partly blind.**
- Seen: hold-out statistics for Sb 403/405/406/407 and B 405.
- Not seen: any A statistic, B 403/404/406/407 and Sb 404.
- The seen numbers do not settle the verdict, because Sb's two-sided mean-shift condition (|Δm(Sb)| ≤ Δm(A)/3) needs:
  - Δm(A) ≥ 0.711 SD at seed 403 and 0.528 SD at seed 405, both at 61.2 px;
  - Δm(A) ≥ 0.435 and 0.414 SD at 112.7 px, same seeds;
  - for Sb 406, Δf(A) ≥ +11.3 points at 61.2 px and +10.3 points at 112.7 px.

  FS1's A shifts on other persons were 0.61-0.79 SD. So Sb 403 at 61.2 px may well fail, and a pass needs Sb 404, 405, 406 and 407 all to pass there.
- The outcome is genuinely open, but the decision to re-run was taken after seeing favourable Sb numbers. This must be disclosed.

**F4, minor: the reader's input handling.** `--c2p` accumulates its arguments, so a later tar overwrites an earlier one (`c2p_read.py:137-141`). A re-run must pass only the new tar and write to a new output path.

**F5, minor: the MS1 clause is ambiguous under "no method".** FINAL section 1 says "If MS1 is unregistered, unread, or B is frozen, the single-source arm is frozen", which presumes that an arm was frozen. The author should confirm before the evening that step 3 also blocks the three-source version.

**F6, major for SE1: with three seeds SE1 cannot be read.** SE1 prereg lines 114-117 say: 4 read seeds must meet a rule on 4 of 4, and 3 or fewer seeds are "not read (too few seeds)". Leases se1a, se1b and se1c carry only seeds 408-410. Seed 411 needs the author's budget approval and a start by 14:15, or SE1 yields no verdict.

**F7, minor: AB1's "replication" wording is wrong.** R1 (AB1 prereg:135-137) labels its outcome "replicated" or "not replicated", but there is no FS1 pass to replicate. Amend R1 to "first test at seeds 408-412" before any AB1 tar is read.

## Q2. Is an A100 PCIe re-run admissible?

**Yes, as an amendment, under these conditions.**
1. The gate failure was decided on development rows that carry no outcome information.
2. The key unknown, A's hold-out reaction, is still unseen.
3. The re-run is fixed in advance, run once, with no setting or tolerance chosen by result.
4. Both runs are reported.

**Do not run `rescore.py` unchanged with TF32 off.** On an A100 that still compares TF32-off scores against TF32-on logits. TT1 measured differences of up to 0.035 on floor rows even within one A100 stack, and the mid-range rows that fail here were not measured. Use `rescore_tf32.py`. It is pinned, runs `rescore.py` unchanged, and reproduces the scorer that stored the logits.

Prediction for pn1fs (the same physical GPU and image as the FS1 fits): max |Δ| < 1e-3 on all 15 networks. TT1 saw ≤ 2.2e-5 from batch composition on the same stack.

**It becomes inadmissible if any of these happens:**
- any gate-failed network's L40S hold-out score is computed, plotted or opened before the re-run read;
- the gate thresholds, reader, margins, sizes, seeds, counting rule or freeze rule change;
- more than one platform or TF32 setting is tried on hold-out rows;
- a third attempt follows a gate failure on the re-run;
- the first run is dropped from reporting;
- the amendment is committed after the re-run's scoring starts, or without the author's yes, since it changes FINAL's inputs;
- the read lands after the freeze time.

## Q3. Was max |Δ| ≤ 0.10 well chosen?

No, given the stack it was applied to.
- **Wrong references.** The 0.044 (A100 SXM4 against PCIe) and 0.022 (RS1) references were measured with matched precision. The one cross-stack reference available, GB10 against A100 at 0.061-0.088 on mid-range rows, left almost no headroom.
- **Wrong statistic.** A single-row maximum over 6,339 rows is arm-dependent (F2) and barely tied to the statistics C2P computes. Decision flips were 0-4 of 6,339 rows, and the 99th percentiles were 0.012-0.043, against an expected A shift of roughly 1 logit.
- **Do not loosen it after the fact.** Under the draft's 0.15, A 403, 405 and 407 still fail, so the verdict is the same. Any threshold that rescues A would be chosen knowing the outcome.
- **Paper wording:** "The registered gate (99th percentile ≤ 0.05, maximum ≤ 0.10, tightened from 0.15 before any score) failed on its maximum condition for all plain networks because scoring used another GPU model and TF32 setting than training. We report the test as uninformative [and the registered re-scoring on the training platform beside it]."

## Q4. What can be said today

**Can be said:**

> "On 300 FFHQ persons no network was trained on, the four protocol-plus-splice networks that passed the platform gate (seeds 403, 405, 406, 407) raised their own-rule flag rate on stored genuine photos by +1.0 to +3.8 points at the trained size (61.2 px) and by −0.3 to +3.4 points at an untrained size (112.7 px). Their mean genuine logit moved toward genuine (−0.02 to −0.24 genuine SD). Stored morphs did not become easier to miss (morph shift +0.06 to +0.11 SD at 61.2 px and −0.02 to +0.04 SD at 112.7 px; storage evasion falsified on 4 of 4 read seeds). Development data only. No plain network passed the gate, so these reactions are not compared with the plain recipe, and the registered storage contrast is uninformative."

Also state that Sb 406 is the largest: native flags 6.0%, upper 95% bounds 6.5 and 5.9 points.

**Cannot be said:**
- "reduced relative to the plain recipe" or "at most one third of it";
- "removed", "absent" or "shortcut-free";
- anything about B (one seed) or about A;
- anything about H-new-2;
- any generalisation beyond development data.

Small absolute reactions are not evidence of reduction, because the plain networks may not react at 112.7 px either.
