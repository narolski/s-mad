## Findings
1. **Major: seeds 408–412 collide with the protocol ablation (AB1).**
   - `src/dispatch/DISPATCHER_BRIEF.md:98` says AB1 "reuses SE1's A and Sb networks", while the prereg (lines 61, 153) said AB1 fits its own networks and prints duplicates.
   - AB1 has no experiment folder and no registration. Once SE1's reader computes C2P statistics for A and Sb at 408–412, any C2P replication on those seeds is no longer blind.
   - Fix: register AB1's replication rule before any SE1 tar is pulled, or state that no confirmatory replication will use 408–412. Correct lines 61 and 153.
2. **Major: G1 often fails even if SE changes nothing.**
   - Same-seed SE and Sb are effectively independent networks. Sb's landmark AUC varies by about 0.030 between seeds, so the per-seed difference varies by about 0.042.
   - With no true effect, P(AUC(SE) ≥ AUC(Sb) − 0.02) is about 0.68 per seed, and "met on ≥ 4 of 5" about 0.49. G1 then lands in "inconclusive" roughly half the time.
   - The reader treats anything other than "met" as a failure, and the table then prints "costs landmark-morph detection".
   - Fix: decide the AUC leg on the seed means (mean SE − mean Sb ≥ −0.02, with an interval). Write the "costs" sentence only when G1 is "not met", and add a row "G1 inconclusive: no claim".
3. **Major: G3's absolute 5% bar on FFHQ is at Sb's own level.**
   - Sb's own-rule flags on ffhq_bona_orig at seeds 403–407 are 2.35, 5.0, 4.45, 5.1 and 3.15%. So the absolute leg fails nearly regardless of SE.
   - Fix: keep the absolute 5% only for development R0 and AF0 photos (Sb 0%). Use only the relative +0.02 for FFHQ and the MS1 native photos.
4. **Major: the arm adds a storage cue that no read covers.**
   - All 830 new blended negatives are native. Stored blended images stay 100% attacks (af0_u5061, 366).

   - Fix (no GPU): add printed reads for SE against Sb on these four cells. Better, make "R3 at 61 and 112 at most 20%" part of the target wording.
   - Also disclose the dilution: the stored share of training rows falls from 14.7% to 12.3%, and draws per original row from 191 to 159. This confounds G2.

## Power (question 6)
- **10% bar:** at a true rate of 5% per cell, a seed meets both cells with probability about 0.98; at 8%, about 0.67, which makes "≥ 4 of 5" about 0.47. The bar is sharp between 7% and 12%. That is adequate, because the effects targeted are large (Sb flags 31–41% of S3 and 83–91% of R3).
