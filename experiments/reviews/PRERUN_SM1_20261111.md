**Findings**

1. **Blocker for any memorisation claim: Part 2 compares five specific renders with five others, not trained partners with untrained ones** (prereg.md:46-51, 91-95).
   - The key bootstrap holds the 10 partners fixed. With 500 keys, its interval is about ±0.05 logit.
   - The detectors' response to each partner (pose, size, texture) varies far more than that. Under no memorisation, the five trained partners come out "higher" about half the time, and the error is shared by all five detectors.
   - ArcFace matching only controls pair similarity.
   - The lead's sentence can stay. The fix belongs in the owner's own addition at line 108 ("shows memorisation if above 0"). Require at least one of these as well:
     - (a) **Difference-in-differences:** score the 5,520 audit images with the Part 3 heads, whole image. Arms (a) and (b) saw the five partners; (c), (c′) and (d) never saw any of the 10. The FE ViT-L/14 header on the same features minus the arm (c) gap is a near-perfect control.
     - (b) Exact sign-flip test over the 5 matched pairs (p ≥ 1/32).
     - (c) About 20 extra unused partners × 100 keys, giving a placebo distribution.
   - Arm (b) is also the positive control that shows whether the test has power.

2. **Major: SMDD-T's partner condition can almost never fire** (lines 110-116).
   - "The releases' gap has an interval including 0" will almost never hold under finding 1, so the partner claim cannot be dropped.
   - The mapping is unstated: every detector or pooled? Does a reversed gap count?
   - Fix: register "no primary detector meets the partner-level criterion", with the strict reading printed beside it. Also state that the 5-point rule and the halving rule use the 5-seed mean point estimate.

3. **Major: (b)→(c) bundles five changes, so a difference cannot be attributed to partners** (lines 56-63). The changes are:
   - partner balance;
   - shared identity pools (both contributors of each morph are genuine training images);
   - source pool (`os25k_bf_t` vs `os25k_m_t`);
   - pair similarity (look-alike rank vs random partners);
   - landmark source (stored file vs new pass).
   - Fix:
     - Make (c′) mandatory.
     - Add (b′): the release's first contributors, each given random partners from unused `os25k_m_t` renders (cap 2, Part 2 keys and unused partners excluded). This needs about 5k more landmarks (CPU minutes).
     - Add a pool-cue check: a 5-fold head on `bf_t` vs `m_t` renders.
   - Cost under 0.2 GPU-h. Needs a registration amendment.
