## Pre-run check: AB1 amendment 3 (H-hb1, landmark_combined read on the history-balanced subset), commit b241a52

**Verdict: GO WITH FIXES.** The read uses no GPU and only development data. The adversary's subset builder does not use network scores. The reader's sha256 matches its pin (`de6e61ae…`, in `pins.sha256`). Four problems need fixing before the read: the "supported" and "falsified" outcomes can both be true at once; the 4-of-5-seed vote does not protect against noise from the subset itself; there is no minimum subset size; and nothing checks that the subset actually converged or still contains blend signal. Each fix is a text change in the prereg plus a few reader lines, made before `lcomb_bal` exists.

### Findings (most severe first)

**1. Major: the two outcome regions overlap, and a non-positive full-set gain is not handled.**
- `prereg.md` amendment 3 and `ab1_hb_read.py:127` test "shrinks" (g_bal ≤ ½·g_full) and "stays" (g_bal ≥ 0.10) independently.
- Sb − B reaches g_full = 0.21, so half of it is 0.105. Any g_bal between 0.10 and 0.105 counts as both.
- `ab1_read.verdict` (`ab1_read.py:134-142`) checks "supported" first, so overlapping seeds fall silently to "supported".
- If g_full ≤ 0 on a seed, "shrinks by half" has no meaning.
- **Replacement text:** "Per seed: *shrinks* if g_full > 0 and g_bal ≤ ½·g_full; *stays* if g_bal ≥ +0.10 and g_bal > ½·g_full; a seed with g_full ≤ 0 counts as neither. Supported if ≥ 4 of 5 seeds shrink; falsified if ≥ 4 of 5 stay; otherwise inconclusive." In the reader, change line 127 to `shrinks_by_half=bool(gf > 0 and gb <= 0.5*gf), stays_ge_010=bool(gb >= 0.10 and gb > 0.5*gf)`.

**2. Major: the 5 seeds share one subset, so the vote does not average out subset noise.**
- All seeds are read on the same `lcomb_bal` rows. Noise from which rows were drawn is common to every seed, so "4 of 5" mainly reflects that one draw.
- My simulation (two-normal model, Sb at 0.89 and B at 0.73, scripts in `/tmp/claude-1000/-home-narolski-mambamorph/c2840dc9-dd37-4619-9347-d8a76080b93f/scratchpad/sim.py`) gives the SD of the paired gain:

| Rows per class | SD of paired gain |
|---|---|
| 50 | 0.04-0.05 |
| 100 | 0.027-0.037 |
| 150 | 0.023-0.030 |
| 300 | 0.016-0.022 |

- The gap between "half" (about 0.08) and the 0.10 bar is only 0.02-0.03.
- The review's quick matching kept 270 morphs. `balance.py` shrinks the caliper by ×0.7 for up to 12 rounds, so the final subset could be much smaller.
- **Add to the prereg:** "Not read if lcomb_bal has fewer than 150 morphs or 150 genuine. Also printed, not binding: the seed-mean g_bal with a joint row bootstrap (same draws for all seeds); and a null from 2,000 random class-balanced subsets of the same sizes (rng 20261117), giving the percentile of each seed's g_bal and of the seed-mean g_bal."

**3. Major: a "supported" result cannot be attributed to history unless the subset converged and still carries blend signal.**
- `balance.py:106-109` can stop after 12 rounds with `converged=false`. The reader never checks this.
- Matching genuine photos with smooth, morph-like surrounds may also select smoother faces. That would remove part of the legitimate blend cue (the review's H-new-1).
- **Add to the prereg:** "The reader asserts that `floors/lcomb_bal_build.json` is committed and pinned, with `converged=true` and n_morph = n_genuine; otherwise not read. It prints the adversary's face-region floor on lcomb_bal. If that floor is < 0.80, a 'supported' outcome is reported as 'gain lost on the subset, not attributable to history alone'."

**4. Major: the makeup of the retained rows is never printed.**
- **Add, printed for retained versus dropped rows within each class:** means and SDs of `pre_ied`, `pre_w`, `pre_png_bytes`, `border_frac`, `scale`, `aligned_png_bytes` (from `ffhqm_meta.csv`); the `nu_gbt` score (`lcomb_heldout.csv`); `face_logreg` and `surround_logreg` (`lcomb_regional_heldout.csv`).
- **Also print a decomposition** of each network's AUC: retained morphs against all genuine, and all morphs against retained genuine. This shows which side drives any shrinkage.
- **Also print, as a sensitivity check, the gain on a probit scale,** √2·Φ⁻¹(AUC). Moving both AUCs toward 0.5 shrinks the raw difference mechanically.

**5. Minor-major: the quintile print will be close to empty.**
- A surround score with AUC 0.79 puts very few morphs in the bottom quintiles. The review's item 6 found 1-10 morphs per quintile with the earlier score.
- `ab1_hb_read.py:122-125` still prints a difference however small the counts.
- **Fix:** `diff=None` when n_morph < 20 or n_genuine < 20 in a quintile.
