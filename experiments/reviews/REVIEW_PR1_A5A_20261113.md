**Verdict: ACCEPT WITH EDITS.** The reader follows the 5a rule on every point I checked. The verdict follows from the numbers. The wording needs four changes:
1. Say that AB1 is not an independent replication.
2. Put the detectable sizes in context. They rule out only large effects.
3. Label the printed side-reads that lean against the predicted direction.

- **Seed averaging.** Each null draw adds diff/5 (`:126`). The observed value is the mean of the per-seed differences (`:128`). Both are on the same scale.
- **Direction.** "Visible" fires only when the value is below the lower null bound (`:132`). A rise above the upper bound gives "inconclusive", as the rule says.
- **AUC condition.** It is two-sided (`:131,134`).
- **Detectable size.** Computed as 2.8 x the null SD (`:130`).
- **Pins.** The pins are checked before any load (`:114`). The pins file hashes to 4dea8c1f… and the reader to 89be37e1…, both matching prereg.md:503,505. The reader is byte-identical at 8f72a08 and 034c4d4. The FS1 pins equal `nets_pins.txt`, and the AB1 pins equal AB1's `.sha256` files.

## Findings, most severe first
1. **Major (over-claim risk): the AB1 agreement is not a replication.** AB1 reads the same 191 hosts, the same 32 linked hosts and the same training list; only the network seeds change. Most of the null's spread comes from which hosts are sampled, so agreement is close to automatic (AB1 null mean logit [-0.397, +0.487] vs FS1 [-0.420, +0.470]). Never write "replicated on AB1". Write "same reading from five further network seeds on the same hosts".
2. **Major (calibration): the read can only exclude large effects.**
   - The smallest detectable shift in mean logit is 0.64. That is about 43% of the clean hosts' median S4−S3 paired margin (1.473; `FS1.five_seed_mean_per_set.clean.margin`).
   - The other detectable sizes are 23.4 points of S3 flag rate, 18.9 points of S4 miss rate and 0.148 AUC (`FS1.smallest_detectable`).
   - The pre-run check expected any effect to be small (PRERUN finding 1). The read was underpowered for the plausible size.
   - Read as an approximate interval (assuming a pure location shift), the result rules out a drop of more than about 0.4 logit for linked hosts, but no smaller one.
   - "No effect detectable at n = 32" must always appear with these sizes and never be shortened to "no effect" or "the link does not matter".
3. **Minor (record): stale reader hash.** `src/PR1/SHA256SUMS:10` still pins c2e8fdd5… (the amendment-5 reader), but the read used 89be37e1…. Prereg.md:487 says the reader is pinned in SHA256SUMS. Add a dated correction line, and update SHA256SUMS if the lead approves; I edited nothing.
4. **Minor (printed side-reads lean against the prediction; do not interpret).** None of these decides the verdict.
   - The median S3 logit is higher for linked hosts (-0.787 vs -1.336). The per-seed difference is positive on 4 of 5 seeds in both FS1 and AB1.
   - The paired margin difference is -0.237.
   - R4 vs R3 differs by +0.047 (null [-0.090, +0.081]). It is positive on 4 of 5 FS1 seeds (s404 is -0.002) and 5 of 5 AB1 seeds.
   - The R3 flag rate is 0.906 vs 0.848.
   - R3 has no hull, so if this pattern is real it is host-level heterogeneity among the 32, not the shape link. Agreement across seeds is not evidence, because all seeds share the hosts.
5. **Minor: the n = 11 print has no null.** Mean logit +0.079, AUC 0.788 vs 0.719. Its direction is opposite to the prediction. Label it "printed, n = 11, no interval, do not cite".
6. **Minor: limits of two printed statistics.**
   - The AUC null is skewed ([-0.084, +0.129]), so the SD-based detectable size of 0.148 is approximate.
   - The S0 flag null is degenerate ([-0.001, 0.006]) because S0 sits at the floor, so it carries no information.

## What the paper and record may say
- **Allowed:** "persons new in appearance". For 32 of the 191, half of the landmark outline entered training through a partner's negative. The 159-host values sit beside the 191-host values and are identical to three decimals.
- **Not allowed:** "no shape leakage", "the shape link has no effect", "replicated on a second seed block", or any reading of the linked hosts as harder or easier.
- **Open author question.** Switching the headline to the 159 clean hosts costs nothing numerically (0.732 = 0.732), so it is a free hedge. Whether to switch is still the author's call.

## Next steps (zero GPU; each needs a registered amendment 5b first, labelled post hoc)
1. **Compare against arms that never saw S3/S0 negatives.** Sp (Sb minus lad_S0 and lad_S3), B and A did not train on matched splice negatives, so no shape link exists in them. Take each host's logit difference between Sb and Sp, then compare linked with clean. The host-level baseline cancels, so the null should be much narrower and the power much higher. The same comparison tests whether the R3 excursion is host heterogeneity. Which Sp score tars exist was not confirmed: listing the results directories was blocked. AB1 prereg.md:58,74 implies Sp exists at FS1 and at 408-412.
2. **Dose-response over all 191 hosts.** For each test host, measure how much of its evaluation-hull landmark shape appears in any training hull (a continuous score), then regress the host's mean logit on it, using a component bootstrap. Using all hosts instead of a 32/159 split should gain power. It needs the landmark files and CPU only.
