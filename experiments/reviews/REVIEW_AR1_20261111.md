# Review of the landmark-morph ladder, first round [AR1], 11 Nov 2026

## Verdict

**The conclusions need qualification.** The ladder shows something real. When benchmark construction cues are removed by design, all four released detectors still separate a two-person landmark morph from a warp-only photo with the same geometry (R2w4 vs R4 0.89 to 0.99), and the size of the warp alone does nothing (R2w vs R2w4 0.49 to 0.52). Five claims go further than the evidence:
- that this explains benchmark success;
- that the signal is "blending as such" rather than the loss of fine detail that averaging two images causes;
- that the outside-face signal is a feature of morphs in general, rather than of tools that blend the whole frame;
- that 0.60 to 0.75 measures identity;
- that the registered falsifier's "supports" branch was met.

Rules 2 and 3 forbid blur and resampling for agents, so the softness question can only be settled by an author item. Several confounds can be checked now with no GPU.

## How the verdict rules were applied

They were applied as registered, with exceptions in items 4, 6 and 7.
- The SelfMAD OpenCV main verdict is "mixed" (R0 -> R2w share 0.25 > 0.2). That is correct.
- ViT-L/14 OpenCV R3 vs R4 is 0.749 [0.71, 0.79], so "mixed" is correct.
- MixFaceNet FaceMorpher at 0.598 / upper bound 0.63 is "no identity reading", which is right at the edge of the rule.
- The R2w substitution (amendment 1) was registered before the scores were read.

## Findings, most severe first

**1. Major: the ladder cannot show that "benchmark success is not explained by benchmark cues".**
- The ladder removes the cues by construction (R0 -> R2 = 0, R1 = identity; result.md:9-11). It shows the detectors can detect blending without cues. It says nothing about how much the cues contribute on the published files.
- The benchmark side of the same experiment shows cue use. The step from raw photo to history-matched photo (E7 frames) is:
  - SelfMAD 0.949 / 0.920 (share of R4f 0.63 / 0.54);
  - ViT-B/16 0.753 / 0.725;
  - MixFaceNet 0.697 / 0.670;
  - ViT-L/14 0.38 (reversed).
- Raw file bytes and width separate published morphs from photos at 1.000 (result.md:93-100, 122).
- So for SelfMAD and ViT-B/16, benchmark-cue sensitivity is confirmed, not refuted.

**2. Major: "reads morphing" is not separated from softness or noise averaging.**
- No softened control and no crossed contrast exist; the owner made no softness measurement (README.md:250).
- The detectors exceed Laplacian variance (0.774) and band power (0.756) by 0.12 to 0.24. But these are single-number or global descriptors, not a ceiling on what softness can do.
- Blending one capture with its own mirror is already flagged at 0.92 to 0.98 (R2wm -> R3m).
- One interpolation pass moves SelfMAD to 0.71, and a band-power probe separates warped from unwarped photos at 0.858.
- SelfMAD is trained on self-blends, and MADation and MixFaceNet on SMDD (whole-frame OpenCV-style morphs). Detecting blend traces is in-distribution for all of them, so flagging twins (99.6% for SelfMAD) is expected.
- Fix the wording to "blend traces (consistent with detail loss from averaging two captures)". The decisive test, a warp-only photo softened to match R4's sharpness, is an author item.

**3. Major: the outside-face signal is mostly a construction artefact of tools that blend the whole frame.**
- R3 shows a ghosted background and clothing outline (README.md:246). Spliced morphs would not have this.
- Border check (my CPU read of 15 + 15 random host frames): every sampled FRGC frame has 0% exact-zero pixels; 11 of 15 colorFERET frames have zero padding, up to 25% of pixels. Blending two padded frames gives half-grey strips. The border-mask baseline (exact zeros, 0.521) cannot see them.
- Per database, R2w vs R4 with the face blanked (my plain AUCs, no intervals, descriptive):

| Detector | FRGC (OC / FM) | colorFERET (OC / FM) |
|---|---|---|
| SelfMAD | 0.90 / 0.93 | 0.87 / 0.91 |
| ViT-B/16 | 0.96 / 0.97 | 0.91 / 0.93 |
| ViT-L/14 | 0.89 / 0.90 | 0.83 / 0.84 |
| MixFaceNet | 0.73 / 0.77 | 0.87 / 0.90 |

- So the blanked-face result survives on FRGC, which has no borders. MixFaceNet's colorFERET excess may come from the padding.
- The two arms are also blanked with different hulls (mean of H and S vs mean of H and B; blank.py:22-27). No blanked R2w4, which would share R4's hull, was built.

**4. Major: the same-person vs two-person contrast is confounded with how different the two photos look.**
- Glintr100 cosine of the same-person pair: median 0.87 (FRGC) / 0.85 (colorFERET). Two-person pair: median 0.24 / 0.19, maximum 0.69 / 0.32 (pool.csv). The two-person partners are impostor-level pairs, not look-alikes.
- More different texture, colour and hair means more ghosting. R2w vs R2w4 rules out warp size only.
- Session cancels, because both arms mix two sessions (REVIEW_AS0 F5). Pose was not measured.
- So 0.60 to 0.75 is an upper bound on identity-specific reading, not the identity step itself.
- SelfMAD varies by database: R3 vs R4 is 0.735 / 0.701 on FRGC and 0.534 / 0.511 on colorFERET (n = 109). Its "mixed" verdict comes from FRGC.

**5. Moderate: the claim that the identity step beats baselines for two detectors does not survive multiplicity.**
- ViT-L/14 holds on both tools: +0.119 [0.06, 0.18] and +0.114 [0.05, 0.18].
- SelfMAD OpenCV is +0.059 [0.01, 0.11], one cell of 8, and its FaceMorpher cell is [-0.01, 0.10].
- Report "ViT-L/14 only; SelfMAD OpenCV marginal, not replicated with FaceMorpher".
- Compute the Bonferroni-at-8 intervals (zero GPU).

**6. Moderate: the falsifier's "supports" branch is not formally met.**
- It requires "reads morphing" on both pools. The FRLL verdicts are descriptive (smiling confound; clean ladder only).
- Say "supported on the primary pool; secondary pool descriptive".

**7. Moderate: the registered negative control is degenerate.**
- tool(S, H) is pixel-identical to tool(H, S) (40 of 40 checked). The guard is therefore missing; say so in every summary.
- The table labels R2w vs R3 as "identity reading" for SelfMAD OpenCV (result.md:53), but no second identity is involved. Rename it "blend reading", disclosed as a presentation change.

**8. Moderate: the failed platform check, G-a and the failed bridge limit transfer, not the ladder contrasts.**
- Every ladder arm is a PNG crop scored on the same DGX. The Kendall 0.97 shortfall is on A46 JPEG crops, where RS1 found that JPEG decoding differs. It cannot create a within-ladder contrast.
- What it does affect:
  - the benchmark thresholds and flag rates (SelfMAD flags 25 to 47% of genuine R0 photos at its FRLL threshold; this is a calibration shift, not an operating point);
  - the bridge.
- The bridge compares published raw-file crops with our 512 aligned-frame crops (analyse.py:200-202). It confounds frame pipeline and file format with the morph itself.
  - The detection criterion is saturated (0.96 to 1.00).
  - Our morphs score more morph-like in 6 of 8 cells.
- So transfer of the ladder's effect sizes to the published FRLL files is not shown.
- G-a held for 2 of 4 detectors, and only on FRLL raw files. REVIEW_FZ1 adds that our frames may favour landmark cells.

**9. Minor.**
- Pooled AUCs mix FRGC and colorFERET (pool offset, AS0 F2). Within a database the CLIP models reach 0.995 on R2w vs R4, so pooling understates the effect. Report stratified within-database AUCs.
- The strict folds are 88 cycle units; the largest holds 163 persons (30%). Valid, but uneven.
- M3 seed 8 was read before its G-c (disclosed).
- M3 "identity reading 16 of 16" sits at 0.76 to 0.78, has no baseline excess, and comes from SMDD-trained networks. M3L is pending.
- AR2's m = 0 is not R0, because mixing also moves geometry.

## Corrected wording

1. "On landmark morphs built without benchmark cues, released detectors separate a two-person morph from a warp-only photo with the same geometry (0.89 to 0.99). Warp size alone does nothing. This shows they detect blending traces, not that the cues play no role on the benchmark."
2. "Most of the separation appears when two captures are blended: a same-person morph is flagged almost as readily (0.81 to 0.96). Swapping in a second, dissimilar person adds 0.60 to 0.75, an upper bound, because the pairs also differ in appearance. That excess beats simple baselines clearly only for ViT-L/14. Whether the blend trace is more than detail loss is not yet tested."
3. "Part of the trace lies outside the inner-face hull (0.76 to 0.92 with the face blanked; it holds on FRGC, which has no padded borders). These tools ghost the second capture's hair, clothing and background, so this part is specific to tools that blend the whole frame."
4. "For regular morphs the audit finds both: blend-trace detection that works without benchmark cues, and, on the published FRLL files, sensitivity to photo history (SelfMAD strongly, ViT-B/16 and MixFaceNet moderately, not ViT-L/14). Neither a second identity nor face-only blending is shown to be what they read."
5. "All ladder numbers are in our pipeline (aligned 512 frames, one DGX). The within-ladder contrasts do not depend on platform. Absolute levels and transfer to the published files do: reproduction held for 2 of 4 detectors, and the bridge failed in 4 of 8 cells."

## New hypotheses

- **H-new-1, detail loss.** Statement: averaging two captures removes fine detail, and the detectors read that loss.
  - Prediction: among R2w and R4 images matched on Laplacian variance and band shares, R2w vs R4 falls below 0.75 for MixFaceNet; the CLIP models stay at or above 0.90 if they read more than detail loss.
  - Test: stratified AUC on existing scores and baseline features.
  - Falsified if all four stay at or above 0.85 within matched strata.
  - Cost: 0 GPU, now. The definitive version (blurred R2w) is an author item.
- **H-new-2, appearance dissimilarity.** Statement: the R3 -> R4 step scales with how different the pair looks.
  - Prediction: R4 score rises with 32-pixel thumbnail distance and with low cos_HB. R3 vs R4 restricted to the most similar decile of pairs is at most 0.60, except for ViT-L/14.
  - Falsified if the decile AUC is within 0.03 of the full AUC.
  - Cost: 0 GPU, now.
- **H-new-3, inner-face blend.** Statement: the detectors still flag a morph whose blend is confined to the face.
  - Arm: a spliced morph, made by pixel copy of R4 inside the hull onto R2w4 outside it. The geometry is identical, and there is no blur or resampling.
  - Prediction: R2w4 vs spliced R4 is at least 0.85 for the CLIP models and falls by 0.05 or more for MixFaceNet.
  - Falsified if all stay within 0.02 of unspliced, or all fall below 0.75.
  - Cost: needs an amendment; about 1 to 1.5 GPU-hours on the shared GB10; fits before 14 Nov.
- **H-new-4, padding strips.** Statement: MixFaceNet's colorFERET face-blanked excess comes from half-grey padding strips.
  - Prediction: within colorFERET, R4 score correlates with the area of padding that differs between H and B (Spearman > 0.2).
  - Cost: 0 GPU.
- **H-new-5, one resampling pass.** Statement: a single interpolation pass drives SelfMAD's R0 -> R2w 0.71 and its E7 0.95.
  - Prediction: R0 vs R2wm on the primary pool is about 0.7 or more for SelfMAD, while the CLIP models stay at or below 0.6.
  - Cost: 0 GPU (existing rows).

## What is missing for "definitively confirm or refute benchmark-cue use"

A full refutation is already ruled out: SelfMAD's E7 step (0.95) confirms cue sensitivity. A definitive answer needs four things:
1. A photo with benchmark cues and no blend: genuine photos through the published resize and format pipeline. This is an author item.
2. A softness-matched control.
3. A spliced morph, to separate face blending from whole-frame ghosting.
4. Look-alike pairs, to measure identity without the dissimilarity confound.

Then report the share of the benchmark AUC carried by cues against the share carried by blending, for each detector.
