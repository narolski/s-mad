# Review of the SMDD five-partner audit and rebuild (SM1): Parts 1 and 2, and the frozen-feature arms of Part 3

11 Nov 2026, about 21:00 CET. I only read files. I ran three small single-thread CPU checks and wrote nothing to disk:
- (i) the existing audit scores in `/mnt/t7/smad_next/SM1/p2scores/*.npz` and `heads/<arm>_s401.npz`, audit rows only;
- (ii) own-rule miss rates for each kind of partner;
- (iii) the sharpness statistic on 1,600 stored 80-px arm-(e) training files (`cv2.imread` and the Laplacian only, no resizing).

All three are post hoc reviewer numbers. They are not registered.

## 1. Verdict: needs qualification

- **Conclusion 1 is sound.** Verify the file names against the image content before writing to the SMDD authors.
- **Conclusion 2 is right in kind but wrong in size, and over-worded.**
  - The "matched unused" partners are not a neutral reference. They score about 1 logit lower than other untrained partners, even for heads that saw none of them. Against SMDD's own evaluation partners, the effect specific to the training partners is 0.4 to 1.0 logit for the two adapted MADation releases, which sit near their score ceiling.
  - The causal demonstration holds for frozen linear heads only, and for partner *images*, not identities.
- **Conclusion 3 rests on a test (T-A) that cannot speak to partners.**
- **Conclusions 4 and 5 leave out their main cost.** More partners and look-alike pairs make the heads react more to storage history. At the fixed camera-photo threshold, halves do not help. The "failure" that arm (e) removes is largely a product of our head recipe.
- **Conclusion 6 misreads the 0.72.** It is an artefact of pooling two storage sizes. Inside each storage size, sharpness still separates the classes at 0.90 to 0.94.

## 2. Findings, most severe first

**F1 (major): the matched unused partners (U) are a biased reference, so the gap sizes are overstated.**
- On heads that never saw any of the 15 partners, the evaluation-split partners (KE: never trained by anyone, our engine) beat U by +0.98 to +1.48 logits (seed 401: arms b′, c, d, e). The registered gap of the trained partners over U is +0.10 to +1.34 on those same heads.
- Released detectors, KE − U: ViT-B/16 1.29 [1.08, 1.52], ViT-L/14 2.56, FE ViT-B/16 0.56, FE ViT-L/14 1.42, MixFaceNet 2.80.
- Released detectors, trained − KE (the effect specific to training): ViT-B/16 **0.43 [0.34, 0.53]**, ViT-L/14 **0.95 [0.78, 1.12]**, FE ViT-B/16 3.87, FE ViT-L/14 3.76, MixFaceNet 13.36. These are key-bootstrap intervals, 4,000 draws.
- For the two adapted releases, U therefore inflates the gap 3 to 4 times.
- The causal head result survives the change of reference: (trained − KE) in arm a minus the same in arm b′ is 4.16 + 1.21 = 5.37, against the registered 5.39.
- The KE partners do not rank at the top of the 30 (placebo-style p 0.09 to 0.29). This supports the assumption that partners are interchangeable units, which the placebo test needs.
- U was matched only on cosine to the keys, inter-ocular distance and yaw. It was not matched on sharpness, colour, background, hair or gender.
- Fix: report the size against KE or the 20 placebo partners. Keep trained − U as the registered row.

**F2 (major): the sharpness acceptance statistic pools storage strata.**
- Arm (e) at stored size, inside the 80-px stratum: AUC **0.919** (800 vs 800; 0.936 against shrunk renders, 0.904 against shrunk halves; my check).
- Inside the 256-px stratum, arm (e) has arm (d)'s composition, which scores 0.936.
- A network sees the stratum trivially, so separability given the stratum is about 0.92 to 0.94. Arms (e) and (f) are no nearer acceptance than arm (d).
- The registered verdict ("not accepted") stands and gets stronger.
- Fix: a post hoc row giving the AUC within each stratum (at loader size too) and a two-variable rule (stratum plus sharpness). Never cite 0.72 as a reduction.

**F3 (major): conclusion 4 omits that partner diversity increases the storage reaction.** From b (five partners) to b′ (2,433 partners):
- the diffusion rise goes from +14.56 to +17.04 (c′ +17.31; look-alike c +21.42);
- MorDIFF history-matched genuine photos flagged at the head's own rule go from 0.21 to 0.65 (c: 0.95);
- corrected-MAD22 FaceMorpher history-matched photos go from 0.04 to 0.36 (c: 0.91) (`result.md:233-246, 272-279`).
- Fix: better native EER must always be printed together with higher false alarms on softened genuine photos.

**F4 (major): the size of the storage "failure" depends on our head recipe.**
- On the same features, the released FE ViT-L/14 header rises +3.33 on diffusion and +0.60 on landmark cells. Our recipe on the release files (arm a) rises +11.32 and +2.59.
- The SMDD-only heads of the frozen-features experiment (FZ1), trained with the release recipe (no standardisation, lr 0.01, flip, 40 epochs), did not move with storage history (0 to +1.5).
- So arm (e)'s +3.47 brings our recipe to where the released header already is.
- Fix: refit every arm with the FZ1 recipe (CPU, existing features) before any statement that "the release" has the failure.

**F5 (major): the halves and JPEG readings depend on which threshold is used.**
- Halves: the registered EER-threshold reading falls 0.072 → 0.026. At the fixed camera-photo threshold (5% of FRGC/colorFERET R0) it **rises**, 0.072 → 0.088 (d+ 0.092). The AUC of halves against camera photos falls, 0.673 → 0.572.
- The AR1 halves are made by the training engine, so they are in-distribution (pre-run review F5).
- At the camera-photo threshold, arms d, e and f flag 11.6 to 13.6% of *untouched* FRLL photos, against 2.6% for arm c. This cost is not in the EER.
- JPEG: the "20% of JPEG-50 photos flagged" is mostly this offset between photo pools. JPEG-50 minus untouched photos at that threshold:

| Arm | a | c | d | e | f |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rise from JPEG 50 | +0.196 | +0.121 | +0.071 | +0.033 | +0.068 |

  History crossing had already removed most of the JPEG reaction, so JPEG copies had little left to remove (204 photos, about ±3 points).

**F6 (major): T-A cannot measure partners, and its threshold is unstable for two of the releases.**
- With training EER 0.00 (both adapted releases), the tie rule of `sm1_stats.weer` places t at the lowest training-morph score, a single image.
- Training morphs are the very images the detectors were trained on.
- One evaluation partner, img005071, has miss rates of 14.8 to 31.8% for three releases (`result.md:89-92`).
- Cleaner reading: own EER on the evaluation split against the training split: 0.20 vs 0.00 (ViT-B/16), 0.02 vs 0.00 (ViT-L/14), 3.42 vs 0.51, 2.09 vs 0.62, 2.20 vs 0.60.

**F7 (moderate): wording of "memorisation" and "causal".**
- Each partner is one StyleGAN2 render, so a partner image (texture, hair, background) cannot be separated from its identity.
- The causal arms (a, b against b′, c, c′, d, e) are frozen linear heads. The release evidence is a ranking test.
- The adapted releases are near their score ceiling: maximum logits 11.75 and 12.38, with 26% and 41% of trained-partner morphs within 0.1 of the maximum (U: 5% and 9%). Their placebo ranks are decided by hundredths of a logit.
- The p floor is 1/C(30,5) = 7.0e-6. The five p values share the same 30 partners and 100 keys, so they are not independent.

**F8 (moderate): our frames and the alignment border.** The Part 3 cells are our aligned frames, where morph frames carry the border our aligner leaves (medians 0.031 to 0.26) and genuine frames carry none (EV1 `result.md:87-91`). SMDD training images have no border, so each arm's response to it is untrained and may differ by arm. The features also use the FE release's input convention (BGR, 0.5/0.5).
- Fix: a border-stratified or border-matched re-read of the steps. Label every EER as "our frames".

**F9 (moderate): partner count is confounded with which partners.**
- The arm with five partners uses SMDD's five. The arms with 50 or more use random pool renders.
- The step b → K = 50 (−2.82) could therefore be "these five are atypical".
- No arm with five random partners exists.

**F10 (moderate): test reuse and the size of the uncertainty.**
- Thirteen arms are read on the paper's benchmark cells.
- The best arm by EER (c) is the worst under history matching.
- Training-pair bootstrap SD of landmark-7 EER is 0.26 to 0.83 points (`p3_full_boot_seeds.csv`), 2 to 10 times the seed SD quoted (0.02 to 0.47). Steps of about 1 point (c′ → c −1.12; d → e +1.94) are marginal.
- Fix: label everything "first-test data, arms fixed in advance". No protocol choice from these cells (as in the protocol-network review, PN1 F9).

**F11 (minor).**
- Part 1 is a fact about the file names. Check the names against the image content on about 100 morphs per split (ArcFace to the named partner against the other four) before contacting the SMDD authors.
- Morphs per partner (2,173 to 3,444) suggest the filter kept different shares of each partner's morphs; descriptive only.
- The 0.006 miss on the FE header tolerance affects only the reference row.
- The login-node hash command stays logged.

**Checked and OK: class balance inside storage strata.**
- Arm (d) has one storage stratum (all 256-px PNG).
- Arm (e) is exactly balanced by construction (`compose.py:73-85`: half of each class shrunk; morph share 0.371 in both strata).
- Arm (f) gets JPEG by image id, independent of class: 0.367 to 0.379 at seed 401. Print the other four seeds.
- The only structural cue left is "unwarped render ⇒ genuine", which pushes camera photos toward genuine.

## 3. Conclusion by conclusion, and what a paper may say

1. **Follows.** "In the SMDD release as distributed, all 15,000 training morphs share five second contributors (evaluation split: five others); the paper reads naturally as five partners per key image." Print only after the content check and the question to the SMDD authors. Never call it a bug.
2. **Partly follows.** "Released SMDD-trained detectors carry a trace of the five training partner images: with unseen first contributors, a morph with a training partner scores above one with an SMDD evaluation partner. The margin is 0.4 to 1.0 logit for the adapted MADation releases, near their score ceiling, and 3.8 to 13.4 for the frozen-encoder releases and MixFaceNet-MAD. The five rank above 25 untrained partners. On frozen CLIP features, training on the five produces the trace and training without them does not."
   - Not acceptable: "identity", "shown causally in the releases", the gap against U as the size.
   - Consistent with the earlier objection: the trace is an additive bonus. Untrained-partner morphs are still caught (own-rule miss 1.4 to 18.4%, against 0.0 to 0.3% with trained partners; my check).
3. **Replace.** "Benchmarks contain no training partner, so the trace has no direct effect on them. It matters where the five reappear (any validation split drawn from SMDD's training split) and possibly in what is learned (item 4)." Drop "4 to 9.5 points" as partner evidence.
4. **Descriptive, with its cost.** "On frozen CLIP ViT-L/14 features (FE convention, our linear recipe, our frames), 50 or more partners instead of SMDD's five lower native landmark EER from 7.6 to 4.2–4.8% and diffusion EER from 20 to 9–10%, but raise flags on history-matched genuine photos (MorDIFF 21 → 65% at the own rule). Whether the number or the identity of the five matters is untested."
5. **Rewrite as in F5.** "Halves lower the diffusion rise (+21.4 → +7.2) and lower flagged halves at the head's EER threshold (7.2 → 2.6%), but not at the camera-photo threshold (7.2 → 8.8%). History crossing brings the rise to +3.5, the released FE header's level, at +6.1 landmark points against arm (c) and +2.0 against the release, and with 12–14% of untouched FRLL photos flagged at the camera threshold. JPEG 90/70 copies did not lower a JPEG-50 reaction that history crossing had already cut to +3 points."
6. **Rewrite.** "One number, the variance of the Laplacian of the grey image, separates SMDD's training morphs from its renders at 0.993 (2,000 v 2,000, 256 px; 0.988 at the loader's 224 px). Part of this is blending itself (a morph against its own warped halves: 0.88). Inside each storage size, the rebuilt arms stay at 0.90–0.94, so none is close to the registered 0.65."

## 4. New hypotheses

- **H-new-1: the partner shortcut displaces general cues.**
  - Statement: with five partners, a head fits partner texture instead of general blending evidence.
  - Prediction: five *random* pool partners give landmark-7 EER of at least 6.5%, near SMDD's five (7.6%), not near K = 50 (4.8%).
  - Falsified if: the five-random arm scores 5.5% or lower, meaning SMDD's five are atypical.
  - Cost: about 15k engine calls on CPU and about 0.05 GPU-h of features. Fits by 13 Nov.
- **H-new-2: diversity swaps the partner shortcut for a softness shortcut.**
  - Prediction: within each class of training images, the correlation of the head's logit with Laplacian variance becomes more negative from b → K = 50 → b′.
  - Falsified if: |Δρ| < 0.05 from b to b′.
  - Cost: 0 GPU (CPU, existing features).
- **H-new-3: the alignment border carries part of the landmark gains.**
  - Prediction: with the border held equal (EV1 border fractions; matched pairs where no border-free stratum exists), the step b → b′ shrinks by more than half.
  - Falsified if: the step stays within 1 point.
  - Cost: 0 GPU.
- **H-new-4: the storage failure depends on the head recipe.**
  - Prediction: refitted with the FZ1 recipe, the arm (a) diffusion rise is ≤ +5 and the a → e reduction is < 3 points.
  - Falsified if: the rise stays ≥ +8.
  - Cost: CPU, under 1 h.
- **H-new-5: memorisation shows on the standard recipe.**
  - Prediction: LoRA arm (a) fits give trained − KE ≥ 1 logit, and the DiD against LoRA arm (c) has an interval above 0.
  - Falsified if: the interval includes 0 for ViT-L/14.
  - Cost: about 2 A100-h of scoring-only jobs on the saved trainable parameters. Fits by 13 Nov.
- **H-new-6: SMDD evaluation numbers carry hidden variance between partners.**
  - Prediction: EER per evaluation partner spans more than 5 points, and a two-way (contributor × partner) interval is at least 3 times the interval over first contributors.
  - Falsified if: the per-partner spread is under 2 points.
  - Cost: 0 GPU (`tab_sub` scores).

## 6. Risks to the story

- **The leading recipe's trace is small.** For adapted MADation the partner trace is 0.4 to 1.0 logit at the ceiling. Do not build the audit claim on it. Lead with the frozen-encoder releases, MixFaceNet-MAD, partner-leaky validation and partner variance.
- **The LoRA set can mislead.** ViT-L/14 LoRA arm (a) may not show the storage failure (A11 validity condition; `CONSOLIDATED_v3.md:44-50`). The ViT-B/16 hedge has only 2 seeds. Arm c is the worst-behaved frozen base, so d/e/f against c will look large: also compare them with arm a.
- **The protocol costs may cancel the gain.** +2 to +6 landmark points, and 12 to 14% of untouched FRLL photos flagged at the camera threshold, can undo "lowest miss at a fixed threshold". Always print flag and miss together.
- **Reviewers will see through the pooled acceptance statistic.** Redefine it now.

## 7. Edits for NOTEBOOK / CONCLUSIONS

- **What we have learned:**
  - "SMDD's training morphs use five partner images in total. Released detectors score morphs with those five higher than morphs with other partners: a little for the adapted MADation releases, strongly for the frozen-encoder releases and MixFaceNet-MAD. On frozen CLIP features, training on the five causes this."
  - "On frozen features, more partners improve native detection but increase false alarms on history-matched genuine photos. Halves and history copies reduce that reaction at a cost in landmark error."
- **How sure we are:** "Partner trace: strong for three releases, small for two. Measured against SMDD's evaluation partners, not the matched set, which is biased. Part 3: frozen heads only, our recipe and our frames, descriptive; sharpness still separates the classes at 0.90 to 0.94 inside every storage size."
- **Future directions:** "LoRA fits scored on the audit set; heads refitted with the release recipe; border-matched re-read; a five-random-partner arm; ViT-B/16 fits of the partner-only arms."
