## Findings, amendment 7

**F1 (major): BN − B mixes the content of the negatives with a change in how often positives are repeated, and the change favours H-c2.**
- I compared the manifests. B's negatives are unchanged in BN (0 ids differ). But the class balance re-repeats the positives: 312 positive ids change multiplicity.
- Primary-contrast positives: lad_R4 has 499 rows in BN against 443 in B (+12.6%). af0_u50 has 3,077 against 2,821. R4 is the positive class of the primary contrast, so the extra R4 exposure could produce "present" on its own.
- Fix (cheapest clean control): add an arm **BV** = B + 312 repeated native negatives, matched by database (260 FRGC, 52 colorFERET, as the dropped S0/S3 rows), built by the SpV rule.
  - I simulated it in scratch with rng 20261172 and the unchanged `balanced_strata`: 8,386 rows, and the positive multiplicity is identical to BN's (Counter equality True).
  - BN − BV then isolates the negatives' content with positives byte-identical. It mirrors Sb − SpV and completes a 2×2 at 8,386 rows (S4 positives yes/no × S0/S3 content vs repeats). The interaction (Sb − SpV) − (BN − BV) becomes printable.
  - Cost: 5 fits, about 3-3.5 H100-hours. Needs amendment 7b by the lead before submission.
- Replacement text for H-c2: "**H-c2 (negatives without positives, BN − BV):** BN − BV on R4 vs R3, where BV = B + 312 database-matched repeated native negatives (rng 20261172), so that BN and BV have identical positive rows. BN − B is printed with the note that BN repeats positives more (lad_R4 499 vs 443 rows)."
- Fallback without BV: keep BN − B, add that note, and change the "present" licence to "the matched negatives, together with the resulting rise in positive repetition (lad_R4 +12.6%), carry the gain".
