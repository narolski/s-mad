## NE1 post-result review (result commit 41df045, development rows only)

**Verdict: needs qualification.** Every number in result.md matches `ne1_read.json`, I found no code error, and the three registered readings follow the table as written (border inconclusive, splices inconclusive, storage floor). Three descriptive sentences are wrong, though:

## Findings (most severe first)

**1. Major: removal above 1 is an over-correction, not "more than full removal" (your Q1).**
- In logits, the half-A eraser cancels the mean paired shift almost exactly. Host-mean Δlogit for H0 vs H0e is +0.487/+0.360/+0.308/+0.313/+0.563 against +0.015/+0.035/−0.014/+0.027/+0.014, so logit removal is 0.97/0.90/1.04/0.91/0.98. H3 vs H1 is 0.96/0.88/1.06/0.90/0.95.
- The per-copy residual is skewed. Its median is −0.11 to −0.19, and 82–90% of copies now score below their own photo (before the eraser, 96–97% scored above). Mean copy rank drops from 0.86–0.90 to 0.10–0.20, against 0.53–0.58 for the photos.
- Per mask, the eraser over-corrects five masks (agedb, casia, lfw, oulu, replay: logits −0.18 to +0.07 after) and under-corrects CFP-FF (+0.20 to +0.44) and MSU-MFSD (+0.14 to +0.19).
- So the cause is the mean-difference eraser combined with a sign-like rank metric applied to a response that varies by mask. The rank metric is not the artefact; it correctly shows that a border dependence of the same size remains, with the opposite sign: |H3| is 0.25–0.37 against |H1| 0.31–0.44.
- "Removal ≥ 70%" was meant to mean "the shift moves toward zero". A one-sided rule cannot tell 1.9 apart from 0.9. Under a two-sided reading (|1 − removal| ≤ 0.3), "separable" holds on 0 of 5 seeds.
- The lead's draft (`IDEAS_mechanism-ledger/TEST_nuisance-erasure.md:61`) defined removal on **flags**. Excess p99 flags over photos, H1 against H3, are 0.123→0.081, 0.075→0.055, 0.048→0.038, 0.048→0.033, 0.166→0.094. That is a 21–43% removal, which would read "not removable (<50%)" on 5 of 5 seeds.
- The registered reading stands. The "3 of 5 separable" count is a literal count and should be labelled as one.

**2. Major: "no detection cost" holds for AUC only.**
- At each head's own 99th-percentile camera-pool cut, landmark_combined recall for H3 vs H1 is 0.840→0.755, 0.876→0.841, 0.834→0.800, 0.900→0.903, 0.948→0.868. That is a loss of 0.03–0.09 on 4 of 5 seeds.
- The border direction is not orthogonal to the head: cos(d3, w) = w·d3/|w| = 0.29–0.52. It is also not orthogonal to T's first principal component: |cos(PC1, d3)| = 0.32–0.55.

**3. Major: the storage floor is a reference-pool ceiling (your Q4).**
- Under H0, half-B FFHQ natives sit at mean rank 0.988–0.990 in the camera pool P, and the stored copies at 0.983–0.989. The largest shift the unit can show is about 0.012, which is below the 0.02 floor. The row could never be tested.
- Sb does react to storage, in a way consistent with MS1 and TT1:
  - Mean logit falls by 0.05–0.22. MS1 reported "Sb −0.20/−0.13 SD" (MS1 result.md:67).
  - Own-rule flags (logit 0) rise from 0.7–4.0% on natives to 2.1–6.9% at fm61 and 1.4–7.0% at amsl112.
  - This matches FS1 contrast-2 (paired storage share 0.155).
- The probe (0.82–0.88) agrees that storage is decodable from the features.
- "No paired storage shift" is wrong as a statement about the network. It is a statement about a saturated unit.

**4. Moderate: the null is an "erase the detection axis" null (your Q2).**
- PC1 is the detector: AUC 0.998–1.000 on T, and |cos(PC1, w)| = 0.98.
- Draws from N(0, Cov(T)) have median |cos| with PC1 of 0.93–0.95. Their removal correlates with that alignment (r = 0.30–0.54), and their landmark AUC falls to 0.72–0.90.
- A post hoc isotropic null (20 uniform directions, rng 7) gives a maximum rank removal of 0.00–0.10. H3 exceeds it by more than 1.4 on 5 of 5 seeds.
- The caveat is fair, but it explains too little: the null is not "near a top component", it erases the detector.
- The registered reading "inconclusive (border)" is correctly applied, because the rows are checked in order and none reaches 4 seeds.

**5. Moderate: H2d is degenerate as group-robust training; the splice wording should lead with the threshold-free read (your Q3).**
- Worst-group accuracy is 0.00–0.05 at every C on every seed, so the C choice is essentially arbitrary. The chosen C were 10/10/1/0.01/10.
- H2d is a valid head, but it is not a group-DRO-like solution.
- More informative: H2d − H1dc is within 0.021 on 5 of 5 seeds, so the group weights add nothing beyond refitting on L. All bootstrap intervals for H2d − H0 include 0, for example [−0.037, +0.120] and [−0.183, 0.000].
- The S4-vs-S3 AUC is 0.704–0.752 for every head and within 0.011 of H0 on every seed.

**6. Minor.**
- The border-probe AUC after the eraser (0.53–0.59) is pooled over masks. It hides the CFP-FF and MSU residual, which drives the remaining flags.
- result.md line 41: seed 406 is −0.006 in the JSON rounding (written as −0.005).

## New hypotheses (all CPU-only on saved features; register as a follow-up NE1b before computing)

- **H-new-1: the border response has rank greater than 1, roughly one direction per mask.**
  - Why: per-mask residuals of opposite sign after the rank-1 eraser.
  - Prediction: a rank-7 per-mask mean-difference eraser, or LEACE on 8-class mask identity, fitted on half A brings |shift| to ≤ 0.3 × |H1| and p99 excess flags to ≤ 0.3 × H1, with AUC within 0.01, on ≥ 4 of 5 seeds.
  - Falsified if |residual| > 0.5 × on ≥ 4 seeds.
  - Cost: 0 GPU, under 1 CPU-h. Fits before Nov 8? No, it is post-freeze anyway; fits before Nov 16.
- **H-new-2: border and detection share an axis.**
  - Prediction: erasing the component of d3 orthogonal to w (or to PC1) removes less than 50% of the excess flags. Erasing d3 itself costs ≥ 0.03 recall at the camera p99 cut on in-domain L test-host ladder R4 against R0 on ≥ 4 seeds.
  - Falsified if the orthogonalised eraser removes ≥ 70% at no recall cost.
  - Tells us whether gradient reversal can work without hurting detection.
- **H-new-3: the S3/S4 limit is in the features, not the head.**
  - Prediction: a host-grouped linear probe for S4 vs S3 on L development hosts, read on test hosts, reaches AUC ≤ H0 + 0.03 (about 0.77).
  - Falsified if the probe reaches ≥ 0.85. Then the head is the bottleneck, and a targeted refit (not group DRO) is worth registering.
- **H-new-4: Sb's storage reaction is a change in the tail and spread of scores, not a mean shift.**
  - Prediction: with the development FFHQ resmatch genuine set (C, 2,000) as the reference pool, the paired upper-tail share (copies above their native's own-rule margin) rises on ≥ 4 seeds, while the mean shift stays ≤ 0. H4 removes ≥ 50% of the extra own-rule flags.
  - Falsified if the extra flags do not exceed native-against-native noise.
