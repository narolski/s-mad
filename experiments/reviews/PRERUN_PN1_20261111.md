## 1. Verdict: GO WITH FIXES

I found no breach of the binding rules. But as built, the two manifests teach the network the very shortcuts the experiment is meant to test, and the pass marks cannot separate "reads blending" from "learned the one trained size". These fixes must happen before the pack is built and any LoRA fit is submitted:
- **F1–F2:** balance the classes within each stratum of database and storage history (no GPU).
- **F3:** add marks on the storage sizes B never saw.
- **F5:** add marks for manifest C, or declare its JPEG rows descriptive.
- **F6:** make the miss rate at a fixed threshold a registered mark, and write down what is reported when the result is neither pass nor kill.
- **F10:** pin the pack hash and include C.

## 2. Findings (most severe first)

**F1 (major). Manifest B makes "stored at 61.2 px" a cue for "morph".** In the B rows, 1,166 of 1,783 stored-copy rows are attacks (P(attack | 61.2 px) = 0.654), against 2,715 of 5,979 native rows (0.454); odds ratio about 2.3. The cause is the class-balance rule (prereg.md:99-102): attacks are repeated 3.19 times. Also, only 363 of 1,456 halves have a 61.2 px copy, against 366 of 728 morphs (manifests_summary.json). The arm meant to remove the storage reaction is therefore trained toward it. A failure of P-c ("does not react to storage") could not be read, and the certificate will flag it. Tier-0 arm B0 has the same imbalance: 33% of attacks are stored copies against 19% of negatives.
- Fix: apply the repetition rule within each stratum of database × history (native, 61.2 px, and for C jpeg90 / jpeg70), so that each stratum is 50/50. No new image is needed.

**F2 (major). Manifest A has a database shortcut that B mostly lacks.** P(attack | FRGC) = 0.688 against P(attack | colorFERET) = 0.359 (odds ratio about 3.9). The reason is the probe set of the alpha-field experiment (AF0): it has 40 FRGC persons with 50 photos but 400 morphs (AF0 pairs_summary.json). In B the same shares are 0.536 against 0.458.
- The test ladder is 77% FRGC (154 of 200 hosts), so A's flag rates at its own 0.5 rule mix storage with database. Depending on how softness and source interact, this can create A's "violation" or hide it.
- The paired shift R0 → R0_61 is immune. The flag-rate parts of the comparison (prereg.md:174-178) are not.
- This is also a source-identity shortcut in the reference arm. Fix: the same stratified rule in A, B and C.

**F3 (major). The storage marks read only the size B was trained on, and the development threshold protects that size by construction.**
- P-c (prereg.md:165-166) reads R0_61. That is in-distribution for B, so passing it cannot tell "softness is not blending" from "61.2 px learned by heart" (your point 3).
- τ_dev (prereg.md:111-117) is set so that the development R0_61 and R0_112 pools have at most 5% flagged. Test R0_61 at τ_dev will therefore sit near 5% for A and for B alike: the "tighter" second threshold adds nothing.
- Fix: read P-c at τ_0.5 and at τ_dev,native (camera photos only), not at τ_dev. Add a mark on sizes and kernels outside training: shift R0 → R0_112 at most 0.3 SD and at most 10% flagged at τ_0.5 and τ_dev,native. Apply the same flag mark to FRLL blur1, resample50 and to the E7 pools at strengths other than 61.2 px.
- Pre-register the reading. If B is flat at 61.2 px but shifts at the milder 112.7 px, the size was memorised. If A also does not shift at 112.7 px, that read is uninformative.
- Only two approved strengths exist. A stronger one (true leave-one-out) needs the author's yes.

**F4 (major). Your point 1, data size.** The counts:

| | Unique images | Draws per row | Draws per attack image |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 1,526 | 939 | about 939 |
| B | 5,099 | 206 | about 657 |

Since B = A plus extra negatives, the protocol and the data volume cannot be separated without a control.
- For the storage reaction the factor is mostly composition: A never sees a soft negative. Volume biases the signal marks instead (P-a, P-d, P-e): if A overfits, B gains on P-e.
- Fewer steps for A: not recommended. With a constant learning rate of 3.33e-6 it confounds optimisation length.
- Moving the decisive fits to the SMDD rebuild (SM1): not a replacement. Its fits are ViT-L/14 (SM1 prereg.md:310-325), where no storage failure is measured (nuisance_vetting.md:51-52). Its history copy is the 80-px one (sota_vetting.md:130-132). The storage half would likely end "protocol not shown to be needed".
- Smallest decisive change: an equal-size arm Bn. Draw from B, seeded and in kind proportions, A's unique counts (674 / 852), giving 1,704 rows and 939 draws per row; seeds 401 and 402, about 2-3 GPU-hours. If Bn ≈ B, it is the protocol; if Bn ≈ A, it is volume.
- Add, for free: training-row against held-out same-kind logit gaps per kind, from the saved scores_train.npz.

**F5 (major). Manifest C has no decision rule (prereg.md:272-275), and the JPEG failure is not shown for ViT-B/16.** Existing SMDD-trained ViT-B/16 networks flag at most 8% of JPEG 50 (nuisance_vetting.md:53); JPEG 50 is a ViT-L/14 failure (line 52).
- Fix: an "A violation" condition for JPEG (more than 10% of FRLL jpeg50 flagged at τ_0.5 or τ_dev,native on both A seeds). A C pass: at most 10% at both thresholds, with the miss rate not worse than B by more than 3 points.
- Pre-register the reading: C low on jpeg70 but high on jpeg50 = qualities memorised.
- State the pipeline difference: training copies are compressed on the 512 frame; the evaluation copies are compressed on the raw photo, then aligned.
- The decisive JPEG test belongs to arm (f) against arm (e) of the SMDD rebuild (SM1), with the ViT-L/14 recipe at full scale (SM1 A10). Label C a small-data check.

**F6 (major). Your point 6: the headline is unmarked and outcomes in the middle are unassigned.**
- The family-mean miss at τ_dev, A against B, is only "printed" (prereg.md:177-178), although the claim is the lowest miss at a fixed threshold. Register it with a paired host bootstrap: B ≤ A + 3 points (non-inferiority); "B lowers the miss" only if the upper bound is below 0.
- Fixed sentence for P-b between 0.75 and 0.90, for one seed passing and one failing, and for P-f unread: "inconclusive, no claim". Drop the label "passes P-a to P-e" (line 171).
- P-e uses point estimates (EER interval about ±2 points with 204 photos); say so.

**F7 (major, certificate and matched-subsample test of section 9).** Folds by connected components cannot work. The AF0 training pairs form one 485-person component and one 40-person component (525 persons), so one fold holds about 70% of the rows (cert.py:51-64, 97-106). Out-of-fold numbers and the bootstrap over components are then degenerate.
- Fix: assign persons to folds at random and drop from each test fold any image whose other person is in a training fold; print the share dropped.
- Name the nuisance-only number (history, engine, pool, border, person frequency) as "the" certificate. With softness cues included it will be high for every manifest by design.

**F8 (minor to major). The floor for the excess (P-a) is taken on the 512 frame** (cues27.py:1-2). The network sees 224 px, where the softness floor is higher (0.87 against 0.76; diffusion_check.md:411-412). Fix: take the maximum also over the 224-px cues that cert_cues.py already computes (c224). No GPU.

**F9 (major, labelling). Go/no-go is read on the test ladder and on benchmark cells.** The card requires decisions on the development ladder (sota_vetting.md:266-268). Within PN1 the arms are fixed in advance, so this is a single test, not selection. Register:
- the choice between B and C, and any later configuration choice, uses development-ladder and AF0-validation reads only;
- PN1's test-ladder, MAD22 and Poisson-set numbers are "first-test data, read once".

The ladder hosts were also read by the landmark ladder (AR1) and AF0 while PN1 was being designed; state that.

The test is uninformative if:
- A shows no violation (possibly masked by F2);
- B flags nothing because all its scores fall (an offset): F6 catches this;
- B passes only at 61.2 px (F3).
