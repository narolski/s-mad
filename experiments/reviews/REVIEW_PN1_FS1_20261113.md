## Verdict

- **The registered reading is correct and was applied as written:**
  - Contrast 1 replicated, 5 of 5 seeds, floor not exceeded.
  - Contrast 2 "uninformative on these seeds", because A's positive control failed.
  - Contrast 3 replicated.
  - Printed sentence "not separated from simple statistics".
  - HN9 "consistent with seed-driven".
- **The freeze rule, applied mechanically, freezes no method.** That is the right reading.
- **The rule is defective, for two reasons:**
  - **The control had almost no power.** Its 0.65 mark was set from two plain seeds that turned out to be the two highest of seven.
  - **The instrument is the wrong shape.** The share of persons whose logit rises cannot see the plain recipe's actual storage reaction, which sits in the upper tail. It also rewards a reversed reaction, which is what B and Sb show.
- **What the data show, descriptively:**
  - The protocol cuts the storage reaction at the networks' own decision rule by about an order of magnitude. It does not remove it.
  - Sb's face-splice gain is real across seeds, but it is level with simple statistics, not beyond them.
- **No result.md sentence may say "removes the storage reaction", "reads the face splice" or "beyond simple statistics".**

## Findings, most severe first

**F1 (critical): the freeze outcome is correct, and the rule's defect is the instrument, not only the threshold.**
- **Mechanical reading:**

  - B's contrast 2 is not "replicated", so step 2 fails.
  - Step 3 applies: no method is frozen (`FINAL_sealed-benchmark-read/prereg.md:32-36`).

- **Power of the control:**
  - Plain-arm (A) shares over seven seeds: 0.778, 0.721 (seeds 401/402), then 0.541, 0.617, 0.626, 0.528, 0.571 (fresh seeds).
  - Mean 0.626, SD about 0.09. On that estimate, P(A > 0.65 on at least 4 of 5 seeds) is about 0.09.

- **A's storage reaction is fully present on every fresh seed; the share statistic simply cannot see it:**
  - Median paired shift is only +0.01 to +0.09 logits, but the 90th percentile is +4.9 to +5.9.
  - Own-rule flags on FFHQ genuine photos go from 6.4–10.7% to 21.1–30.7%: 294 to 436 discordant persons, all in one direction, McNemar p < 1e-88.

- **The statistic is one-sided and counts signs.** Sb at 0.08–0.18 "passes" because of a reversed bulk reaction, not an absent one. The A14 review had flagged this risk (`REVIEW_PN1_A14_DEVREAD:172`).

## Freeze options

**(a) Accept "no method frozen; audit-only final read".**
- What the paper can claim: the audit findings, plus a development-only ablation table. No benchmark number for our method.
- Hostile reviewer: "An analysis paper with no working method; the protocol is shown only on a self-built development set."
- Clean, but it abandons the goal of a state-of-the-art comparison.

**(b) A new confirmatory test on data no fresh network has scored (recommended).**
- **Data:**
  - The MS1 FFHQ hold-out: 300 new persons with native, fm61 and amsl112 copies (112 px is a storage size no network was trained on), plus 150 u50 morphs and their stored copies. These rows are not in the 19,797 rows the FS1 networks scored. The prereg says no PN1 network has scored this hold-out; confirm before registering.
  - The TT1 FS1 block, already drafted.
- **Storage mark, relative to A within each seed:**
  - X passes a seed if its own-rule flag increase (stored minus native, paired) is at most one third of A's.
  - |mean shift in SD| of X is at most one third of A's.
  - Control: A's increase is at least +5 points.
  - X passes overall on at least 4 of 5 seeds, at both storage sizes.
- **Procedure:** the author amends FINAL §1 and commits it before any score, disclosing that the margins were set knowing the FS1 effect sizes. Cost: under 1 GPU-hour.
- What the paper can claim: "storage reaction reduced to ≤ 1/3 of the plain recipe's on unseen persons and an unseen storage size, 5 seeds". Never "removed".
- Hostile reviewer: "You replaced a failed gate." Answer: the gate failed on its control, not on the method; the replacement is registered before new data; both results are reported.

**(c) An author-ruled freeze of Sb without a test**, labelled "freeze rule not met; exploratory".

## Plain summary for the author

The spliced recipe is a real and stable improvement on our development data: about +0.19 AUC over protocol-only and +0.13 over plain on every fresh seed. But it is only as good as the best simple statistics on that set, not better.

The storage test could not decide, because the plain networks reacted to storage differently from what the threshold assumed. Looked at the right way, the protocol cuts the storage reaction about tenfold at the decision rule, but does not remove it.

By the written rule, no method is frozen. The cheapest legitimate way forward is a newly registered storage test, relative to the plain arm, on 300 unread FFHQ persons. It needs about one GPU-hour and your amendment of the freeze rule before any scoring. Fixing the FINAL threshold pool is needed in every case.
