### 1. Verdict: needs qualification

Both registered readings follow correctly from the outcome tables, but both sit on a knife-edge:
- Part A: seed 405 fails the plateau rule by 0.00004 (100-host minus 50-host AUC is 0.02004 against a 0.02 limit). Seed 404 passes the CI rule by 0.0001 (upper bound 0.04988 against 0.05).
- Part B: E7c fails on seeds 405 and 406 with flag removal of 0.662 and 0.667 against a 0.70 bar. Those are the two seeds with the smallest excess flags for H1 (0.048).

The cost interpretation has to be rewritten. I re-ran the head-matched nulls (random rank-7 subspaces that remove the same share of ŵ as E7) and read their FFHQ cost. They lose only −0.04 to +0.054 recall, so the "E7 removes most of ŵ, so the refit cannot rebuild detection" story is refuted. The cost is specific to the border span. Since landmark_combined has a surround shortcut (floor 0.942), the shared feature may be that shortcut rather than morph evidence. In Part A, "more same-person against two-person information" over-claims: on the same 291 hosts, simple morph cues alone reach 0.773 [0.742, 0.805].

### 2. Findings (most severe first)

**F1. Major: the cost inference is mis-attributed (your Q2).**
- **What the nulls show.** For every head-matched null, the FFHQ recall loss after refit is −0.040 to +0.054 (medians −0.013 to +0.021). E7 loses 0.269 / 0.060 / 0.188 / −0.011 / 0.141. E7 exceeds the largest of the 20 nulls on 4 of 5 seeds (all but 406).
  - So the projection of ŵ onto the span (0.68-0.90) does not explain the cost; the specific border span does.
  - That makes "border and the FFHQ cost share a subspace" stronger than you wrote, and the mechanism you gave wrong.
- **Why that span may be a shortcut.** FLOORS.md:24 and :35 put shortcut cues alone at 0.964-0.982 on landmark_combined, and the surround alone at 0.942 (FLOORS.md:69). A frame/surround subspace is exactly what a border eraser would remove.
- **Why "saturated" understates the in-domain check.** It was foreseeable: the FLOORS.md stage-3 `ri_R4_vs_R0` floor is 1.000 [1.000, 1.000] on these same hosts. The pre-run check (finding 4) proposed this contrast too, so the flaw is shared.
- **Replacement for result_ne1b.md lines 60-65 and 72-77:**

  "The registered in-domain cost check was saturated, and this was foreseeable: shortcut cues alone separate R4 from R0 at 1.000 (FLOORS stage 3). It cannot detect a cost. Printed only, cross-domain: on FFHQ landmark_combined, refitting after E7 loses 0.269 / 0.060 / 0.188 / −0.011 / 0.141 recall at the p99 camera cut. The AUC loss is 0.075 / 0.001 / 0.081 / 0.021 / 0.021. A post-hoc check (not registered) finds that 20 head-matched random rank-7 subspaces, which remove the same share of ŵ, lose at most 0.054. So the loss is specific to the border span, not to removing the head direction. landmark_combined differs from its genuine photos in surround history (surround-only floor 0.942), so this span may carry a surround shortcut as well as morph evidence. NE1b does not decide which. LEACE costs little (−0.05 to +0.03)."

  Under "Does not show", replace "points to border and detection sharing features" with "shows that the border span and the heads' landmark_combined response overlap; it does not show a cost to morph detection."

**F2. Major: Part A attributes the probe gain to identity without support (your Q3).**
- **What the floor shows.** The `ri_S4_vs_S3` floor (FLOORS.md stage 3; same 291 hosts, 110 components) is 0.773 [0.742, 0.805] for the logistic model, 0.719 without F27/BLEND. H0 (0.739-0.769) and the probe (0.760-0.791) both sit inside it. So the extra S4/S3 separability may be the blend-softness difference D.
- **The C grid is too narrow.** C = 10, the top of the grid, was chosen in 25 of 25 folds. That and the rising learning curve mean the ceiling may be slightly understated.
- **The MLP result.** The MLP scoring below the linear probe at ~230 hosts says nothing about nonlinear information.
- **Replacement for lines 36-40:**

  "A linear probe fitted on held-out development persons separates S4 from S3 slightly better than the frozen head: +0.003 to +0.039 (bootstrap lower bound above 0 on 4 of 5 seeds), +0.015 to +0.033 on the 80 clean test hosts. It is still rising with more fit persons, and C sat at the top of its grid in every fold. The best probe reaches 0.791, far below the 0.85 bar. No linear or small-MLP head that we tested reaches it, and extrapolating the learning curve, a larger refit set is unlikely to close the gap. Simple morph cues alone separate these same rows at 0.773 [0.742, 0.805], so this gain cannot be read as identity information."

**F3. Major: the residual flags are a dispersion effect, not mean-shift leakage (also explains "unreachable").**
- After E7c the mean logit shift per mask is exactly 0, yet the copy/photo logit SD ratio stays 2.5-4.7 on CFP-FF and MSU-MFSD (H1: 6-18). The paired Δlogit SD is 0.73-1.05.
- Any first-moment eraser, E7 or LEACE, therefore leaves copies whose tails cross the p99 cut.
- So "criteria unreachable" is a real property of mean-based erasers, not just a design accident. Add one sentence saying so, and mark it post hoc.

**F4. Minor: LEACE has no bug (your Q4).**
- The half-A class-mean spread after erasure is 1e-15 on 5 of 5 seeds, and the rank is 7.
- The transfer gap comes from the oblique projector: the largest singular value of I − M is 13-22 (it is 1 for the orthogonal E7 eraser), with cond(Σ) at 1e4-2.8e4 even after Ledoit-Wolf.
- Half-B mean differences are 5-11% off the half-A span, and LEACE amplifies that part, giving 0.05-0.06 residual spread against 0.024-0.033 for E7.
- LEACE also guarantees first moments only, so by F3 it cannot fix tail flags. Add that sentence after line 54.

### 6. Short edits for the conclusions page

- **What we have learned:** "A rank-7 eraser cancels the frozen heads' mean border response but not their border false alarms. Bordered copies still have 2.5-4.7× wider scores. Removing the border span costs recall on FFHQ landmark_combined, and random subspaces of the same overlap with the head do not."
- **How sure we are:** "Development data, 5 seeds, both readings on a knife-edge (one seed, a margin ≤ 0.04 on a ratio). The in-domain cost check was saturated by design. The specificity check against head-matched nulls is post hoc."
