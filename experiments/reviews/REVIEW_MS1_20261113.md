## 1. Verdict

The mechanical verdicts follow the registered rules, and every recomputed number matches `ms1_read.json`:
- P1-S supported: CFD own-rule flag rate, Sb median 0.0349 against SbM3 0.000; U 25/25, p = 0.00397.
- P1-T untestable at the floor: Sb median 0.0141 is below the 0.03 floor.
- P2 inconclusive: median difference 0.0057; U 24/25.
- Guards: all hold. FINAL's MS1 rule: not met.

The interpretation needs qualification. The fall in genuine flags is real and partly a ranking effect on CFD. But
SbM3 also shifts its operating point toward "genuine" on FFHQ-style images. On the one attack pool from a pipeline it
never trained on (FFHQ landmark_combined), the own-rule miss rate doubles. The registered guards could not see this,
because G1 reads AUC only and G2 sits at its ceiling. "P3 supported" does not separate the arms: Sb meets the same
bound. P1-S should be reported as "fewer genuine flags, with a printed attack-side cost and no attack read on CFD",
not as threshold transfer.

## 2. Findings (most severe first)

F1 (major): a cost the guards could not detect. Own-rule miss on FFHQ landmark_combined morphs (1,000): Sb
0.470/0.260/0.302/0.268/0.271 (median 0.271); SbM3 0.595/0.605/0.424/0.585/0.513 (median 0.585); U 24/25, p = 0.008.
Genuine flags on the resmatch photos fell from 0.064 to 0.007 while AUC barely moved (0.874 against 0.861): a
threshold shift, not a ranking change. landmark_complete misses did not change (0.011 against 0.010). AUC of
landmark_combined morphs against CFD genuine photos fell from 0.952 to 0.866 (about 23/25). SbV matches Sb (miss
median 0.312), so the change comes with the FFHQ content. P1-S has no CFD attack pool; the only cross-pipeline attack
read says the lower flag rate comes partly from moving the threshold region. Fix: print under P1-S; no verdict change.

F2 (major): calibration or ranking. The logit scale did not move: development camera photos median −3.486 against
−3.527; development attacks (R4 + u50) median +3.440 against +3.463. CFD did move in rank: AUC(CFD vs R_cam, 417,
both TF32 off) 0.964 against 0.901, complete separation. The move is in the upper tail: CFD median −3.355 against
−3.493; 95th percentile −1.27 against −3.40; maximum +3.11 against −0.88. Attacks against CFD at ceiling in both arms
(0.9999 against 1.0000). At an attack-anchored threshold (5th percentile of development attacks, about +3.3) both arms
flag 0 of 831 CFD images in every seed: the P1-S gain lies only between the genuine floor and the attack mode. Part
of the drop in CFD AUC against development camera photos comes from SbM3's own camera photos spreading upward (SD
0.117 against 0.069; seeds 405, 407 camera-photo logits up to +0.05 and +0.33; development flags 0.4%). On TT1 the
change is a whole-distribution shift (median −2.24 against −3.21) with almost no rank change (AUC 0.999 against
0.993), closer to pure calibration.

F3 (major): P3 "supported" is uninformative: the rule is SbM3 median ≤ 0.05 and Sb's median 0.037 also meets it.
The arms differ (Sb 0.017-0.067, SbM3 0.007-0.013, complete separation), but that is an unregistered printed contrast,
and FFHQ is a trained source for SbM3.

F4 (moderate): SbV is close to a null re-weighting: within-stratum oversampling at fixed 6,250 steps leaves expected
exposure per image unchanged; it does not control dilution (each FRGC/colorFERET image seen 0.63x as often in SbM3)
or the splice share (0.064 against 0.103). "Not reproduced by the row count alone" is correct but weak; it must not
be read as "the third source is needed".

F5 (moderate): other guard-blind costs: development S4 against matched S0/S3 AUC 0.891 against 0.881 (complete
separation in favour of Sb); own-rule flags on S0/S3 0.18 against 0.22; FS1 contrast-2 storage share on ffhqm 0.155
against 0.236. Report as printed reads.

F6 (moderate): the P1-S effect is small and near the floor: Sb's median only 0.005 above 0.03; seed 407 at 0.018, 406
at 0.104; absolute change about 3.5 points (about 29 of 831 images). Sb's flags are female-skewed in every group (e.g.
CFD-AF 0.088 against AM 0.000; MF 0.194 against MM 0.038; WF 0.056 against WM 0.043). Only 10 images are flagged in
all 5 Sb seeds. SbM3 flags 2 CFD images in total (cfd_0046, cfd_0797), both in Sb's union.

F7 (minor): precision cleared: TF32 on (fit job) against off (held-out step) on the same 417 R_cam images for every
network on the L40S: max |Δlogit| 0.002-0.022, median about 1e-4, 0 own-rule flips in 15 networks. No deciding read
mixes settings. All fits torch 2.2.1 / CUDA 12.1 on L40S; driver versions differ by host (595.91 m1b, 570.211 m3),
each seed's arms share one host: no confound between arms.

F8 (minor): G2 at AUC 1.000 / miss 0.000 detects nothing; KDEF 0 for every network (max logit ≤ −3.2 except one image
