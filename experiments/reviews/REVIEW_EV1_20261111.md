# Independent review of the fixed-threshold re-read of existing scores (EV1), 11 Nov 2026

Verdict: corrections required (wording and framing; every recomputed number matches).
"0.5 rule" = the release's own decision, probability of morph above 0.5, which needs no threshold pool.

## Corrected statements (use these)

- **SelfMAD release** (its crop applied to our aligned frames; published FRLL numbers are matched when
  the genuine class is neutral-only: 1.0 / 0.1 / 0.1 / 12.9 / 3.7 against 0.99 / 0.00 / 0.00 / 10.34 /
  3.45). At its own 0.5 rule it flags 0% of unprocessed genuine photos, 44.6% after a 50%
  shrink-and-enlarge, 55 to 79% with the DiM and MorDIFF histories and 97.1% of photos stored small.
  Genuine photos with the morphs' history are flagged more often than the morphs themselves in six
  families (for example MorDIFF 79% against 58%). This needs no threshold pool and no class ruling.
- **MADation ViT-B/16 release** (our frames). The 63 to 65 pixel storage history raises the score of
  99 to 100% of FRLL photos by 1.3 to 1.6 logits, and of 99% of 2,000 FFHQ photos by 0.36 logits
  (paired; 0.1 to 2.6% flagged at the 0.5 rule on FFHQ, 4 to 11% on FRLL). Shown on two photo pools.
- **MADation ViT-L/14 release** (our frames). Its scores are saturated here: all genuine logits lie
  within 0.3 logits, together with five morph families, and none of the AMSL morphs exceeds 0.5. JPEG
  quality 50 shifts every photo by 0.13 logits, which moves 42 photos across the development threshold
  and none across the release's own rule. Resolution history alone has no effect. **The earlier
  figures "24 to 34% at JPEG 50, 24.5% stored small, 22% FFHQ" are withdrawn as evidence about this
  release**; they await the own-crop re-score (RS1).
- **Miss rates depend on the threshold pool** (ViT-B/16: 11 to 63% family mean). Print the pool as a
  column. At 5% false alarms on the unprocessed FRLL photos, family-mean miss on our frames is 39%
  (ViT-L/14), 27% (ViT-B/16), 8% (SelfMAD); protecting every history-matched pool raises it to 66 / 79 /
  87%, and protecting only full-frame pools at 100 pixels or more to 49 / 55 / 87%.
- **Storage histories below passport resolution.** The DiM and MorDIFF histories and the stored-small
  pool have an inter-eye distance of 61 to 70 pixels (the passport minimum is reportedly 90; clause to
  be verified). Call them "the benchmark's own storage history", not "benign processing".
- **Null rows.** "In all 17 cells a file attribute separates the classes perfectly, and in 11 of 12
  scored cells so does the border our own alignment leaves around the smaller morph files. A high
  score on these cells cannot by itself show morph detection. Whether any detector uses these cues is
  not tested here." The sentence "no detector beats the best null" is withdrawn (it is true of any
  detector when the null is perfect). The border is created by OUR aligner: genuine frames have none,
  morph frames 3 to 26% (MorDIFF frame 54%).
- **Identity dose.** Slopes are not distinguishable from zero within gender; the pooled slope is a
  gender composition effect.
- **FEI** shows the neutral-above-smiling ordering of the ViT-L/14 release is specific to FRLL (0.41 on
  FEI); it does not settle expression against capture detail.
