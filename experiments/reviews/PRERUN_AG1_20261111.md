## 2. Findings

**1. Major: the main rule conflicts with the author's newer position.** The author's position is in 07_DECISIONS_NEEDED.md:36-44 (commit c68d9d6, 15:08, five minutes after the prereg at 15:03). It calls one-photo GAN reconstructions "generated-image detection, a different task… do not penalise morph detectors on it". 14_CLASS_DEFINITIONS.md does not exist yet. The prereg (l.57-60) and 11_AUDIT_PLAN (l.28, 35-37) instead treat R2 as a processed genuine photo, and "R0→R2 share ≥0.5 → reads the benchmark". **Fix:** before generation, register both readings, each with its own verdict rule. Reading A is the current one. Reading B counts R0→R2 as generated-image detection with no penalty and decides on R2 vs R4 and R3 vs R4. The author's definition then picks one before any score is read.

**2. Major: registered rules are missing from ag1_read.py.**
- G-b is computed (l.208) but never used in `read()`.
- G-a only prints the EER (l.268).
- The RT check is described (l.14-15) but not computed.
- Holm (prereg l.76-77) is not implemented.
- The primary FERET+FRGC pool is never pooled; the loop at l.216 reads each database separately, about 100 persons each. AG3 is also split by database (l.274), 40 persons each, though it was registered as 80 edges. FERET and FRGC also get identical bootstrap weight matrices (same seed, n=100, l.221).
- Matched bins (l.288-308) have no confidence interval and no verdict. They silently return None when `--logs` is not given.
- The primary family is inconsistent. The prereg calls ViT-B/16 "deciding" (l.47) and also "the four releases" (l.76). RELEASES (l.29) counts SelfMAD twice (rel and own), and MixFaceNet is missing (11_AUDIT_PLAN l.103 lists it).
- **Fix:** implement all of these. Add a gate-only mode whose output is committed before any score file is opened.

**3. Major: the matched-bin rule controls for a consequence of the identity difference.** Final loss is mostly the 10×ID term plus ID-Diff, and both are driven by how different the two identities are. Twin contributors have w600k cosine 0.84–0.91 on average; morph contributors 0.14–0.17 (pairs.csv). So the loss tertiles will largely separate R3 from R4. The few overlapping cells would hold the worst twins and the most successful morphs. The prereg (l.66-67) makes this small, biased subset "the stated one". **Fix (amendment before data):** go back to 11_AUDIT_PLAN l.133, "also reported". If matching is kept, match only on the non-identity terms (MS-SSIM, perceptual) and on latent distance, require that the bins cover at least 50% of R4, and add a bootstrap interval.

**4. Major: R3 vs R4 still differ in capture session.** From photos.json, the date gap between the two photos in a morph is much larger than in a twin: median 306 vs 121 days for FERET, 160 vs 63.5 for FRGC. Only 37/100 FERET and 35/100 FRGC morphs have the second photo within 60 days of the twin's second photo. Re-pairing by session costs look-alike similarity (FERET at ±30 days: mean cosine 0.161 → 0.111) and is infeasible for FRGC at ±60 days within gender. **Fix:** register a CPU-only sensitivity analysis: R3 vs R4 restricted to about 72 same-session morphs, and stratified by date gap.

**5. Major: AG2 is confounded by implementation.** PUB is the original MIPGAN; R2 is our re-implementation. A "collapse" or "reversal" in AG2 could come from that difference rather than from generated vs camera. **Fix:** read AG2 for a release only if its PUB vs BR bridge AUC lies in [0.40, 0.60] (as criterion 5 at 11_AUDIT_PLAN l.163). Otherwise label it "implementation-confounded".

**6. Major (compliance): resampling and blur in input prep, already run.** build_inputs.py:187-188 runs `gancommon.ffhq_align`, which includes LANCZOS resizing, Gaussian blur and median blend of padded borders (gancommon.py:193-201) and a bilinear quad warp. It then calls a re-typed cv2 INTER_AREA resize. The crops already exist (14:58); 158 of 604 photos were reflect-padded (FERET 145/200, FRGC 13/200, FRLL 0). These are the same calls as the registered H4 path (h4_prep.py:69-74), but none of them is on the allowed list in OWNER_BRIEF l.18-20. The docstring's "imported unchanged" is inaccurate: the sequence is re-typed. **Fix:** record this as the A33/H4 path, get the author to acknowledge it (ruling 2 covered landmark tools only), and correct the docstring. Within a pool, padding is balanced between twins and morphs (mismatch 29 vs 31 FERET, 11 vs 13 FRGC). Across pools it is not, so cross-pool comparisons are confounded.

**7. Major (bug): FERET photos cannot be read after generation.** ag1_manifest.py:82 and :159 open camera photos with `cv2.imread`. All 200 FERET sources are `.ppm.bz2`, so `prep_eval.kps(None)` crashes. This breaks FERET R0, the FERET G-b threshold, and SelfMAD-own scoring. **Fix:** decode once with `read_bgr` and store as lossless PNG. This is CPU work after generation and does not block it.

**8. G-b is only partly adequate (Q4).**
- AUC ≥0.90 is close to guaranteed by construction, so it is a sanity check, not a validity test.
- "R4 verifies both contributors" uses the very photos fed to the generator (l.203). **Fix:** also verify against photos the generator never saw (s2(p) for A, s1(q) for B; zero GPU).
- The threshold comes from camera-vs-camera impostor pairs. **Fix:** also print the false-match rate of R2 images against other people's camera photos at that threshold.
- "Twins verify A" is a condition in 11_AUDIT_PLAN l.70 but has no threshold here.
- RT only checks GPU determinism, and `cudnn.deterministic` is not set (ag1_gen.py:75-77). In effect G-b has no negative control. State that, fix a tolerance, and use the R3 vs RT detector AUC as a noise floor.
- When the morph side fails, the label should be "morph construction weak", not "twin invalid".

**9. AG3 dose definition (Q3): sound at 0.5, but the reading over-claims.**
- The terms match `generate.mipgan` exactly at α=0.5 (ag1_gen.py:166-174 vs generate.py:136-143), and the dose is monotone: the optimum has dA/dB = α/(1−α).
- The α=0 point (R2) is not the limit as α→0. R2 has ID-Diff ≡ 0; the limit gives 12·dA instead of 10·dA.
- α also scales B's latent, perceptual and MS-SSIM targets, so "ramp = identity" (11_AUDIT_PLAN l.135) overstates what a ramp shows.
- **Fix:** read the curve against the dose actually achieved (glint cosine to A and to B at each level).

**10. Is R2 a fair "processed genuine image" for R2 vs R4 (Q1)?** Partly. R2 starts at its own inversion with ID-Diff ≡ 0 (ag1_gen.py:211,215). R3 and R4 start from an average of two inversions, which is closer to the generator's mean latent. So R2 vs R4 mixes three things: latent averaging, a second photograph, and a second identity. R3 vs R4 is the only identity contrast. The prereg hypothesis (l.6-8, "rather than… two identities") can be falsified by a detector that does not read identity at all. **Fix:** reword it to "two photographs", or require R3 vs R4 "identity reading". Print latent distance and initial latent distance per rung before any score.

**11. Minor issues.**

- The FRLL pairing uses s1–s1 cosine (build_inputs.py:265), unlike the primary pool and the docstring.
- No finiteness check on the latent or loss in ag1_gen: a NaN run writes a black PNG and exits 0.
- `frame()` returns None for frames that already exist (ag1_manifest.py:81-85), so the aligned flag is wrong on re-runs.
- The aligned-rate ≥0.90 rule is not implemented.
