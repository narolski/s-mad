## 2. Findings, most severe first

**F1 (major): the mechanism in conclusion 1 is contradicted by the data.**
- The draft names "published MorDIFF files, steps, sharpness, storage". But B and D are both ours, both 100 steps, both stored the same way (median 150×193 px each). PNG bytes differ only because the content differs.
- Reviewer check (head of seed 401), Spearman correlation with PNG bytes inside each arm: B +0.02, D −0.07, B20 −0.02, D20 −0.11.
- B against D inside PNG-size fifths: 0.779 (overall 0.790); B20 against D20: 0.632 (overall 0.626).
- Correlation with Laplacian variance on the central crop, inside each arm, is negative: −0.27 to −0.38. Sharper images score lower.
- So "matched by PNG size" only means that a different cue of equal strength exists. The P4 paired-difference rule was never applied to the head.
- Fix: see the conclusion 1 rewrite in section 3.

**F2 (major): the registered rule was a weak test. Call the pass "uninformative about mixing", not "hollow".**
- The contrast was chosen while the release's preview was known: 0.852 at 100 steps against 0.655 at 20 (`prereg.md:4-6`).
- The rule is on point estimates. Every lower bound (0.725 to 0.728) is below the 0.75 mark.
- Five seeds of a linear head on cached features are effectively one network: the spread is at most 0.004 and training AUC is 1.000. "5 of 5" adds nothing over "1 of 1".
- In both B against D and B20 against D20, only the reconstruction is decoded from its own inverted noise map (PRERUN_DG1 F2). The 20-step rungs therefore match steps and the published files (AD1 bridge 0.48 to 0.51), not the rendering path.
- Applied to AD1's primary 20-step contrast, the same rule would read "falsified": 0.626 to 0.628, below 0.70 in all five seeds.
- Report it as: "Supported under the registered rule (100 steps, point estimates); not supported on the 20-step rungs that match the published morphs; neither contrast isolates mixing."

**F3 (major, an under-claim): the SMDD heads show the ladder pattern of a weak second-person reader at 20 steps.**
- The ladder rule (`ROUND3_BRIEF.md:19-20`): a second-person reader separates R4 from R3 and does not separate R3 from R2.
- The SMDD heads at 20 steps:

| Contrast | AUC |
|---|---|
| B20 against C20 (R4 against R3) | 0.618–0.622 [0.523, 0.712] |
| C20 against D20 (R3 against R2) | 0.51 [0.46, 0.56] |
| D20 against R0 | 0.53 |
| R1 against R0 | 0.52 |

- The release shows the same pattern: 0.653, 0.498, 0.561, 0.465.
- Expression does not explain it. Neutral pairs against C20 give 0.648 and smiling pairs 0.594 (reviewer check). C20 against neutral D20 gives 0.495, against smiling D20 0.528.
- Limits: the signal is weak and comes from one pool. The head does not track identity cosine inside an arm (rho −0.07 to +0.01). In A43, a second person and the disagreement between the two inputs cannot be separated: minimum cosine to the sources is 0.485 for B20 against 0.868 for C20, with no overlap.
- Wording: "weak ordering consistent with a second person, not shown to be identity". Test it on the new diffusion ladder (H-new-3).

**F4 (major): conclusion 2 follows only in part.**
- Supported: one shared direction separates both kinds of mix from the reconstruction. Reviewer transfer check, logistic probe with C = 1 and FZ1's folds:
  - trained on C20 against D20, it gives B20 against D20 0.940 (DINOv2 0.987);
  - that same direction ranks C20 above B20 (0.111; DINOv2 0.076);

- Not supported: "not a second person". The probe trained on B20 against C20 reaches 0.945 [0.908, 0.975].

- G-c's label "readable from frozen features" should read "the arms are linearly separable".
