## Findings, most severe first

**F1 (blocker for the wording). The A18 increment is explained by how similar the two inverted noise maps are.** Numbers are in the table above. Two further points:
- **The trace and the identity are tied by construction.** Two photos of the same person invert to correlated noise maps (median lowest-band cosine 0.40 for SAME against 0.17 for X; FZ2 amendment 2). So a floor built on noise-map similarity also absorbs input similarity. This test cannot separate "reads the second identity" from "reads the noise-correlation trace". It does show that the network adds nothing beyond that similarity.
- **Stratifying is not possible.** In the middle tercile of the floor score (23 X / 21 SAME) the floor itself scores 0.994 and the network 0.95–0.98. The floor is saturated, so there is no headroom to test within strata.
- **Shard 7's codes are lost.** All extended reads therefore rest on 133 rows and 23 cycles. Shards 0–1 codes are on Athena; fetching them would only enlarge the floor's training set.

**F2 (major). The direct identity reads find nothing.**
- Within X (133 rows): the network logit against the face-recognition cosine between the two contributors (glintr100, `experiments/AR1/pool.csv` cos_HB) gives ρ = −0.10 (p = 0.24). Partial given F: −0.12. Partial given the extended floor on shard 6: +0.07.
- Caveat: the range is narrow (cos_HB 5–95% is 0.15–0.34), because partners were assigned as look-alikes (`src/AR1/pool.py:153-169`).
- Within SAME (134 rows): the network tracks noise and code similarity (ρ about −0.24 to −0.31 with c0, c2, low-pass cosine and semantic-code cosine). It does not track the face-recognition cosine once the floor is controlled (+0.09; on shard 6, −0.02).
- The best existing evidence points to the noise path. The probe P_XS transfers to noise-only mixes (NOISEX over NOISES 0.907) but hardly to semantic-only mixes (SEMX over SEMS 0.579; FZ2 `result.md:97-100`).

## Question 6: consequence for the paper and the method

This afternoon's position still stands for SMDD-type training. A18 does **not** turn it into "the signal exists but SMDD training does not teach it". The supported version is:

> "A recipe trained on mixes from this generator, with same-person mixes as genuine, separates two-person from same-person mixes of unseen persons (0.99, one generator, FRGC). That signal is matched by the similarity of the two inverted noise maps. Whether it reads the second person is untested."

The method value is different from identity detection. Render-matched genuine controls (REC, SAME) could cut the false alarms on diffusion-processed genuine photos that every SMDD-trained fit produces (A15: REC over camera photos 0.99+).

Cheapest test that could make this a method component, using only existing images:
- Add DG1 shards 0–5 to the protocol training set: X20 as attack, REC20 + SAME20 as genuine.
- Read on shards 6–7 (REC flag rate at the camera threshold, X detection) and on the FFHQ development families.
- 2 arms × 2 seeds, about 6–8 GPU-hours on Athena, where the packs already are.
- It could claim: "render-matched controls lower diffusion-render false alarms on development data for this generator".
- It could not claim: second identity, other generators, or any benchmark.
- Note that MorDIFF is built on the same diffusion autoencoder, so a later benchmark gain would be partly same-family.
- Another generator, or other persons, needs new images from the author.
