# Post-result review: threshold transfer on unseen genuine sources (TT1), commits cf963d1 and c391815

## Verdict

The numbers reproduce exactly, and the registered rules were applied as written. The conclusions need qualification, for three reasons:
- **Scale.** The headline AUCs of 0.99-1.00 mostly reflect how tightly the camera photos cluster. They do not show that unseen photos come close to the decision boundary.
- **The border result is overstated.** The border alone shifts camera photos by only a few hundredths of a logit.
- **The strongest finding is missing.** After the 112 px storage path, arm A flags about half of its own training-source camera photos. Protocol B removes this. result.md does not report it.

"The own rule transfers for B" holds narrowly: false alarms only, on these black-bordered low-resolution frames, two seeds, each seed on its own machine. The same B_s401 network flags 17.8% of FFHQ genuine photos at the same rule. Sb's "supported" verdict rests on a 5% bar it clears by 0.1-0.3 points. Excluding Bn_s401 under TT1-D3 (per-network gate) is acceptable and changes no verdict.

## Findings (most severe first)

**F1. Major: TT-H2 does not say what the AUCs mean.**
- The camera photos (R_cam) are nearly a point mass: the 5th-95th percentile spans 0.05-0.15 logit, at about −3.67 (B) or −3.51 (Sb). The head floor is about −3.83.
- Unseen-family medians sit +0.32 to +0.81 logit above R_cam for A and B, and +0.95 to +1.71 for Sb. That is 0.04-0.26 of the unit u, which is about 6.5-7.3.
- So for B the bulk of unseen photos is still about 3.2 logits below zero.
- Reconciliation with the low flag rates: AUC measures displacement from a very tight reference, and flag rate measures the upper tail crossing 0. B's 95th percentile over all unseen frames is −1.10 (s401) and −2.53 (s402). Sb's is +0.16 on both seeds. Both statements are true together.

**F2. Major (under-claim): the crop-geometry reference contains the clearest mechanistic result, and result.md omits it.** Own-rule flag share of the 417 camera photos after the 112 px path, with the paired median shift:

| Arm | Flagged | Paired median shift |
|---|---|---|
| A | 46.0% / 56.6% | +2.85 / +5.37 logit |
| B | 1.0% / 0.2% | +0.04 / +0.05 |
| Bv | 0.7% / 0.2% | about the same as B |
| C | 1.9% / 0.2% | about the same as B |
| Bn_s402 | 0.2% | about the same as B |
| Sb | 7.7% / 3.4% | +0.09 / +0.14 |

- At 224 px every arm shifts by only +0.03 to +0.07, with 0-1% flagged.
- So A has a storage-size shortcut on its own training source. It appears somewhere between 1.7x and 3.5x enlargement, and protocol B removes it.
- This also explains TT-H5 for A. At 112 px A scores geometry-matched camera photos above the real packs (R3(g) 0.16-0.41). For A, the "source matters" verdict therefore rests only on the four anti-spoofing sets.

**F3. Major: TT-H3, "the border alone moves the camera photos strongly", is an over-claim.** These are paired (same image) median shifts:
- Camera photos: +0.04 to +0.10 logit in six families, and +0.21 to +0.38 at CFP-FF's larger border (median border 53% of the frame).
- That is about 10-20% of each family's unseen excess, except CFP-FF (35-75%; A_s402 0.38 of 0.50).
- Direction: toward "morph" on camera photos, and growing with border size. In the regression (R4(d)) the border coefficient is +0.15 to +0.47 u.
- On FFHQ genuine photos the direction is mixed: paired median −0.04 to +0.11, and flag shares fall for B_s401 (17.8% to 13.0%) but rise for Sb_s402 (7.0% to 13.4%). Blanking also removes FFHQ's own surround, as the M3 caveat says.

The border is a real but small cue. It does not undermine the own-rule flag rates, which describe the pipeline as deployed. It weakens only "source" attributions for CFP-FF. The geometry reference matters here because it includes border, geometry and resampling together, and on it the anti-spoofing excess remains about 0.3-1.5 logit against a shift of at most 0.07.

**F4. Major: "own rule transfers for B" needs its limits stated.**

What it does show:
- Every family's upper bound is ≤ 10% on both seeds; point values are 0.0-4.9%, the highest being LFW on s401.
- The verdict survives Clopper-Pearson intervals: the largest upper bound is 8.3% (s401) and 5.5% (s402). The bootstrap gives degenerate [0, 0] intervals when a family has zero flags.

What it does not show:
- That scores are unchanged. Ranks shift completely.
- That τ_cam transfers. 99-100% of unseen photos are flagged at τ_cam.
- That attacks from these sources are caught. There are no morphs.
- That the rule transfers to sharp sources. B_s401 flags 17.8% of FFHQ genuine photos, which would fail TT-H1's own 10% bar.

There is also an invisible trade-off. The earlier PN1 tier-1 reads put B's own-rule miss at 28.8% / 38.9% (gating-family mean). The seed with the fewest genuine flags (B_s402) has the most misses. TT1 cannot see this trade. Those tier-1 numbers include families now ruled benchmarks, so they cannot be re-read or extended.

**F5. Moderate: Sb "supported" is knife-edge.** The AgeDB lower bounds are 0.0509 (s401) and 0.0534 (s402), against the 5% bar, with the heuristic inflation factor k = 2.03. With k ≥ 2.2, Sb_s401 no longer fails AgeDB. Sb_s401's OULU-NPU bound (0.0495) just misses. State it as "supported, narrowly".

**F6. Moderate: the seed spread sets the evidence level.** Seed 401 vs 402:
- B: F̄ (mean family flag share) 0.024 vs 0.002.
- C: 0.117 vs 0.019.
- FFHQ genuine for B: 17.8% vs 6.4%.

Seed equals machine. B − A is consistent on both seeds, but the intervals cover image sampling only. Every arm sentence is a two-seed lead.

**F7. Sb > B is a lead, not noise.**
- At logit > 0, Sb − B is +0.021 / +0.031. The gap grows at every cut-off I recomputed (shares above the cut-off):

| Cut-off (logit) | Sb − B, s401 / s402 |
|---|---|
| 0 | +0.021 / +0.031 |
| −0.5 | +0.030 / +0.047 |
| −1 | +0.055 / +0.082 |
| −2 | +0.33 / +0.34 |

- Bv − B is between −0.003 and 0 at every cut-off. Bv uses pack 2 and Sb's row volume, so neither the pack nor the data volume explains the gap; the spliced positives do.
- Sb lifts the whole unseen distribution: 35-41% of unseen photos sit above −2, against 1-8% for B.
- With two seeds it is not an arm effect.

**F8. Rules check.**
- AUCs reproduce with mid-ranks (scipy `rankdata` matches `wauc`).
- The interval inflation is implemented as registered: [p − k(p − lo), p + k(hi − p)], with k = 1.18 / 1.78 / 2.03 (tt1_read.py:19, :61-66). Two flaws:
  - Lower bounds are not clipped at 0 (AgeDB −0.001 for Bn_s402 and Bv_s402).
  - Zero-event families give zero-width bootstrap intervals.
- Pins verified: tt1_read.py `4546f520…`, tt1_read.json `9ac3a0b8…`, result tar `c9bb574c…`, list `c0055b51…`, manifests `a7aebe16…` and `8b3c6fa9…`.

**F9. TT1-D3 (per-network gate) is acceptable.**
- It was written after a platform datum, before any TT1 score existed. It excludes a printed-only arm and changes no tolerance.
- Bn is in no TT-H6 pair and has no deciding role, so no verdict changes.
- Bn_s401 repeated its miss exactly on tt1c, so this is deterministic, not instability. The likely cause is TF32: the stored camera logits were made with TF32 on (pn1_score.py), and the row is off the floor (stored −0.133).
- The FS1 block must re-register the rule explicitly before scoring: if a deciding network fails the gate, the decision is "not available"; it is never read on one seed.

**F10. Platform: what the sealed final read must pin (Q3).**
- GPU model and compute architecture (A100-SXM4, sm_80), driver, CUDA 12.1, the exact torch 2.2.1 build and cuDNN version, timm 1.0.27, Pillow 10.2.0, numpy 1.26.4.
- TF32 set explicitly for both matmul and cuDNN, equal to the setting that produced any stored scores or thresholds it is compared with.
- Attention backend, autocast and dtype, `cudnn.benchmark=False` / deterministic mode.
- Batch size 64 and list order, so batch composition is fixed.
- Rescorer, network and base-weight sha256.
- A pre-registered gate on rows that span the logit range. Floor rows are insensitive: on the DGX, R_cam max |Δ| was 0.005 while FFHQ was 0.088. The action on failure is fixed in advance.
- GB10 / torch 2.12 is excluded. Decode and resize are bit-identical across stacks, so the difference is in the network computation.

These photos are still clearly displaced from the training camera photos in score, so a threshold calibrated on camera photos does not transfer (99-100% flagged)."

## MS1 in the light of TT1 (Q7, no rule changes)

- **P1-T floor.** Sb's F_T is 0.045 / 0.033. The pool MS1 uses (21,089, including the 54 out-of-band frames) changes this by at most 0.001. Given 10-fold seed spreads in the arms that were read, a median below 0.03 over seeds 403-407, and so "untestable (floor)", is a real possibility.
- **P1-T support** needs SbM3 at about 0.02 or below, with near-complete separation.
- **P2 is at ceiling.** Every PN1 arm scores 0.987-1.000 against the camera photos, so "supported" (a 0.05 drop) is implausible. A "falsified" (SbM3 ≥ Sb) would be decided by differences of about 0.001 and must be described as a ceiling artefact.
- **Useful printed reads:** per-family f_k beside Sb_s401/402 from TT1; F at −0.5 / −1 / −2; median excess in logits and in u; geometry-112 flag shares and border flag shares per arm.
- MS1's in-pack scores are not bit-comparable with TT1's rescore, so compare only within MS1.

## Risks to the story

- Fresh seeds may break "B transfers". One seed like C_s401 (F̄ 0.117) is enough.
- The low-resolution unseen claim inherits the border and storage confound.
- A final read on a different stack can flip rows near 0.

Hedge: register the platform pins (F10) now, and phrase every arm claim as a two-seed lead until the FS1 block is read.
