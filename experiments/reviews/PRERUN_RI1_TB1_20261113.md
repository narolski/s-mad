### RI1 findings, most severe first

**R1 (blocker for the P1 premise). The registered spread V is biased by construction.**
- Evidence: prereg.md:62-63 and ri1_read.py:149-155.
- L_k − L_0 = (e_k − e_0)·(h − x). Under the null (occlusion noise unrelated to identity), its SD grows with ‖h − x‖ = √(2(1 − cos_HX)).
- The pool gives cos_HB median ≈ 0.23 and cos_HS ≈ 0.87, with a maximum cos_HB of 0.65 below the minimum cos_HS of 0.68.
- Without the divisor, R4's V is therefore about 2.4 times R3's for scale reasons alone. The printed no-divisor variant would pass by construction.
- The registered divisor, mean(1 − cos(e_0, src)), grows roughly with ‖h − x‖². It over-corrects: on a midpoint approximation R3's null V is about 2.7 times R4's. The primary read is pushed toward "kill".
- Replace §5 "Spread" with: "Spread. u = (emb(H_src) − emb(X_src)) / ‖·‖, X = B for R4 and S for R3. V = SD over kept k of (e_k − e_0)·u (the null-scale-free projection). Validity gate: on the camera photo R0 of the same hosts, AUC(V(R0; u_HB) vs V(R0; u_HS)) and AUC(V(R2w4; u_HB) vs V(R0; u_HS)) must lie in [0.40, 0.60]. Otherwise P1 is 'invalid (projection carries scale)' and the outcome is inconclusive. The draft's divided and undivided V are printed only and never change the reading."

**R2 (major). An undisclosed deviation: the draft's P1 strata are dropped.**
- The draft (P1 Read) asked for P1 "overall and within quintiles of the geometry gap G and of cos_HS". The ledger M1(c)(3) lists the same strata as the pose and session control.
- The registration (§5) and the code compute P1 overall only.
- Add to §5: "Printed: P1 within quintiles of G (BD1 d_values.csv of the host's S4/S3) and within cos_HS quintiles of R3 against all R4. Within class: Spearman ρ(V, cos_HB) over R4 and ρ(V, cos_HS) over R3."
- Add to §9: "On this ladder cos_HB and cos_HS do not overlap (max 0.65 against min 0.68). A pass cannot separate 'two identities' from 'FR-dissimilar inputs'; only look-alike pairs (LA1) can."

**R3 (major). The registered controls do not match the contrasts they are meant to control.**
- Evidence: prereg.md:73-74 and ri1_read.py:185-187.
- S0 vs R0 mixes the seam with warp. S0 is R2w pasted onto R2w4, so the clean seam-only contrast is S0 vs R2w4.
- R4/R3 and S4/S3 compare two warp magnitudes, but R2w4 vs R0 compares a warp against no warp.
- R4 vs R3 has no blend-energy stratification. BD1 found D alone separates S4 from S3 at 0.636, and FR confidence drops with ghosting.
- Replace the control line with: "Controls that must stay within [0.40, 0.60] (two-sided): R0_61 vs R0, R2w4 vs R0, S0 vs R2w4 (seam, same base and hull), R2w4 vs R2w (warp magnitude, OpenCV R2w rows added to the embedding). S0 vs R0 and R3 vs R0 are printed."
- Replace the signal line with: "R4 vs R3 is read within quintiles of the host's S4/S3 D (same inner content), with the pure-D reference; the outcome-table R4/R3 ≥ 0.65 refers to this within-D value."
- Code: add `("ladder","R2w","opencv","")` to `rows()`. In ri1_read.py:200, change `> 0.60` to `abs(auc - 0.5) > 0.10`.

**R4 (major). The code's outcome logic differs from the registered P1 pass rule.**
- §5 defines a pass as ≥ 0.70 with a lower bound > 0.62. ri1_read.py:231 and :234 test `p1 >= 0.70` only.
- Fix: use `p1v == "pass"` in both lines.
- Add an outcome row: "P1 ≤ 0.60 and R4/R3-within-D ≥ 0.65 → 'Δ reads something other than regional identity leaning; closed'."

**R5 (major). The 27-statistic floor is printed but binds nothing, and it lacks the known splice shortcuts.**
- The ADV prereg calls RI1's own floor "binding", but no row of the RI1 outcome table uses it.
- The 27 cues (splice_nat.npz) contain no seam colour step and no hull inside/outside sharpness ratio. AR1 found the seam step separates S4 from S0 at about 0.71, and the sharpness ratio reaches 0.65-0.72.
- Add to the "Next" column of row 1: "and the cross-fitted gain of Δ over [27-statistic logistic + PN1 floor3 features (seam tone luma, R−G step, inside/outside sharpness ratio)] on S4 vs S3 is ≥ +0.03 (same 5 host folds)."

**R6 (minor).**
- FFHQ is used only for the z-score. Every AUC and the standardised logistic are unchanged by it, so the 9,000 FFHQ crops (about 20% of the CPU time) inform no number. Either drop them or print "R0 vs FFHQ genuine Δ AUC (source dependence)".
- The FFHQ rows are role hn2, from `/mnt/t7/smad_heldout/ffhq_morphs`. Label them as such.
- 215 of the 291 partners are themselves hosts, so folds and bootstraps are host-disjoint but not person-disjoint. Disclose this, or fold by connected components.
- No counting rule exists for faces that are not detected. Add: "if a rung has < 95% found, its contrast is 'not measured'."
- The border control is dropped. This is acceptable because ladder comparisons share the aligner's border, but keep the stated "not tested" and add "no deployment claim".

### TB1 findings, most severe first

**T1 (major). The controls are not "the same model".**
- Evidence: prereg.md:49 says "Controls (same model)", but tb1_read.py:213-215 fits a new 5-feature logistic for each control. That measures whether storage, seam or warp is detectable at all, not whether the S4/S3 statistic reacts to them.
- Replace with: "Controls: the S4-vs-S3 fold models (fitted on S4/S3 rows of the training folds) score each control row out-of-fold by host. AUC must lie in [0.42, 0.58]. The separately refitted control AUCs are printed as separability ceilings."

**T2 (major). The warp-magnitude and seam confounds are uncontrolled, as in R3.**
- S4's ring is R2w4 and S3's ring is R2w, so the two geometries differ outside the hull.
- S0 vs R0 also uses different masks (m7 against mH, tb1_measure.py:88).
- Add binding controls "R2w4 vs R2w (both on m7)" and "S0 vs R2w4 (m7)". Compute r for R2w on m7. Print S4 vs S3 within G quintiles.

**T3 (major). The hard hull edge leaks colour steps into the bands.**
- d is zero outside the hull and steps at the boundary (pixel copy, no feather). S4 carries half the partner's skin-tone step (AR1).
- The direction-free logistic will pick that up and call it texture. D_dc is not among the strata, although the draft listed it as a covariate.
- Replace §3 P with: "d is centred by its hull mean before the FFT. E_k is summed (a) over the hull and (b) over the hull eroded by 16 px (mask morphology only), with (b) printed. Printed within D_dc quintiles."
- For S, add: "r_k also printed with a 16 px guard band either side of the hull edge, and within quintiles of the seam tone step."

**T4 (major). Same issue as R5: the floor does not bind, and the comparison is unequal.**
- A best single statistic is compared against a 5-feature logistic. The inside/outside sharpness ratio, which is TB1's S in one dimension, is missing.
- Use the same binding gain text as in R5, with r in place of Δ, and print the cross-fitted 27-statistic logistic baseline beside it.

**T5 (minor). Wording and rule order.**
- Row 3's reading over-claims. Replace "no texture-level second-identity trace beyond blend energy; BD1's residual is not texture" with "no band-profile difference of the luminance half-difference in AS0's five octave bands at equal D; other texture statistics are not tested".
- State the priority: the control row is applied first, as the code already does.
- Reading all 291 hosts at once is acceptable because every rule is fixed beforehand. Still print the increment on the 191 test hosts on their own, since the development hosts served PN1 selection.
