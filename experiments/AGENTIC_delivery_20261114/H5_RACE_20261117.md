# H5: SelfMAD MAD22 scoring raced on WCSS, Athena and the GB10 (2026-11-17)

The author asked for this race. Same scoring as `src/AD5/score/selfmad_m22_dgx_20261117.sh` (H5 amendment 1, 9253df0):
6 networks (the selfmad_hrnet_w18 release and the retrains selfmad_S_s401..s405) x 2 crop variants = 12 npz.
Settings are rel mode, --det selfmad_hrnet_w18 with each network's own weights, and the frozen score.py/ar1_common.py pins.
This is inference only. No EERs were computed here.

## What was shipped
- `h5pack.tar` (sha 7a16d7c0...) holds the RS1 regen-pack code (pins unchanged), `ad5_score_athena.py`, the 6 weights
  files, `score_v1/v2.csv`, `athena_manifest.csv` and `crops_sha256.txt` (the sha256 of the 57,302 GB10 crop files
  that the manifests point to). The s402 and s404 weights were converted again from their sha-checked archives with
  convert_selfmad.py (only epoch_100.tar). Converting s401 again gave the known sha 93481a9b, so the conversion is deterministic.
- Crops: the GB10 regen crops (`RS1/regen_run/crops/v{1,2}`, only the files the manifests use) were sent as one tar
  in 10 parts of 1 GiB, each sha-checked, to WCSS Lustre and to Athena group storage.
  Every job checks all 57,302 files against the GB10 list before scoring.
- Crop equivalence: the RS1 prod crops (`results_rented/prod_v{1,2}_rented/crops_sha256.txt`) are NOT byte-identical
  to the GB10 regen crops. 22,664 of 28,253 v1 files match (80.2%) and 21,619 of 29,049 v2 files match (74.4%).
  The MAD22 sets are affected too. So the existing Athena RS1 path was not used, and only the GB10 crop files were scored.
