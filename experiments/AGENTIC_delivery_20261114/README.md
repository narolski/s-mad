## Scientific review coordination

Trigger a bounded scientific review after an independently reviewed result, failed hypothesis, unexpected reproducibility issue, or material capacity change. The result owner supplies a compact evidence packet: registered question, all seed effects and intervals, control outcomes, deviations/failures, source hashes, interpretation limits and remaining deadline. Unreviewed patterns are explicitly provisional and cannot trigger a benchmark change.

The scientific reviewer returns at most three ranked hypothesis cards. Each separates observation from explanation, includes the strongest alternative, a distinguishing prediction and falsifier, the cheapest admissible test, and the consequence of positive/negative/inconclusive outcomes. Prefer an already registered unread comparison or zero-GPU analysis over new fits when it answers the question. Rank by decision value, independent information, credibility and probability of completing before cutoff; do not rank by likelihood of a small p-value.

Controls checks whether the proposed contrast actually separates explanations; statistics checks estimand, precision and multiplicity; computation supplies end-to-end completion and resource scenarios; method prepares the experiment. The lead chooses at most one new primary experiment plus bounded independent diagnostics and records deferred/rejected ideas. Register before execution. At the next result the scientific reviewer updates the hypothesis, not the already registered success threshold.

A scientific-review author cannot be the sole independent pre-run approver of its own proposed experiment or the sole conclusions reviewer of its preferred explanation. The lead assigns another reviewer. This role can recommend that a null/control paper is stronger than another detector search.
