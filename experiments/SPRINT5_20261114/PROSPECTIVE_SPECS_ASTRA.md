## 3. Constrained negative-tail objective

Three arms, equal rows and updates: BCE; BCE+mean-negative penalty (uniform-score-lowering control); BCE+negative CVaR penalty. Do not relabel NE1 group refits as this experiment. Use logit margin m=z-tau_anchor with tau_anchor frozen from baseline calibration; tail loss CVaR_q(softplus(m)) with q=.10. Implement Rockafellar form eta + mean(relu(loss-eta))/q, with eta optimized on TRAINING rows. Aggregate identity-balanced negative losses, and weight sources equally, not images. A minibatch with tiny tail is not stable: use stratified large effective batch/accumulation and log effective tail count.

Detection constraint: for each predeclared positive family, training surrogate mean softplus(-m) no more than frozen baseline training reference plus preregistered epsilon; update nonnegative dual variable deterministically, capped and logged. Exact scalar lambda, dual step/cap, epsilon and update schedule must be pinned in prereg. A practical first fixed recipe is lambda=.1, dual step=.01, cap10, epsilon=.02; these are prospective engineering defaults, not literature guarantees, and require gradient/smoke sanity before production. Stop divergent/nonfinite runs, no outcome-tuned retries.

Primary test: lower heldout negative FPR at matched DETECTION operating point: each arm's threshold set on independent positive calibration to90% sensitivity, then assess heldout negative FPR and heldout positive sensitivity. Prespecify NI delta=.02 absolute sensitivity per positive family as a proposed utility margin for independent review. A favorable FPR effect only counts if NI passes all families. Also report baseline frozen-threshold results, AUC and quantiles. A uniform logit shift leaves threshold-adjusted results unchanged, so cannot pass by itself. If sample size cannot establish NI, report inconclusive rather than improvement. Do not calibrate on heldout negatives or reuse training references as evaluation.

Row draws/source, label share, splice fraction, image resolution and augmentation RNG budget equal. Nested image IDs make exposure auditable. Match capture count per identity within each variant setting, or state that variants include capture diversity.

Same unique rows across breadth arms is mathematically incompatible with changing identity count: match number of unique rows if feasible by trading within-ID variants, or explicitly use the factorial estimand. Do not claim isolated person-count effect while both unique-row count and person count change. Recommended first minimal contrast: K identities x2V variants vs2K identities xV variants, equal2KV UNIQUE rows and equal repetitions. This estimates breadth-vs-variant allocation, not pure independent main effects. Optional third KxV cell repeated toR identifies benefit of added unique content only descriptively. Need multiple preregistered nested subset draws paired across arms; otherwise conclusions condition on a single identity selection. Allocate each of five training seeds a prespecified subset draw and explicitly acknowledge joint subset/optimizer variability. Endpoint ordinary heldout morph sensitivity at fixed calibration rule plus genuine FPR, all stratified by source. Include per-ID exposure histogram and assert exact row scheduler equality.
