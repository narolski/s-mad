## Tail threshold and reported effects

Use one pooled, image-weighted positive calibration threshold per model, shared across all six genuine cells and all positive evaluation families. For n positive calibration scores sorted ascending, let k=floor(.10*n). Set tau=nextafter(z[k], negative infinity) in float64 (zero-based indexing). Prediction is strictly score>tau. Thus at most k calibration images fall below the decision even with ties; for the original n70, tau is immediately below the eighth ordered score. Report achieved calibration sensitivity and actual held-out sensitivities; “90%” describes the calibration operating point, not a guaranteed held-out sensitivity. Do not use a camera quantile, zero threshold or training anchor for this primary.

The primary output is the vector of six genuine false-alarm-rate differences CVaR−mean, each computed as the mean of the five paired per-network rate differences. Negative means fewer false alarms. Report all five effects and original per-network sensitivities. Use ordinary image-weighted rates; an identity-weighted sensitivity analysis, if ever desired, needs separate prospective registration. Do not pool logits, choose a source cell, choose a seed or replace this vector with a best-cell result. A macro-average may be displayed only as an explicitly descriptive auxiliary summary.

BCE−mean is reported with the same calibration, populations and uncertainty procedure, without being selected as a competing winner. Also report the registered AUC/score-quantile diagnostics and frozen-baseline-threshold rates. The baseline threshold must come from the SAME70 positive-calibration IDs of the admitted Sb408 model, not from the TRAIN_ONLY anchor. Its archive is already known by hash but has not been opened here; a later root read release must separately bind its exact score member and allowed IDs. It is one common baseline, not five new fits. Frozen-baseline threshold results are auxiliary, never a substitute for the main recalibrated operating point.

## Tail bootstrap and simultaneous NI

Use numpy.default_rng(2026111403), PCG64, with10000 attempted draws. Build sorted source strata and sorted full components once. For each draw in order: (1) sample evaluation components with replacement within each source, retaining all their positive and camera rows jointly; (2) independently sample calibration components within their own source strata, retaining all their calibration rows; (3) sample five paired seed indices with replacement. Reuse these same indices across all three models, both contrasts, every genuine cell and every positive family. Recompute each model's positive threshold from that draw's calibration rows. This incorporates calibration variability; do not bootstrap each arm independently. Fix this call order in implementation and synthetic tests. Metadata-only draw support must be verified before any real read; an unexpected empty required non-sparse cell or nonfinite value aborts with diagnostics, not silent draw deletion.

For the six primary FA differences, report pointwise descriptive percentile95% intervals [.025,.975] and label them non-simultaneous. These do not independently establish a favorable overall FPR verdict. No new .03/4-of5 or AUC gate is invented for tail.

The utility family contains exactly EIGHT one-sided NI guards: four registered positive families (`af0_u50`, `af0_u5061`, `lad_R4`, `lad_S4`) for each fixed contrast (CVaR−mean and BCE−mean). All guards use held-out sensitivity differences at their respective independently calibrated thresholds. The margin is −.02. Use Bonferroni alpha=.05/8=.00625, i.e. the .00625 percentile lower bound of each eligible guard's joint bootstrap differences; pass only if that lower bound is >=−.02. This is an approximate simultaneous one-sided95% bootstrap family, conditional on validity of its marginal bootstrap bounds. It is NOT marginal95% IUT and does not assert finite-sample coverage. Missing guards are never removed from the denominator. At10000 draws the lower tail has62.5 nominal draws, avoiding the much coarser119-cell Bonferroni tail.

Prospective support rule for these utility guards: absent family, fewer than20 independent evaluation components, fewer than20 calibration components, or zero bootstrap variance => INCONCLUSIVE; unexpected nonfinite supported-guard draws => fatal. No zero-event automatic pass. The support rule is engineering conservatism, not proof of coverage. Here all four family slots are already missing or sparse: af0_u5061 is absent from admitted held-out positives, and the three available family counts are10/17/17. Therefore a complete simultaneous NI utility claim is unavailable regardless of candidate scores. Numeric descriptive effects can still be computed; the overall favorable utility/FPR verdict must remain INCONCLUSIVE. This is the known, unresolvable-with-current-population limitation, not a reason to alter the primary or create new data now.

The original119 genuine camera cells,132 camera calibration people/order127 and S4 detection panel may be shown separately only after their pilot-specific provenance is independently admitted. They remain exactly pinned, with all52 sparse cells retained. This proposal does not claim that auditing the new tail-primary split establishes eligibility of every inherited auxiliary cell.
