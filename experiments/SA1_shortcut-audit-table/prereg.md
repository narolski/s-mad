# Shortcut audit table (SA1): registrations of the two zero-GPU gap checks (lead's instruction, 13 Nov 2026)

Each line is registered at the git commit that adds it, before anything is computed. No image is opened; only file
names, manifest rows and pair lists are read.

- **G9 (partner audit of our own training manifests, CPU).** For each training manifest (A, B, Bn, C, Sb, Sp, SbM3, SbV), count the unique two-person training morphs (af0_u50 with their 61 px copies mapped to the source morph, lad_R4, lad_S4) and their contributors from the pair lists (AF0 `manifest.csv`, MS1 `af0_pairs.csv` and `ladder.csv`, AR1 manifests), and print the number of distinct second contributors, the largest share of morphs that share one contributor, and the share of morphs whose contributor appears in 10 or more morphs; reading rule: "no SMDD-like partner structure" if no contributor is in more than 1% of the unique morphs of any manifest, otherwise "partner structure present" with the counts printed (SMDD reference: 5 second contributors for 15,000 morphs).
- **G11 (zero-GPU check for a paired expression pool in development data).** From file names only, count persons of the Chicago Face Database (CFD, all three subsets) and of KDEF that have both a neutral and a happy image (CFD `-N` with `-HC` or `-HO`; KDEF `NES` with `HAS`, front view), and whether the neutral image is the one already aligned in the MS1 pool; reading rule: "a paired expression pool exists" if at least 100 persons have both, otherwise "no usable pool"; note: CFD and KDEF are MS1 held-out evaluation sources (never training), so any later scoring there is a development read of a held-out source and needs its own registration and the lead's approval.
