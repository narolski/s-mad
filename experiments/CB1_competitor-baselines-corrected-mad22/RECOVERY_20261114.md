It never reached `DONE` before the time limit.

The job script starts an infinite GPU-monitor loop in the background as `MP`. Later it starts classifier workers and uses a bare `wait`. That waits for **all** background children, including the monitor, whose termination occurs only after the wait. It therefore cannot complete normally even after training and scoring finish. This is a shell orchestration defect, not a failed model fit or missing H100 architecture support. The script also loses individual worker exit codes through the bare wait. Its 30-minute partial-copy pattern omits `.pt.last` checkpoints.
